Festo Pneumatics FAQ

Q: How do I order a custom stroke Festo pneumatic cylinder like DSBC or ADN?
A: Provide the Festo type code (e.g., DSBC-50-200-PPVA-N3 or ADN-32-50-A-P-A) or the 6- to 8-digit Festo part number from the nameplate. As an experienced Festo pneumatic supplier, we verify bore size, stroke length, and cushioning options for rapid dispatch.

Q: Can you supply replacement parts for older Festo DNC cylinders and CPE valves?
A: Yes, Festo DSBC is the official ISO 15552 successor to the legacy DNC series with identical mounting fit. We supply both DSBC drop-in upgrades and original spare parts or seal kits for legacy DNC, ADVU, and CPE models.

Q: What communication protocols do Festo valve terminals (VTUG, MPA, CPX) support?
A: Festo valve manifolds support virtually all modern industrial fieldbus and Ethernet protocols, including PROFINET, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP, PROFIBUS DP, Modbus TCP, and IO-Link.

Q: Are Festo pneumatic components suitable for washdown and heavy-duty environments?
A: Absolutely. Festo provides corrosion-resistant stainless steel cylinders (CRDSNU, CRHD), FDA-compliant clean design actuators, and IP65/IP69K washdown valve terminals, perfect for food packaging and pharmaceutical applications.
