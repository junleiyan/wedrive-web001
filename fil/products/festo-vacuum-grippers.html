Festo Vacuum Systems, Grippers at Sensors: OVEM/VN, DHPS/HGPT/DRVS, SMT-8M/SPAU/SDE5 at QS/PUN-H

Tatak: Festo | Pangunahing Teknolohiya: IO-Link Vacuum Ejectors, Precision Gripping, Magnetic at Pressure Sensing | Pandaigdigang Supplier ng Festo
Smart Handling, End-of-Arm Tooling (EOAT) at Industrial Sensing:
Bilang nangungunang pandaigdigang Festo pneumatic supplier, ang WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED ay nagbibigay ng kumpletong ecosystem ng Festo vacuum handling components, mechanical grippers, rotary actuators, electronic sensors, at pneumatic tubing/fittings. Ininhinyero para sa high-speed pick-and-place, ligtas na paghawak ng bahagi, at real-time na pagsubaybay sa kondisyon, pinananatili ng mga sangkap na ito ang mga automated lines sa pinakamataas na kahusayan.

Mga Pangunahing Pamilya ng Bahagi na Saklaw sa Pahinang Ito:
• Serye ng OVEM / VN (Vacuum Generators at Suction Ejectors): Matalinong single-stage vacuum generators (OVEM) na may pinagsamang supply/ejector pulse valves, vacuum sensor, LCD display, air-saving function, at IO-Link, kasama ang compact inline/T-shaped ejectors (VN) at ESS/ESV suction cups.
• Serye ng DHPS / HGPT / DRVS (Parallel Grippers at Rotary Drives): High-precision double-acting at single-acting parallel grippers (DHPS, HGPT) na may T-slot jaw guidance at vane-type rotary actuators (DRVS, DSM) na may adjustable swivel angles hanggang 270°.
• Serye ng SMT-8M / SPAU / SDE5 (T-Slot at Pressure Sensors): Solid-state magneto-resistive proximity switches (SMT-8M-A) para sa Festo cylinder T-slots, dual-color LCD pressure transmitters (SPAU) na may IO-Link, at compact programmable pressure switches (SDE5).
• QS Fittings at PUN-H Tubing (Push-In Connectors at Polyurethane Tubing): Leak-free Quick Star (QS, QSL, QST) brass/PBT push-in fittings (Ø 4–16 mm) at hydrolysis-resistant, food-grade polyurethane tubing (PUN-H) sa iba't ibang kulay at diyametro.

Ang hanay ng Festo Vacuum, Grippers at Accessories ay nagbibigay ng lahat ng kailangan upang ikonekta, paandarin, hawakan, at tuklasin sa mga automated machinery. Nagkakabit ka man ng robotic end-of-arm tool na may IO-Link OVEM vacuum generator at DHPS parallel gripper, nagpapalit ng T-slot cylinder switches (SMT-8M), o nag-iimbak ng maramihang rolyo ng PUN-H tubing at QS push-in fittings, sinusuri ng aming teknikal na koponan ang nozzle diameters, gripping forces, electrical plug styles (M8/M12/flying leads), at thread sizes upang matiyak ang 100% factory-compliant na paghahatid.

Gabay sa Festo End-of-Arm Tooling at Sensing: OVEM vs. DHPS vs. SMT-8M/SPAU

Gabay sa pagpili ng inhenyeriya upang tukuyin ang vacuum ejectors, mechanical grippers, cylinder sensors, at pneumatic piping para sa automated workstations:

Pamilya ng Serye Tungkulin sa Paggana Pangunahing Bentahe sa Inhenyeriya Inirerekomendang Kondisyon ng Operasyon
OVEM / VN Vacuum Paglikha ng Venturi vacuum gamit ang compressed air Pinagsasama ng OVEM ang vacuum generation, supply valve, ejector pulse valve, vacuum switch, at air-saving cycle control sa isang compact IP65 unit. Makinis at hindi buhaghag na workpieces (sheet metal, salamin, karton, PCBs) na nangangailangan ng mabilis na cycle times at vacuum verification.
DHPS / HGPT / DRVS Mechanical jaw gripping at pneumatic rotary indexing Ang DHPS ay may self-centering T-slot guided jaws na may mataas na gripping force-to-weight ratio; nagbibigay ang DRVS ng makinis at walang backlash na rotary motion. Hindi pantay, buhaghag, o mabibigat na bahagi na nangangailangan ng positibong mechanical clamping at 90°/180° part orientation.
SMT-8M / SPAU / SDE5 Magnetic piston detection at pneumatic pressure feedback Inilalagay ang SMT-8M sa Festo T-slots mula sa itaas at ikinakandado gamit ang quarter-turn screw; ipinapakita ng SPAU ang live pressure sa bar/psi/MPa na may red/green alerts. Kinakailangang PLC end-of-stroke verification at real-time air/vacuum pressure monitoring sa automated lines.
QS Fittings at PUN-H Pneumatic air distribution at flexible drag-chain tubing Tinitiyak ng QS nitrile rubber sealing rings ang agarang bubble-tight connection; nilalabanan ng PUN-H ang moisture hydrolysis at pagtubo ng mikrobyo sa basang lugar. Lahat ng industrial pneumatic circuits, high-flex energy chains, at food/packaging washdown environments.
