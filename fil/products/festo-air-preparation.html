Mga Festo Air Preparation Unit (FRL): Serye ng MS4-LFR/MS6-LFR, MS9/MS12, MS6-SV/MS6-EE at VPPM/LRP

Brand: Festo | Mga Pangunahing Pamantayan: ISO 8573-1, ISO 13849-1 (PL e) | Pandaigdigang Supplier ng Festo Pneumatic
Malinis na Compressed Air, Kaligtasan ng Makina at Tumpak na Kontrol ng Presyon:
Bilang nangungunang pandaigdigang supplier ng Festo pneumatic, nagsusuplay ang WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED ng kumpletong modular na linya ng mga Festo MS-series air preparation (FRL) service unit, safety soft-start/exhaust valve, at electronic proportional pressure regulator. Ang maayos na nakondisyong compressed air ay pumipigil sa maagang pagkasira ng mga valve at cylinder habang pinapataas ang energy efficiency ng buong sistema.

Mga Pangunahing Pamilya ng Air Preparation na Saklaw sa Pahinang Ito:
• Serye ng MS4-LFR / MS6-LFR (Modular Filter Regulators): Compact at mid-size na pinagsamang filter-regulator unit (40 mm at 62 mm grid widths; G1/8 hanggang G1/2) na may 5 µm o 40 µm filtration, lockable rotary knob, integrated pressure gauge, at manual, semi-automatic, o fully automatic condensate drain.
• Serye ng MS9 / MS12 (High-Flow Main Line FRL Combinations): Heavy-duty main supply air preparation unit (90 mm at 124 mm grid widths; G3/4 hanggang G2) na naghahatid ng napakalaking flow rate hanggang 22,000 l/min na may coalescing micro-filters (0.01 µm) at activated carbon filters para sa cleanroom at food-grade air.
• Serye ng MS6-SV / MS6-EE (Safety Soft-Start at Quick Exhaust Valves): Two-channel redundant safety exhaust valve (MS6-SV-E/D) na sertipikado hanggang Performance Level e (PL e) ayon sa ISO 13849-1, kasama ang mga standard electrically actuated on/off soft-start valve (MS6-EE / MS6-DL).
• Serye ng VPPM / LRP (Proportional Pressure Regulators): Closed-loop multi-sensor proportional pressure regulator (VPPM) at precision mechanical regulator (LRP) na may 0–10V, 4–20mA, o IO-Link analog control para sa laser cutting, web tensioning, at welding force control.

Gumagamit ang portfolio ng Festo Air Preparation ng uniporme at tool-free na modular assembly system na nagpapahintulot sa mga inhinyero na pagsamahin ang mga shut-off valve, filter regulator, fine/micro filter, lubricator, branching module, pressure sensor (SPAU), at safety dump valve sa iisang leak-free service unit. Kung kailangan mo ng isang pamalit na filter element, kumpletong pre-assembled MS6 FRL combination, o high-precision VPPM proportional valve, sinusuri ng aming technical team ang port thread sizes (G o NPT), pressure ranges (D5: 0.5–4 bar, D6: 0.5–7 bar, D7: 0.5–12 bar), at bowl/drain options upang matiyak ang 100% factory-compliant na paghahatid.

Arkitektura ng Festo Air Preparation: MS4/MS6 vs. MS9/MS12 vs. MS6-SV vs. VPPM

Gabay sa teknikal na pagpili upang matukoy ang tamang filtration cascade, flow capacity, safety exhaust compliance, at dynamic pressure regulation:

Pamilya ng Serye Papel sa Sistema Pangunahing Kalamangang Inhinyero Inirerekomendang Kondisyon ng Operasyon
MS4-LFR / MS6-LFR Pangunahing particulate filtration at pressure regulation sa antas ng makina Pinagsasama ang centrifugal separator, sintered filter (5 µm o 40 µm), at diaphragm regulator sa iisang compact housing na may reversible flow direction. Mga standard pneumatic control panel na nagsusuplay sa mga cylinder at solenoid valve terminal hanggang 6,500 l/min.
Serye ng MS9 / MS12 High-capacity main header conditioning at multi-stage filtration Kayang pangasiwaan ang napakalaking volume ng hangin hanggang 22,000 l/min na may minimal pressure drop; sumusuporta sa 40 µm → 1 µm → 0.01 µm oil-removal cascades. Main factory ring lines, malalaking press, foundry equipment, at high-consumption packaging plants.
MS6-SV / MS6-EE Kaligtasan ng makina at kontroladong pneumatic startup/exhaust Nagbibigay ang MS6-SV ng sertipikadong dual-channel fail-safe exhaust (PL e) na may integrated soft-start ramp upang maiwasan ang biglaang paggalaw ng cylinder sa power-up. Safety-guarded automated cells, robotic welding stations, at mga press na nangangailangan ng CE / ISO 13849-1 compliance.
Serye ng VPPM / LRP Dynamic closed-loop o ultra-precise static pressure regulation May tatlong integrated pressure sensor ang VPPM para sa mabilis at walang overshoot na tugon sa PLC analog setpoints (0–10V / 4–20mA) at IO-Link diagnostics. Dynamic web tensioning, ultrasonic welding force, paint spraying, at laser cutting gas regulation.
