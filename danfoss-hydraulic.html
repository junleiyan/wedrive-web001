Danfoss Hydraulics FAQ

Q: How do I identify and order a replacement Danfoss hydraulic motor or pump?
A: Provide the 8-digit Danfoss ordering code (e.g., 151-0314 or 151B3004) or the full model code (e.g., OMP 160, OMR 200, OMS 315, OMT 400, or Series 45 JRL060B) from the nameplate. As a specialized Danfoss hydraulic supplier, we verify shaft, flange, and port specs for exact replacement.

Q: Can you supply Eaton Char-Lynn and Vickers by Danfoss hydraulic components?
A: Yes. Following Danfoss's integration of Eaton Hydraulics, we supply the full lineup including Danfoss orbital motors (OMP/OMR/OMS/OMT), Eaton Char-Lynn motors (2000, 4000, 6000 Series), and Vickers by Danfoss vane pumps (V10, V20, 2520VQ) and DG4V valves.

Q: What configurations are available for Danfoss PVG proportional valves (PVG 32, PVG 16, PVG 120)?
A: We supply both individual PVG modules (PVB basic modules, PVE electrical actuators, PVP pump side modules, spools) and complete multi-section load-sensing PVG valve banks built to your flow and pressure requirements.

Q: What industries rely on Danfoss hydraulic pumps, motors, and steering units?
A: Danfoss Power Solutions hydraulics are trusted across construction machinery (excavators, loaders, cranes), agricultural equipment, marine deck winches, forestry machines, and heavy industrial hydraulic power units.
