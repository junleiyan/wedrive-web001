Neugart NPS & NPL Right-Angle Flange Output Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: Precision Right-Angle Flange Output Planetary Gearheads (NPS064 to NPS110 Range: 18 Nm to 460 Nm)

The Neugart NPS & NPL Right-Angle Flange Output Series (featured model: NPS090 2-Stage 1:10 Ratio) is Neugart's high-precision right-angle drive solution, engineered specifically for high-load, space-critical servo applications requiring **flange output according to ISO 9409-1**. By placing the input motor at a 90° angle via precision hypoid/spiral bevel gearing combined with planetary reduction, the NPS/NPL series dramatically reduces axial installation length while providing extraordinary torsional stiffness. Designed with heavy-duty angular contact or tapered roller output bearings, these gearboxes withstand high radial and axial overhung loads, making them the ultimate drive interface for rack-and-pinion linear gantries, Delta robots, belt indexers, and rotary tables. Featuring low torsional backlash (**<3 to <6 arcmin**) and lifetime synthetic lubrication, the NPS (economy right-angle flange) and NPL (precision right-angle flange) lines offer dynamic motion control across 3 frame sizes (064, 090, 110). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter selection for Neugart NPS & NPL right-angle flange gearboxes worldwide.
