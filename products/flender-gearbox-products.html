FLENDER Gearboxes, Planetary Drives & Couplings

Recognized globally for extreme torque performance and mechanical durability, FLENDER sets the industry standard for power transmission. Discover our comprehensive catalog covering Universal Helical & Bevel-Helical Gear Units (H & B series), Industrial Planetary Drives (PLANUREX, NEO PLANUREX, SIP, FLENDER ONE, DUORED), MOTOX Series Modular Gearmotors (originally developed by Siemens/Flender, transferred under Innomotics, and continuously supported via original OEM manufacturing), and High-Performance Industrial Couplings (N-EUPEX, RUPEX, ZAPEX, ARPEX, FLUDEX). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Flender equipment, spare parts, and exact 1-to-1 replacement units worldwide.

Expert Technical Sourcing & System Integration Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in technical sourcing, model designation decoding, and global express supply for the complete FLENDER Power Transmission & Couplings portfolio. Whether your cement plant, steel mill, paper pulping reactor, or port crane requires heavy helical speed reducers (H2SH, H3SH, B3SH, B4SH), industrial planetary units (PLANUREX 3, SIP), MOTOX gearmotors (D/Z 38, F 48, K 88), or flexible pin couplings (N-EUPEX Type A, B, H), all units can be supplied pre-tested directly through verified OEM factory distribution channels.

By providing us with your existing Flender nameplate order code or serial number (e.g., H2SH 08 - 2003456-010-1 or N-EUPEX A 200), our engineering team verifies exact shaft extension dimensions, ratio verification, thermal rating requirements, and mounting positions to guarantee a 100% compliant replacement quotation and rapid delivery.
