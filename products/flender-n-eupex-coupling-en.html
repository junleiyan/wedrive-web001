Flender N-EUPEX Type A 160 Flexible Pin Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: N-EUPEX & N-EUPEX DS Flexible Pin & Bush Couplings (Size 58 to 710)

The Flender N-EUPEX Type A 160 is the world's most widely specified three-part flexible pin and bush elastomer coupling for industrial power transmission[cite: 22]. Engineered to compensate for shaft misalignments (angular, radial, and axial) while effectively damping torsional vibrations and shock loads[cite: 22], the Type A three-part design allows replacement of flexible elastomer packets without moving the driven or driving machinery[cite: 22]. Available in 23 standard sizes (size 58 to 710) covering nominal torques from 19 Nm to 85,000 Nm[cite: 22], N-EUPEX couplings feature fail-safe cast iron construction with flexible NBR or TPU packets[cite: 22], and are widely specified across pump drives, fan drives, belt conveyors, compressors, mill drives, and crane machinery[cite: 22].
