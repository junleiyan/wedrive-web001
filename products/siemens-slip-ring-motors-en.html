Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS HV Wound-Rotor Slip-Ring Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS HV Slip-Ring (High Starting Torque Modular Heavy Range)
High Starting Torque Heavy Engineering — Siemens to Innomotics:
The SIMOTICS HV wound-rotor slip-ring motor architecture represents Siemens' specialized high-voltage drive solution designed specifically for applications that require exceptionally high starting torque while strictly limiting starting inrush current on weak power grids. Following Siemens' corporate carve-out of its large-scale motors and drives division, this heavy industrial product line is engineered and manufactured under the independent brand Innomotics.

Engineering Superiority for Extreme Load Acceleration:
• Maximum Starting Torque at Minimum Inrush: By connecting an external Liquid Resistance Starter (LRS) or stepped rotor resistor bank to the wound-rotor slip rings, starting torque can be adjusted up to breakdown torque levels ($M_K$) while holding the line starting current below 1.2 to 1.5 times the rated current ($I_N$).
• Motorized Brush-Lifting Device (Optional): To eliminate continuous brush wear, carbon dust accumulation, and routine ring maintenance during regular running, a heavy-duty motorized brush-lifting and short-circuiting mechanism is available to automatically lift the brushes once rated speed is reached.
• MICALASTIC® VPI Insulation: Both the high-voltage stator and the three-phase wound rotor utilize Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (Class F, evaluated to Class B temperature rise), providing superior mechanical rigidity against centrifugal forces during heavy start cycles in cement, mining, and metal shredding plants.

The Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS HV Wound-Rotor Slip-Ring Series delivers exceptional mechanical durability, high thermal overload capacity, and smooth acceleration for machinery with enormous moments of inertia. Available across supply voltages of 3.3 kV, 6.0 kV, 6.6 kV, 10.0 kV, and 11.0 kV (50 Hz and 60 Hz) spanning power ratings from 500 kW to 15,000 kW+ in IEC frame sizes 450 to 900, these drives feature modular cooling configurations (IC611 air-to-air heat exchanger or IC81W air-to-water heat exchanger). Standard equipment includes hydrodynamic sleeve bearings with forced lubrication, segregated slip-ring inspection housings with IP55/IP65 protection, vibration surveillance probes, and embedded Pt100 RTDs in stator slots, rotor windings, and bearing shells. They are specified worldwide for cement ball mills, SAG mills, raw material crushers, underground mine hoists, shredders, and heavy blast furnace exhaust fans.
