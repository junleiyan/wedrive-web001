Siemens & Innomotics Gearboxes, Gearmotors & Low-Voltage Motors

Brand & Ownership Evolution Notice:
To optimize its industrial portfolio, Siemens carved out its low-voltage AC motors and geared motors businesses into an independent enterprise named Innomotics. As a result, the renowned MOTOX and SIMOGEAR gearmotor lines, along with standard SIMOTICS 1LE0/1LE1/1TL0/1MB0 Low-Voltage AC Motors, are now produced and supported under the Innomotics brand identity.

Note: Heavy industrial gearboxes (such as the H, B, and PLANUREX planetary series) were previously carved out and operate under the independent Flender brand. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original equipment and parts across all these lineages directly via authentic OEM factory channels, ensuring seamless 1-to-1 mechanical compatibility for your legacy Siemens, Innomotics, and Flender drives.
