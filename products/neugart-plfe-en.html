Neugart PLFE & WPLFE Flange Output Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: Compact Flange Output Planetary Gearheads (PLFE064 to PLFE110 Range: 20 Nm to 460 Nm)

The Neugart PLFE & WPLFE Flange Output Planetary Series (featured model: PLFE090 1-Stage 1:5 Ratio) is Neugart's ultra-compact flange output drive platform, engineered specifically for high-torsional-stiffness servo applications requiring direct mechanical coupling via **ISO 9409-1 flange mounting**. By replacing the traditional output shaft with a compact flange face, the PLFE (inline) and WPLFE (right-angle) series drastically shorten overall drive length while enabling direct bolt-on mounting of pinions, belt pulleys, rotary tables, and drive flanges. Built with deep-groove ball bearings and precision spur gearing, PLFE gearheads provide exceptional tipping moment rigidity, low torsional backlash (**<3 to <5 arcmin**), and high dynamic efficiency (up to 97%). Available across 3 modular frame sizes (064, 090, 110) covering 1-stage (3:1 to 10:1) and 2-stage (12:1 to 100:1) reduction ratios with output torques up to **460 Nm**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter selection for Neugart PLFE & WPLFE flange output planetary gearboxes worldwide.
