Rossi A Series Worm Gearmotors & Speed Reducers

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Monolithic Worm Gearmotors & Reducers (Size 32 to 250 Range: 30 Nm to 19,000 Nm)

The Rossi A Series Worm Gearmotor Platform (featured model: MR A 63 UO3A 1.5 kW 1:30 Ratio) represents Rossi's flagship universal right-angle drive system, engineered specifically for high reliability, smooth motion, and extreme structural rigidity in automated conveyors, packaging machinery, food processing, mixers, and general material handling. Manufactured around Rossi's signature **monolithic (single-piece) cast-iron housing**, A Series gearboxes eliminate split-casing joints and gasket distortion, achieving zero oil leakage and high resistance to heavy overhung loads. Featuring case-hardened ground alloy steel worm shafts paired with high-tin bronze worm gear rims, these units deliver optimized sliding contact efficiency, silent power transmission, and self-locking capabilities on high ratios. Covering continuous torque output ratings from 30 Nm up to 19,000 Nm across 14 frame sizes (Size 32 to Size 250) and gear reduction ratios from 7:1 to 16,000:1 (in combined double-worm configurations). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Rossi A Series gearmotors, replacement units, and exact technical model code decoding across global industrial applications.
