Baldor-Reliance Industrial DC Motors & BSM Brushless AC Servo Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: PMDC, Laminated DC & BSM Brushless Servo Motors (1/4 HP to 300 HP / 0.45 Nm to 40 Nm)

The Baldor-Reliance Industrial DC & BSM Servo Motor Series (featured model: BSM90N-275AA Brushless Servo & CDP3445 PMDC Motor) provides specialized high-dynamic drive solutions for precision motion control, variable-speed machinery, and legacy DC drive retrofits. Baldor's **BSM N-Series & C-Series Brushless AC Servo Motors** feature high-energy Neodymium-Iron-Boron (NdFeB) magnets, low-inertia rotor designs, IP65 sealing, and custom resolver/absolute encoder options for high-acceleration packaging, printing, CNC, and robotic systems. Baldor's **Industrial DC Motors** encompass compact Permanent Magnet DC (PMDC) units (CDP / DPM Series, 1/4 HP to 5 HP) and heavy-duty Laminated Frame DC Motors (DPF / C-Face Series, up to 300 HP) designed for SCR drive power controllers. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, exact spec number verification, feedback alignment, and full factory replacement support across the complete Baldor DC and BSM Servo catalog.
