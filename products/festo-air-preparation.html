Festo Air Preparation Units (FRL): MS4-LFR/MS6-LFR, MS9/MS12, MS6-SV/MS6-EE & VPPM/LRP Series

Brand: Festo | Core Standards: ISO 8573-1, ISO 13849-1 (PL e) | Global Festo Pneumatic Supplier
Clean Compressed Air, Machine Safety & Precision Pressure Control:
As a leading global Festo pneumatic supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies the full modular range of Festo MS-series air preparation (FRL) service units, safety soft-start/exhaust valves, and electronic proportional pressure regulators. Properly conditioned compressed air prevents premature valve and cylinder wear while maximizing system energy efficiency.

Core Air Preparation Families Covered on This Page:
• MS4-LFR / MS6-LFR Series (Modular Filter Regulators): Compact and mid-size integrated filter-regulator units (40 mm & 62 mm grid widths; G1/8 to G1/2) featuring 5 µm or 40 µm filtration, lockable rotary knobs, integrated pressure gauges, and manual, semi-automatic, or fully automatic condensate drains.
• MS9 / MS12 Series (High-Flow Main Line FRL Combinations): Heavy-duty main supply air preparation units (90 mm & 124 mm grid widths; G3/4 to G2) delivering massive flow rates up to 22,000 l/min with coalescing micro-filters (0.01 µm) and activated carbon filters for cleanroom and food-grade air.
• MS6-SV / MS6-EE Series (Safety Soft-Start & Quick Exhaust Valves): Two-channel redundant safety exhaust valves (MS6-SV-E/D) certified up to Performance Level e (PL e) per ISO 13849-1 alongside standard electrically actuated on/off soft-start valves (MS6-EE / MS6-DL).
• VPPM / LRP Series (Proportional Pressure Regulators): Closed-loop multi-sensor proportional pressure regulators (VPPM) and precision mechanical regulators (LRP) with 0–10V, 4–20mA, or IO-Link analog control for laser cutting, web tensioning, and welding force control.

The Festo Air Preparation portfolio uses a uniform, tool-free modular assembly system that lets engineers combine shut-off valves, filter regulators, fine/micro filters, lubricators, branching modules, pressure sensors (SPAU), and safety dump valves into a single leak-free service unit. Whether you need a single replacement filter element, a complete pre-assembled MS6 FRL combination, or a high-precision VPPM proportional valve, our technical team verifies port thread sizes (G or NPT), pressure ranges (D5: 0.5–4 bar, D6: 0.5–7 bar, D7: 0.5–12 bar), and bowl/drain options to guarantee 100% factory-compliant delivery.

Engineering selection guide to specify the right filtration cascade, flow capacity, safety exhaust compliance, and dynamic pressure regulation:

Series Family System Role Key Engineering Advantage Recommended Operating Condition

Standard pneumatic control panels supplying cylinders and solenoid valve terminals up to 6,500 l/min.
MS9 / MS12 Series High-capacity main header conditioning & multi-stage filtration Handles massive air volumes up to 22,000 l/min with minimal pressure drop; supports multi-stage 40 µm → 1 µm → 0.01 µm oil-removal cascades. Main factory ring lines, large presses, foundry equipment, and high-consumption packaging plants.
MS6-SV / MS6-EE Machine safety & controlled pneumatic startup/exhaust MS6-SV provides certified dual-channel fail-safe exhaust (PL e) with integrated soft-start ramp to prevent dangerous cylinder lurching on power-up. Safety-guarded automated cells, robotic welding stations, and presses requiring CE / ISO 13849-1 compliance.
VPPM / LRP Series Dynamic closed-loop or ultra-precise static pressure regulation VPPM features three integrated pressure sensors for rapid, overshoot-free response to PLC analog setpoints (0–10V / 4–20mA) and IO-Link diagnostics. Dynamic web tensioning, ultrasonic welding force, paint spraying, and laser cutting gas regulation.
