TR-Electronic Incremental Encoders: IEV58 & IEH58 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: IEV58, IEH58, IEV582 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Dynamic Speed Tracking & Programmable Flexibility:
As a leading TR incremental encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic, factory-direct TR-Electronic rotary encoders. The flagship TR IEV58 and IEH58 series represent a leap forward in dynamic drive control, allowing users to program exact pulse resolutions to drastically reduce spare parts inventory.

Engineering Excellence for Motor Control:
• PC-Programmable Resolution: Eliminate the need for multiple fixed-pulse encoders. The IEV58 / IEV582 series can be freely programmed from 1 up to 65,536 pulses per revolution (ppr), ensuring exact matching to any drive requirement.
• Flexible Output Logic: Standard models support both Push-Pull (HTL) and RS422 (TTL) output signals, accommodating both long cable runs in heavy industry and high-frequency precision inputs for CNC servo drives.
• Superior Optical Scanning: Delivering exceptional jitter-free signal quality (including Sin/Cos 1Vpp options) required for highly dynamic velocity loops in printing presses, web tensioning, and high-speed packaging machinery.

The TR Encoders IEV58 (Solid Shaft) & IEH58 (Hollow Shaft) Series guarantee continuous operational reliability and precise speed feedback. Built on the global 58mm industrial standard size, these incremental encoders provide robust bearing structures capable of withstanding high axial and radial loads. Whether you need a drop-in replacement for an older ZE65 incremental encoder or are optimizing a new conveyor drive system, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the perfect configuration.
