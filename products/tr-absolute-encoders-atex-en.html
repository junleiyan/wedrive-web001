TR-Electronic Absolute Encoders: ATEX Explosion-Proof Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Approvals: ATEX Zone 1/21, IECEx | Global TR Encode Supplier
Certified Safety for Hazardous Environments:
As a leading TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides highly specialized TR-Electronic explosion-proof rotary encoders. The TR ATEX series is engineered with flameproof enclosures (Ex d) to guarantee absolute measurement precision in the most volatile industrial atmospheres.

Engineering Excellence for Explosive Zones:
• Maximum Safety Standards: Compliant with the ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU and IECEx, these encoders are certified for use in Zone 1 and Zone 2 (Gas) as well as Zone 21 and Zone 22 (Dust) environments.
• Flameproof Enclosures (Ex d): The robust ADV 88 and AEA 115 series utilize pressure-resistant aluminum or stainless steel housings, ensuring that any internal ignition cannot propagate to the explosive external atmosphere.
• Industrial Ethernet in Ex Zones: TR-Electronic bridges the gap between safety and Industry 4.0, offering ATEX-certified encoders equipped with advanced protocols like PROFINET, EtherCAT, PROFIBUS, and SSI for seamless integration into modern process control systems.

The TR Encoders ATEX Series delivers uncompromising reliability for critical applications in the oil and gas industry, petrochemical plants, offshore drilling rigs, and industrial paint shops. With protective housings capable of withstanding extreme mechanical impacts and corrosive chemicals, these absolute rotary encoders ensure your hazardous area automation runs safely and precisely. Whether you need a drop-in replacement for a legacy PROFIBUS ATEX encoder or are designing a new facility utilizing PROFINET, our technical sourcing team will verify the correct ATEX zoning and interface requirements.
