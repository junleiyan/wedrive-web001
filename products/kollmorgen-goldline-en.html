Kollmorgen Goldline B & M Series Permanent Magnet Servo Motors

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: Legacy Permanent Magnet AC Servo Motors (B-102 to B-806 / M-403 to M-806 Range: 0.84 Nm to 120 Nm)

The Kollmorgen Goldline Servo Motor Series (featured model: B-202-A-11 2.5 Nm 4000 RPM) is Kollmorgen's classic permanent magnet AC servo motor platform, widely recognized for its rugged thermal construction, high peak acceleration torque, and industrial reliability. Divided into the **Goldline B-Series** (low-inertia rotor for rapid start/stop indexing) and **Goldline M-Series** (medium/high-inertia rotor designed for high load inertia matching), these motors incorporate high-coercivity permanent magnets, Class H winding insulation, MS style military connectors, and heavy-duty IP65 housing seals. Covering continuous stall torque ratings from 0.84 Nm up to 120 Nm across 6 primary frame sizes, Goldline motors utilize factory-aligned 2-pole resolvers for robust feedback in harsh vibration environments. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, exact part number decoding, factory repair-replacement units, and 100% compliant mechanical/electrical upgrade paths mapping legacy Goldline motors to modern **Kollmorgen AKM and AKM2G series servo motors**.
