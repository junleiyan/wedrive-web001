SEW P032 Series Planetary Gearbox & Combination Gearmotors

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Series: P Series Industrial Planetary Gear Units (Size 032)

The SEW P032 Series is a high-capacity heavy industrial planetary gear unit engineered for heavy-duty drive solutions demanding extreme torque capacity (~84 kNm nominal torque) in a space-saving housing layout[cite: 24]. By combining the P032 planetary stage with SEW's standardized primary gear units—including the K-Series (Helical-Bevel), R-Series (Helical), F-Series (Parallel Shaft), and S-Series (Helical-Worm)—this modular drive system delivers massive reduction ratios, outstanding mechanical efficiency, and superior thermal stability[cite: 24]. It is extensively utilized across heavy apron feeders, bucket wheel reclaimers, industrial mixers, heavy shredders, winches, and mining bulk material processing drives[cite: 24].

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

We specialize in providing complete industrial drive packages and replacement solutions for the entire SEW-Eurodrive P032 series planetary range[cite: 24]. Whether you require a right-angle bevel-planetary unit (PK032 / P032 KF)[cite: 24], coaxial helical-planetary unit (PR032 / P032 RF)[cite: 24], parallel shaft unit (PF032 / P032 FF)[cite: 24], or worm combination (PS032 / P032 SF)[cite: 24], all configurations can be delivered pre-assembled with modern IE3 DRN series motors (DRN180M4 to DRN225M4).

When retrofitting an existing heavy application featuring an obsolete primary drive motor (DV180, DV200, or DV225)[cite: 24], upgrading to an IE3 DRN motor ensures complete dimensional interchangeability while substantially reducing operating thermal stress and energy costs. Simply send us your existing SEW serial number or nameplate code (including options like BE brake, TF thermistor, or backstop), and our engineering team will provide a 100% compliant replacement specification and quotation.
