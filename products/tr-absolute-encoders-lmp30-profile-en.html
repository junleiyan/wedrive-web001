TR-Electronic Compact Linear Systems: LMP-30 Profile Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: LMP-30, LMP30 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Ultra-Flat Profile & Wear-Free Magnetostrictive Precision:
As a leading TR linear encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic TR-Electronic compact linear measurement systems. The TR LMP-30 Profile series combines non-contact magnetostrictive technology with an exceptionally slim, flat aluminum extrusion—engineered specifically for space-restricted factory automation, packaging lines, and OEM machinery.

Engineering Excellence for Space-Critical Linear Axes:
• Low-Profile Slim Design: Featuring a compact 30mm cross-section, the LMP-30 fits effortlessly into tight machine frames, pneumatic slides, and compact handling systems where standard 46mm profile transducers are too bulky.
• Zero Mechanical Wear: Utilizing a free-floating or captive slider position magnet that moves along the profile without physical contact, the LMP-30 completely eliminates the wiper wear, noise, and dead-band issues of traditional linear potentiometers.
• Modern Automation Interfaces: Engineered for rapid plug-and-play integration into decentralized control architectures, offering native support for Analog (0-10V, 4-20mA), IO-Link, SSI, and CANopen.

The TR Encoders LMP-30 Profile Series delivers reliable, absolute linear position feedback immediately upon power-up—eliminating time-consuming homing sequences. Resistant to industrial dust, cutting fluids, and continuous machine vibration, these compact linear transducers maximize machine uptime. Whether you are upgrading worn-out potentiometers on an injection molding machine or sourcing OEM sensors for high-speed assembly lines, our technical team ensures you receive the exact stroke length and electrical output required.

TR-Electronic LMP-30 Cursor Topologies & Structural Concepts

Engineering comparison between the position magnet options and compact profile benefits of the LMP-30 series:

Cursor / Feature Design Characteristic Mechanical Advantage Ideal Industrial Application
Guided Slider Magnet Captive cursor rides smoothly inside the anodized aluminum T-slot rail with ball-joint linkage. Compensates for lateral and angular installation misalignments up to ±1.5 mm without binding. Direct mechanical linkage to moving machine carriages, clamping jaws, and pneumatic slides.
Free-Floating Magnet Hovering magnet mounted to the moving part with a 1–4 mm air gap above the sensor profile. 100% contact-free operation—zero friction, zero mechanical drag, and infinite mechanical life. High-cycle, high-speed reciprocating axes where mechanical contact cannot be tolerated.
30mm Flat Extrusion Ultra-slim height with adjustable sliding mounting clamps along the entire length. Enables flush mounting inside narrow structural channels without custom machining. OEM packaging machinery, textile looms, and compact pick-and-place linear gantries.
