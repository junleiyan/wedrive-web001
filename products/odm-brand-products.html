WeDrive ODM Brand: High-Performance Chinese Replacement & Procurement Solutions

To solve long delivery lead times and high capital costs associated with imported power transmission drives, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides comprehensive WeDrive ODM Brand Solutions. We bridge global industrial clients with China's top-tier drive manufacturing capabilities through three specialized service pillars: 1:1 Dimensional Drawing Replacement Drives (designated with WD model prefixes such as WDKAF127, WDR87, WDFF77), China Industrial Procurement Agency, and Full-Process Custom Mold Tooling & OEM Manufacturing.

Important Technical & Feasibility Disclaimer:
• Technical Evaluation & Verification Required: Please note that NOT ALL products or legacy machinery models can be directly replaced or recreated with new custom molds. All technical replacement feasibility and tooling projects must undergo rigorous CAD verification, material analysis, and engineering confirmation by WeDrive and partner factory technical teams.
• Engineering & Tooling Costs Notice: Custom pattern making, foundry mold development, 3D scanning, or non-standard engineering evaluations may incur upfront tooling fees, sample charges, or technical assessment expenses depending on project complexity.
• Brand Cross-Reference: Products supplied under the WeDrive ODM Brand (utilizing model prefixes like WDKAF, WDR, WDFF, WDK, WDS) are independent, high-performance alternative drives. All model prefixes and technical parameter references are utilized exclusively for physical dimension matching and performance comparison.

1. 1:1 Interchangeable Drive Solutions (Identical Dimension & Parameter Replacement)

When existing imported gearmotors or reducers fail, long factory lead times can halt production lines for months. WeDrive provides exact 1:1 physical and mechanical replacement gearmotors designated with the WD model prefix (e.g., WDKAF127, WDR87, WDFF77, WDK87, WDS67).

Detailed CAD Drawing Comparison 100% Drop-In Installation

Our engineering team performs a rigorous 2D/3D CAD overlay comparison checking output shaft diameter, keyway dimensions, center height, foot bolt hole centers, and motor flange interfaces. We guarantee 100% drop-in installation without any site modification.

Substantial Cost Optimization 30% - 60% Savings

By supplying directly from China's top-tier high-precision gear manufacturing lines, WeDrive WD-prefixed units offer identical torque output, gear ratio precision, and service factors at 30% to 60% lower costs, reducing capital expenditure dramatically.

2. China Sourcing Agency & Procurement Services (Purchasing Products to Client Specifications)

Finding reliable, high-quality industrial suppliers in China can be challenging due to language barriers, technical miscommunication, and quality control risks. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED acts as your resident China Industrial Procurement Agent, managing the end-to-end supply chain for your specific gearmotor, motor, or gearbox requirements.

Tailored Vendor Search & Factory Audits Verified Manufacturers

We evaluate dozens of specialized Chinese manufacturers based on your technical specification sheet. We conduct on-site factory audits verifying ISO 9001 certifications, CNC machining precision, heat treatment equipment, and assembly line cleanliness.

Rigorous Pre-Shipment Quality Control Full Test Reports

Our QA engineers inspect every batch before dispatch. We perform gear tooth contact pattern checks, coordinate measuring machine (CMM) shaft audits, noise/vibration testing, oil tightness checks, and full-load trial runs with video documentation.

3. Custom Mold Engineering & Full-Process OEM Tooling (Custom Tooling & Technical Feasibility)

For discontinued legacy machinery, proprietary equipment designs, or special OEM housing requirements, standard off-the-shelf gearboxes will not fit. Subject to factory technical evaluation and feasibility approval, WeDrive offers Full-Process Custom Tooling, Pattern Development, and Mold Creation tailored to your exact CAD specifications.

Technical Verification & Engineering Assessment Pre-Production Review

Not all obsolete gearboxes can be recreated. Our factory engineers evaluate wall thickness, stress points, casting metallurgy, and gear tooth profiles to determine feasibility before initiating pattern development.

Transparent Tooling & Development Costs Tooling & Pattern Fees

Custom mold fabrication and non-standard gear tooling typically involve upfront pattern fees, sample production costs, or engineering design expenses. We provide clear, itemized cost estimates prior to project launch.
