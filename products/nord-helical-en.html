NORD SK 32 UNICASE Helical Inline Gearmotor

Brand: NORD DRIVESYSTEMS | Series: UNICASE Helical (SK 02...10382) & NORDBLOC.1 (SK 071.1...973.1)

The NORD SK 32 is a high-efficiency 2-stage inline helical gearmotor engineered with NORD DRIVESYSTEMS' signature UNICASE single-piece cast iron housing[cite: 20]. By eliminating casing joints under stress, the SK 32 ensures superior structural rigidity, leak-proof reliability, extended bearing operational life, and quiet gear mesh transmission[cite: 20]. Available across a versatile array of mounting styles—including foot-mounted (SK 32), B5 flange (SK 32F), B14 face flange (SK 32Z), and foot/flange combination (SK 32FZ)[cite: 20]—it is extensively deployed across conveyor belts, packaging machinery, pumping systems, and industrial automation drives[cite: 20].
