KSB Amarex Submersible Sewage & Wastewater Pumps

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: Submersible Non-Clogging Sewage & Sludge Pumps (Flow: Up to 3,000 m³/h | Head: Up to 120 m)

The KSB Amarex Submersible Sewage Pump Series (encompassing **Amarex N** and heavy-duty **Amarex KRT** models, featured model: Amarex KRT F 80-210) is KSB's flagship fluid handling platform engineered for heavy-duty municipal wastewater treatment plants, industrial effluent pumping, storm-water lift stations, and raw sewage transport containing stringy solids, sludge, and fibrous materials. Driven by integrated **ATEX explosion-proof submersible electric motors** (available in high-efficiency IE3 variants), Amarex pumps feature non-clogging impeller geometries including free-flow vortex impellers (**F**), cutter impellers (**S**), single-vane impellers (**E**), and closed multi-channel impellers (**K**). Constructed with heavy-duty grey cast iron (JL1040), Duplex stainless steel, or wear-resistant white iron, Amarex units incorporate bi-directional mechanical seals in tandem arrangement with an environmentally friendly oil reservoir. Operating reliably across discharge sizes from **DN 50 to DN 300** with motor outputs up to **220 kW**, delivering maximum discharge heads up to **120 meters** and capacities up to **3,000 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, impeller selection, motor power matching, and global express supply for KSB Amarex submersible pumps worldwide.
