Baldor-Reliance RPM AC Inverter Duty & Vector Drive Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: Laminated Frame Inverter & Vector Duty Motors (5 HP to 1000 HP)

The Baldor-Reliance RPM AC Vector Drive Motor Series (featured model: DLP2530 30 HP FL250 1750 RPM) is North America's standard-setting laminated frame motor platform engineered specifically for closed-loop variable frequency drive (VFD) vector operation. Built with a compact, square laminated stator frame architecture, these motors deliver exceptionally low rotor inertia for rapid dynamic acceleration, peak torque response, and thermal efficiency. Designed to provide continuous **1000:1 constant torque down to zero speed (0 RPM)**, RPM AC motors feature Class H ISR® (Inverter Spike Resistant) copper winding insulation, integrated quadrature encoder options (1024 / 2048 PPR), insulated non-drive end bearings to eliminate VFD shaft currents, and auxiliary blower cooling (TEBC / DPBV) for zero-speed full torque operation. Covering output power ratings from 5 HP up to 1000 HP across square laminated frame sizes FL180 to FL440, Baldor RPM AC vector motors deliver 100% direct mechanical and drive alignment for plastics extruders, paper winders, overhead cranes, mine hoists, and high-precision web tension lines.
