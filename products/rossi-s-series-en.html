Rossi S Series Heavy Duty Industrial Gearboxes

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Heavy Industrial Monolithic Parallel & Bevel-Helical Gearboxes (Size 140 to 400 Range: 10,000 Nm to 400,000 Nm)

The Rossi S Series Industrial Gearbox Platform (featured model: S 2I 200 UO2A 1:25 Ratio) is Rossi's heavy-duty industrial drive series, engineered specifically to handle high continuous output torque, heavy shock loads, and high thermal dissipation in mining conveyors, bucket elevators, mixers, cranes, and heavy processing machinery. Built around Rossi's signature **single-piece monolithic cast-iron housing**, S Series gearboxes eliminate split-casing joints and gasket distortion under load, achieving superior oil sealing and maximum resistance to torsional stress. Featuring carburized, hardened, and precision-ground alloy steel helical and spiral bevel gear sets, these units deliver maximum fatigue strength and quiet power transmission. Available in **Parallel Shaft (2, 3, and 4-stage helical: S 2I, S 3I, S 4I)** and **Right-Angle Shaft (2, 3, and 4-stage bevel-helical: S CI, S 2IV, S 3IV)** configurations across 12 heavy frame sizes (Size 140 to Size 400), covering torque output capacity up to **400,000 Nm** and gear ratios from 2:1 to 1,250:1. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Rossi S Series heavy industrial gear units, forced oil cooling packages, and technical replacement support for global process plants.
