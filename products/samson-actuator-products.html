Samson Process Automation Engineering Advantages & Technical Systems

Samson control valves and actuation systems lead the process automation industry through modular construction and intelligent diagnostic integration[cite: 12]:

1. Modular Valve & Trim Engineering Exchangeable Trim Sets

Samson Type 3241 and 3251 control valves feature top-entry valve bodies that allow rapid replacement of plug and seat trim sets without removing the valve body from the pipeline[cite: 12]. Trim options include equal percentage, linear, perforated noise-reduction cages (AC-1/AC-2), and Stellited/carbide hard-facing for cavitating liquids and erosive steam flows[cite: 12].

2. Integrated Diaphragm & Positioner Mounting Zero Air Piping Leak

The Samson Type 3277 pneumatic actuator features an internal air duct design that connects directly to Type 3730 or Type 3725 positioners without external tubing[cite: 12]. This prevents tubing mechanical damage, eliminates ambient air leakage, and secures IP66 / NEMA 4X weatherproofing in harsh offshore and chemical environments[cite: 12].
