Bonfiglioli TQ Precision Planetary Servo Gearbox (TQ & TKB Series)

Brand: Bonfiglioli Riduttori | Category: Low-Backlash Precision Servo Planetary Drives (TQ 060-170 & TKB 060-170)

The Bonfiglioli TQ 090 (featured model: TQ 090 1/10 Low-Backlash) represents the pinnacle of motion control engineering from Bonfiglioli's high-precision TQ Coaxial and TKB Right-Angle Bevel-Planetary series. Designed for high dynamic servo applications, fast indexers, and cyclic indexing motion, Bonfiglioli precision planetary gearboxes achieve extremely low torsional backlash (< 3 arcmin for single stage, < 5 arcmin for 2-stage), high torsional stiffness, and smooth continuous rotation up to 6,000 rpm input speeds. Manufactured with high-strength alloy steel helical gearing, full needle roller planet bearings, and integrated motor clamping couplings, TQ and TKB series gearboxes support output torques from 30 Nm up to 1,000 Nm across 6 standard frame sizes (060 to 170). With universal AC servo motor input adapters, Bonfiglioli precision planetaries provide 100% direct 1-to-1 mechanical replacement for Neugart PLN/WPLN, Wittenstein Alpha LP/TP, and Apex Dynamics AB/AF servo reducers across CNC machine tool feed axes, industrial packaging, semiconductor handling, and multi-axis robotics.
