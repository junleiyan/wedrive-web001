Neugart High-Precision Low-Backlash Planetary Gearboxes

As a premier German manufacturer of precision servo power transmission solutions, Neugart GmbH is synonymous with ultra-low backlash, extreme torque density, and silent high-speed operation. Explore our comprehensive product catalog covering Neugart Economy Line (PLE, WPLE, PLQE), Precision Line (PLN, WPLN, PSN), Flange Output Reducers (PLFE, NPS), and Hygienic Design Stainless Steel Planetary Gearboxes (HLA). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Neugart planetary gearboxes with full factory motor mounting kit options and worldwide express delivery.

German Motion Precision — Neugart GmbH:
Neugart sets global benchmarks in dynamic motion control, engineering planetary gearheads that feature patented PCS shaft clamping, optimized sun gear bearings, and precision helical/spur gear teeth designed for zero-slop coupling with all major servo motors.

Engineering Advantage & Technical Ecosystem:
• Ultra-Low Backlash: Precision series planetary units offering backlash ratings down to <1 arcmin for extreme positioning accuracy.
• PCS Clamping System: Friction-locked, dynamic input shaft coupling system compatible with Siemens, Kollmorgen, SEW, Fanuc, and Yaskawa servo motors.
• Modular Mounting Versatility: Available in inline shaft, right-angle bevel-planetary, compact flange output, and IP69K hygienic washdown constructions.
• WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sizing cross-referencing, ratio verification, and global express supply.
