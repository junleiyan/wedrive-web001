3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) regarding China Industrial Sourcing

Q1: How does WeDrive charge for its China sourcing agency services?

A: We offer flexible commercial models including transparent agency commission percentages or fixed project-based service fees. All factory-direct pricing from Chinese manufacturers is presented transparently to the client without hidden markups.

Q2: What happens if a sourced gearbox or motor fails technical inspection during pre-shipment testing?

A: WeDrive QA engineers enforce strict acceptance criteria. If a product fails dimensional tolerances, noise limits, or pressure tests during inspection, the factory must rework or replace the unit at their expense before shipment authorization is granted.

Q3: Can WeDrive help consolidate shipments from multiple Chinese industrial suppliers?

A: Yes. We provide central warehousing and cargo consolidation services. We receive equipment from different specialized factories, inspect all items, wrap them in seaworthy fumigated wooden crates, and consolidate them into a single sea or air freight shipment to optimize shipping costs.

Q4: What technical documentation is needed to begin an industrial sourcing project?

A: To begin, provide us with a detailed technical specification sheet, product nameplate photos, dimensional drawings, electrical requirements (voltage, frequency, IP rating), and required quantity. Providing environmental conditions (temperature, duty cycle) helps us verify thermal capacities.
