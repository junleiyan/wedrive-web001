Busch Mink Dry Claw Vacuum Pumps & Compressors

Brand: Busch Vacuum Solutions | Category: Oil-Free Dry Claw Industrial Vacuum Pumps & Compressors (Pumping Speed: 40 m³/h to 1,200 m³/h)

The Busch Mink Dry Claw Vacuum Pump Series (featured model: MM 1202 AV) represents Busch's energy-efficient, non-contacting claw vacuum technology engineered for industrial packaging, CNC routing tables, wood processing, pneumatic conveying, and environmental suction systems. Driven by two claw-shaped rotors moving in opposite directions without metal-to-metal contact, Mink vacuum pumps require no operating fluid in the compression chamber. This 100% oil-free compression design completely eliminates oil changes, filter cartridge replacement, and process gas contamination while providing exceptional power savings of up to 60% compared to conventional vacuum pumps. Engineered with durable cast-iron chambers, precision timing gears, and integrated inlet filters, Mink units deliver continuous ultimate pressures down to **20 hPa (mbar)** across capacities ranging from **40 m³/h to 1,200 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model cross-referencing, and global express supply for Busch Mink dry claw vacuum pumps worldwide.
