Neugart WPLE & WPLQE Right-Angle Economy Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: Economy Right-Angle Bevel-Planetary Gearheads (Frame 040 to 160 Range: 8 Nm to 1,000 Nm)

The Neugart WPLE & WPLQE Right-Angle Economy Planetary Series (featured model: WPLE080 1-Stage 1:5 Ratio) is Neugart's high-efficiency 90° drive solution, engineered specifically for space-critical servo applications requiring compact right-angle motor integration. By pairing a high-precision **spiral bevel input stage with planetary gear stages**, the WPLE (standard round output) and WPLQE (heavy-load square output flange) series eliminate axial motor projection into machine pathways without compromising torque or positioning accuracy. Featuring low torsional backlash (**<8 to <11 arcmin**) and dynamic transmission efficiency up to 94%, these units offer high cost-effectiveness for packaging conveyors, rotary feeders, Delta pick-and-place robots, and automated transfer axes. The WPLQE variant incorporates reinforced deep-groove ball bearings to absorb elevated radial and axial shaft loads. Available across 5 modular frame sizes (040, 060, 080, 120, 160) covering ratios from 3:1 to 100:1 with output torques up to **1,000 Nm**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter selection for Neugart WPLE & WPLQE right-angle planetary gearboxes worldwide.
