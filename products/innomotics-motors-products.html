Siemens & Innomotics Industrial Electric Motors Catalog

Brand Heritage & Sourcing Continuity Notice:
Innomotics is the independent enterprise established when Siemens carved out its low-to-medium voltage electric motor and converter divisions. An Innomotics motor is 100% identical in engineering DNA, manufacturing plants, tooling, and strict quality control standards to the classic Siemens motor. The original world-class manufacturing plants (including locations in the Czech Republic, Germany, and China) continue full operations without change.

Meanwhile, Siemens retains its high-dynamic motion control portfolio under Siemens Digital Industries, including the world-renowned Siemens servo motor series (SIMOTICS S-1FK7, S-1FT7, S-1FK2), precision servo planetary gearboxes, and large high-voltage industrial drives. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% genuine motors directly through verified OEM factory channels.
Looking for industrial gearboxes or geared motors? This catalog page is 100% dedicated to standalone electric motors. If your project requires gear units, please visit our Siemens & Innomotics Gearboxes and Geared Motors Catalog or the Siemens Flender & SIMOGEAR Gearbox Brand Page.
