Busch Seco Dry Rotary Vane Vacuum Pumps

Brand: Busch Vacuum Solutions | Category: Oil-Free Dry Rotary Vane Vacuum Pumps & Compressors (Pumping Speed: 10 m³/h to 140 m³/h)

The Busch Seco Dry Rotary Vane Vacuum Pump Series (featured model: SV 1040 C) is Busch's compact, completely oil-free vacuum solution engineered for high-frequency industrial automation, pick-and-place robotics, packaging machinery, printing paper feed systems, and medical suction units. Utilizing self-lubricating **special graphite vanes** that glide smoothly against a precision-cast iron cylinder wall, Seco pumps operate entirely without operating fluids. This dry design eliminates oil vapor contamination, oil level maintenance, and fluid disposal costs. Built with an integrated intake filter, direct-driven motor flange, and low-noise air cooling shroud, Seco pumps offer continuous ultimate pressures down to **120 hPa (mbar)** across nominal capacities from **10 m³/h to 140 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model cross-referencing, and global express supply for Busch Seco dry rotary vane vacuum pumps worldwide.
