Baldor-Reliance Single Phase Industrial Electric Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: Single Phase Capacitor Start / Run Motors (1/3 HP to 15 HP)

The Baldor-Reliance Single Phase Industrial Motor Series (featured model: L3608T 3 HP 184T 4-pole) is North America's premier single phase motor platform, engineered specifically for commercial and industrial applications where three-phase power supply is unavailable or cost-prohibitive. Utilizing **Capacitor Start / Capacitor Run (CSCR)** and **Capacitor Start / Induction Run (CSIR)** electrical topology, these motors generate massive locked-rotor torque to start heavy loads under full pressure. Built with heavy-gauge steel or cast-iron frames, high-grade copper windings, internal centrifugal switches, thermal overload protection options, and dual 115/230V or 230V AC terminal configurations, Baldor single phase motors deliver exceptional reliability across **TEFC (Totally Enclosed Fan Cooled)** and **ODP (Open Drip Proof)** enclosures. Covering output power ratings from 1/3 HP up to 15 HP across NEMA frame sizes 56 to 215T, these motors deliver 100% direct mechanical replacement for commercial air compressors, water pumps, hydraulic power units, woodworking equipment, and agricultural farm duty machinery.
