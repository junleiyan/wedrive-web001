Flender ZAPEX ZNN 280 Heavy-Duty Gear Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: ZAPEX ZI & ZW Double-Gimbal Gear Couplings (Size 83 to 1000)[cite: 20]

The Flender ZAPEX ZNN 280 is a heavy-duty double-gimbal curved-tooth gear coupling engineered for ultra-high torque transmission in compact industrial drivetrains[cite: 20]. Featuring precision-machined crowned teeth[cite: 20], the ZAPEX ZNN 280 delivers a nominal torque rating of 62,000 Nm (peak capacity up to 140,000 Nm) while accommodating angular, parallel, and axial shaft misalignments with minimal restoring forces[cite: 20]. Covering 20 frame sizes from size 83 to 1000 (1,020 Nm to 7,200,000 Nm)[cite: 20], ZAPEX couplings feature standard double-engagement designs (ZNN), extended axial float units (ZNA), single-engagement cardan drives (ZNNA), attached brake discs (ZWB/ZNNB), high-speed steel designs (ZWS), and vertical executions (ZWNV)[cite: 20]. They are heavily specified in steel rolling mills, continuous casting lines, crane hoists, cement kilns, marine propulsion, and mining winches[cite: 20].
