Flender ZAPEX ZNN 280 Heavy-Duty Gear Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: ZAPEX ZI & ZW Double-Gimbal Gear Couplings (Size 83 to 1000)

The Flender ZAPEX ZNN 280 is a heavy-duty double-gimbal curved-tooth gear coupling engineered for ultra-high torque transmission in compact industrial drivetrains. Featuring precision-machined crowned teeth, the ZAPEX ZNN 280 delivers a nominal torque rating of 62,000 Nm (peak capacity up to 140,000 Nm) while accommodating angular, parallel, and axial shaft misalignments with minimal restoring forces. Covering 20 frame sizes from size 83 to 1000 (1,020 Nm to 7,200,000 Nm), ZAPEX couplings feature standard double-engagement designs (ZNN), extended axial float units (ZNA), single-engagement cardan drives (ZNNA), attached brake discs (ZWB/ZNNB), high-speed steel designs (ZWS), and vertical executions (ZWNV). They are heavily specified in steel rolling mills, continuous casting lines, crane hoists, cement kilns, marine propulsion, and mining winches.
