SEW X3F300e Industrial Gearbox (Xe Generation Size 300)

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Category: Flagship Industrial Gear Units (X290e-X320e Range, M, MC, ML Series)

The SEW X3F300e is a 3-stage parallel helical ultra-heavy-duty industrial gearbox engineered within SEW-Eurodrive's flagship Xe Generation (Generation e) platform. Delivering a massive nominal output torque capacity of 350.0 kNm, the Size 290 to 320 Xe Series incorporates optimized gear tooth contact geometry, expanded housing surface thermal dissipation, low-friction seal assemblies, and extended bearing operational life. Designed to cover nominal torques up to 475 kNm at the peak of the X platform, the X290e, X300e, X310e, and X320e sizes provide complete 1-to-1 dimensional compatibility to directly replace legacy M Series (M2PSF90 / M3RSF90 / M3PSF100), MC Series (MC09), and ML Series flagship industrial speed reducers in primary mining conveyors, cement kilns, port container cranes, ball mills, and heavy industrial agitators.
