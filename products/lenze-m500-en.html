Lenze m500 IE3 Premium Efficiency Three-Phase AC Motors

Brand: Lenze SE | Category: Three-Phase AC Motors (m550-P IE3 Series: 0.12 kW to 55.0 kW)

The Lenze m500 Motor Series (featured model: m550-P90L4 1.5 kW IE3) is Lenze's advanced three-phase AC electric motor platform, purpose-built to comply with global IEC energy efficiency standards while providing seamless, direct mounting onto Lenze g500 series gearboxes (g500-H, g500-S, g500-B, g500-W). Designated as **m550-P** for IE3 Premium Efficiency performance, m500 motors cover output ratings from 0.12 kW up to 55.0 kW across frame sizes IEC 63 to 225M. Featuring reinforced VFD-rated winding insulation for frequency inverter duty (paired with **Lenze i550 and i510 inverters**), modular spring-applied holding brakes, and HTL/TTL incremental encoders, Lenze m500 AC motors deliver 100% direct mechanical compatibility to replace legacy **Lenze MDEMA** and **MDFQA** motor series in industrial machinery worldwide.
