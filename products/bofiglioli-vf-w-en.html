Bonfiglioli W 63 Worm Gearbox (VF & W Series)

Brand: Bonfiglioli Riduttori | Category: Worm Gearboxes & Helical-Worm Geared Motors (VF 27-250 & W 63-110)

The Bonfiglioli W 63 (featured model: W 63 U 1/19 P80 B5) is a benchmark cubic-body worm gearbox from Bonfiglioli's world-renowned W Series, engineered alongside the classic rounded VF Series. Combining high torque density, smooth operation, and modular mounting options, Bonfiglioli VF and W worm speed reducers cover nominal output torques from 13 Nm to 7,100 Nm. Manufactured in lightweight aluminum die-cast housings (VF 27-49 and W 63-86) and heavy-duty cast iron housings (VF 130-250 and W 110), these units feature case-hardened ground steel worms and high-tin bronze worm wheels. With standard IEC motor flanges, hollow output shafts, and torque arms, Bonfiglioli VF/W gearboxes provide 100% direct 1-to-1 mechanical compatibility to replace Motovario NMRV, SEW S Series, and Rossi A Series worm drives across conveyors, packaging machinery, food processing, and material handling systems.

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in providing technical sourcing, replacement selection, and global supply for the complete Bonfiglioli VF & W Worm Gearbox family. Whether your plant requires a standard foot-mounted worm gear unit (VF 30, VF 44, VF 49), a cubic universal housing (W 63, W 75, W 86, W 110), a heavy cast iron speed reducer (VF 130 to VF 250), or a high-ratio double-worm combination (W/VF, VF/VF), all units can be supplied pre-configured with IEC B5 or B14 motor flanges, single/double output plug-in shafts, torque arms, or safety reaction protection covers.
