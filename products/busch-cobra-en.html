Busch COBRA Dry Screw Vacuum Pumps

Brand: Busch Vacuum Solutions | Category: Oil-Free Dry Screw Chemical & Industrial Vacuum Pumps (Pumping Speed: 100 m³/h to 2,000 m³/h)

The Busch COBRA Dry Screw Vacuum Pump Series (featured model: NC 0300 B) represents Busch's advanced non-contacting, oil-free vacuum technology designed specifically for demanding chemical processing, pharmaceutical synthesis, plastics degassing, solar cell coating, and industrial vacuum applications. Operating via two counter-rotating **variable pitch screw rotors**, COBRA pumps achieve exceptional internal compression efficiency while discharging gases completely free from oil or operating fluid contamination. Engineered with direct water cooling, uniform thermal distribution, and specialized corrosion-resistant coatings, these units deliver extreme vapor tolerance, low ultimate pressures down to **0.01 hPa (mbar)**, and long maintenance-free service intervals. Available across multiple modular models (NC 0100 B, NC 0200 B, NC 0300 B, NC 0630 C, and Industry/Chemical variants) covering capacities up to **2,000 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model cross-referencing, and global express supply for Busch COBRA dry screw vacuum pumps worldwide.
