Flender B2SH07 Industrial Bevel-Helical Gearbox

Brand: Flender GmbH (Siemens AG) | Series: B Series Right-Angle Industrial Gear Units

The Flender B2SH07 is a benchmark 2-stage right-angle bevel-helical industrial gearbox engineered for space-saving mounting, high operational reliability, and maximum torque density[cite: 20]. Combining a high-precision spiral bevel gear input stage with helical gear reduction stages[cite: 20], the Flender B series is available in 2, 3, and 4-stage layouts (B2, B3, B4) covering sizes 03 through 26 with nominal torques up to 1,400,000 Nm[cite: 20]. Key industrial applications include bucket elevators, heavy belt conveyors, crane hoist drives, ball mill auxiliaries, shredders, and water aerators[cite: 20].
