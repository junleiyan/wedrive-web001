Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS SD 1LE5 Severe Duty Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS SD (1LE55, 1LE56 Severe Duty Range)
Global Severe Duty Heavy Motor Lineage — Siemens to Innomotics:
The SIMOTICS SD 1LE5 platform represents Siemens' top-tier severe-duty low-voltage motor line engineered for the most demanding global industrial processes. Following Siemens' strategic restructuring, the 1LE5 heavy-power product family is now manufactured and supplied under the independent brand identity Innomotics.

Engineering Supremacy for Extreme Industrial Applications:
• Maximum Mechanical Rigidity: Built in heavy cast-iron frames (IEC sizes 315, 355, 400, and 450) with optimized cooling rib geometry, premium EN-GJL-200 / EN-GJS cast housings, and regreasable heavy-duty roller/ball bearings.
• Ultra-High Energy Efficiency: Available in IE3 Premium (1LE55), IE4 Super Premium (1LE56), and cutting-edge IE5 Ultra Premium designs spanning power ratings from 200 kW up to 1,000 kW and higher.
• Process Industry Reliability: Full compliance with IEEE 841, petrochemical severe duty standards, and harsh marine environments, serving continuous-operation pipelines, heavy mine conveyors, rolling mills, cement crushers, and municipal intake pump stations.

The Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS SD 1LE5 Series is engineered to withstand extreme environmental stress, mechanical vibration, abrasive dust, and aggressive chemical atmospheres. Designed for line operation (DOL) and inverter duty (VFD) up to 690V, 1LE5 motors feature the DURIGNIT IR 2000 Class F insulation system with Class B temperature rise (80K reserve), standard SPM shock pulse bearing monitoring nipples, bidirectional internal grounding brushes, and IP55/IP65/IP66 enclosure protection. They represent the premier low-voltage drive choice where unplanned downtime is unacceptable.
