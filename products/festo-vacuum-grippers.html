Festo End-of-Arm Tooling & Sensing Guide: OVEM vs. DHPS vs. SMT-8M/SPAU

Engineering selection guide to specify vacuum ejectors, mechanical grippers, cylinder sensors, and pneumatic piping for automated workstations:

Series Family Functional Role Key Engineering Advantage Recommended Operating Condition
OVEM / VN Vacuum Compressed-air-driven Venturi vacuum generation OVEM integrates vacuum generation, supply valve, ejector pulse valve, vacuum switch, and air-saving cycle control into one compact IP65 unit. Non-porous smooth workpieces (sheet metal, glass, cartons, PCBs) requiring rapid cycle times and vacuum verification.
DHPS / HGPT / DRVS Mechanical jaw gripping & pneumatic rotary indexing DHPS features self-centering T-slot guided jaws with high gripping force-to-weight ratio; DRVS provides smooth, backlash-free rotary motion. Irregular, porous, or heavy components requiring positive mechanical clamping and 90°/180° part orientation.
SMT-8M / SPAU / SDE5 Magnetic piston detection & pneumatic pressure feedback SMT-8M drops into Festo T-slots from above and locks with a quarter-turn screw; SPAU displays live pressure in bar/psi/MPa with red/green threshold alerts. Mandatory PLC end-of-stroke verification and real-time air/vacuum pressure monitoring on automated lines.
QS Fittings & PUN-H Pneumatic air distribution & flexible drag-chain tubing QS nitrile rubber sealing rings ensure instant bubble-tight connection; PUN-H resists moisture hydrolysis and microbial growth in wet areas. All industrial pneumatic circuits, high-flex energy chains, and food/packaging washdown environments.
