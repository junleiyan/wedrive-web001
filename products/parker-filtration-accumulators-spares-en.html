Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) – Parker Filtration, Accumulators & Spares

Q1: How do I identify the exact replacement filter element for my Parker EPF, WPF, or 15P/30P filter?

Check the stamped part number on the top endcap of the existing element or send us a photo of the metal nameplate on the filter head. We cross-reference the exact element length, micron rating (02Q, 05Q, 10Q, 20Q), seal material (NBR or FKM Viton), and collapse pressure rating (20 bar or 210 bar).

Q2: Can I order just the replacement rubber bladder kit instead of a complete Parker EHV accumulator?

Yes. For repairable top-repairable and bottom-repairable Parker EHV bladder accumulators (from 1 liter up to 54 liters), we supply complete factory-sealed Replacement Bladder Kits containing the elastomer bladder, integrated gas valve stem, fluid port seal ring, anti-extrusion ring, and locknut.

Q3: What is included in a Parker SensoControl diagnostic test kit?

A typical SensoControl Serviceman Plus or Service Master Plus diagnostic kit comes in a protective hard-shell carrying case containing the handheld digital display/logger unit, 1 or 2 pressure/temperature sensors (e.g., 0–400 bar or 0–600 bar), high-pressure EMA3 microbore test hoses, M16x2 / BSPP / SAE test point couplings, power charger, and SensoWin PC analysis software.
