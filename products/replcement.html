Brand: WeDrive | Category: 1:1 Dimensional Drop-in Replacement Drives (Torque: Up to 500,000 Nm | Power: Up to 4,500 kW)

The WeDrive ODM Brand 1:1 Drop-in Replacement Series (encompassing the 4 core gearmotor families **WDR, WDFF, WDKAF, WDS** and heavy industrial gearboxes **WD-H / WD-B**, featured benchmark model: WDKAF127) is WeDrive's engineered power transmission platform designed specifically to solve long factory lead times, high replacement overheads, and supply chain disruptions for global plant operators. 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) regarding WeDrive 1:1 Replacements

Q1: How does WeDrive guarantee that the replacement drive will fit my machine without modification?

A: Before production or dispatch, WeDrive engineers perform a detailed 2D/3D CAD overlay comparison against the original installation drawing. We cross-check critical dimensions including output shaft diameter, keyway width and depth, center height, foot bolt hole spacing, flange pitch circle diameter, and torque arm hole positions. Production only proceeds once 100% dimensional match is confirmed.

Q2: Can WeDrive gearboxes replace drives with non-standard gear ratios or custom shaft sizes?

A: Yes. Subject to factory technical evaluation, our gear hobbing and CNC machining facilities can manufacture custom gear ratios and non-standard output shafts. Please note that special non-standard machining or pattern making may require a technical feasibility review and upfront tooling charges.

Q3: Are WeDrive replacement gearmotors mechanically durable under heavy industrial shock loads?

A: Absolutely. WeDrive gears are manufactured from high-grade 20CrMnTi alloy steel, carburized and quenched to HRC 58-62, and ground to ISO Class 5-6 precision. Housings are cast from high-rigidity QT450 ductile iron or HT250 grey iron, delivering high thermal capacity and mechanical strength comparable to top international standards.

Q4: What information is needed to receive an exact 1:1 replacement quote?

A: Simply send us a clear photograph of your existing drive nameplate, along with details of the driven equipment (motor power kW, output RPM, mounting position, and shaft configuration). If you have physical dimensional drawings, providing them will accelerate our CAD verification process.
