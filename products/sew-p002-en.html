SEW P002 Series Planetary Gearbox & Combination Gearmotors

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Series: P Series Industrial Planetary Gear Units (Size 002)

The SEW P002 Series is a highly compact, heavy-duty industrial planetary gear unit engineered to deliver maximum torque density (~24 kNm rated nominal torque) in heavy industrial machinery[cite: 21]. By mounting a P002 planetary stage together with SEW primary gear units—such as Helical-Bevel (PK002 / KF), Helical (PR002 / RF), Parallel Shaft (PF002 / FF), or Helical-Worm (PS002 / SF)—this modular combination achieves extreme gear reduction ratios and flexible spatial installation[cite: 21]. It is widely deployed in apron feeders, rotary thickeners, winches, shredders, and heavy material handling drives[cite: 21].

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

We specialize in providing complete industrial drive packages and replacement solutions for the entire SEW-Eurodrive P002 series planetary range[cite: 21]. Whether you require a right-angle bevel-planetary unit (PK002 / P002 KF)[cite: 21], coaxial helical-planetary unit (PR002 / P002 RF)[cite: 21], parallel shaft unit (PF002 / P002 FF)[cite: 21], or worm combination (PS002 / P002 SF)[cite: 21], all configurations can be delivered pre-assembled with modern IE3 DRN motors (DRN100L4 to DRN180L4).

When retrofitting an existing heavy application featuring an obsolete primary drive motor (DV100, DV112, DV132, DV160, or DV180)[cite: 21], upgrading to an IE3 DRN motor ensures complete dimensional interchangeability while substantially reducing operating thermal stress and energy costs.
