TR-Electronic Absolute Rotary Encoders

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Series: CEV582, CMV582, CEH582, CEV65 (Optical & Magnetic Multi-Turn Measurement)
Precision Engineering & Global Sourcing Notice:
As a leading TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic, factory-direct TR-Electronic rotary encoders. Renowned for their ruggedness in heavy industries, TR absolute encoders deliver distinct, immediately available position values upon power-up, eliminating the need for reference runs (homing) after power failures.

Engineering Excellence & Fieldbus Integration:
• Generation 582 Platform: The highly modular 58mm series (CEV582, CMV582) supports virtually all modern Industrial Ethernet and Fieldbus protocols, including PROFINET, EtherCAT, PROFIBUS, SSI, CANopen, and DRIVE-CLiQ.
• Optical & Magnetic Scanning: High-resolution optical scanning (CEV series) for peak precision, or robust magnetic scanning (CMV series) offering superior resistance to condensation, dirt, and extreme thermal shocks.
• Heavy-Duty Resiliency: Available with solid shafts, blind hollow shafts, and through hollow shafts (CEH series) up to IP67/IP69K protection ratings, specifically engineered for steel mills, wind power pitch control, packaging automation, and automated guided vehicles (AGV).

The TR-Electronic Absolute Encoder portfolio guarantees maximum operational reliability under the most demanding environmental conditions. From the compact C_V36 for tight installation spaces to the indestructible heavy-duty ATEX explosion-proof units, TR encoders ensure seamless machine control. The multi-turn gearing mechanisms operate entirely without batteries, retaining rotational position mechanically over the entire lifespan of the sensor. Whether you require a direct replacement for the legacy CEV65M series or are designing a new robotic cell with PROFINET IRT, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the exact configuration required.

TR-Electronic Legacy CEV65 to Generation 582 Upgrade Guide

For decades, the 65mm TR encoder (e.g., CEV65M) was the industry standard. This guide explains how to seamlessly retrofit to the modern 58mm platform:

Legacy TR Series Obsolescence / Upgrade Reason Modern TR-Electronic Replacement Mechanical & Electrical Retrofit Notes
CEV65M / CMV65M Transition from classic 65mm to the global 58mm industrial standard size. CEV582M / CMV582M Adapter Flanges Available Using TR-supplied adapter flanges, the new 58mm encoder drops perfectly into existing 65mm mounting holes while retaining exact shaft dimensions (e.g., 10mm or 12mm).
Legacy PROFIBUS / INTERBUS Migration from older serial fieldbuses to modern Industrial Ethernet. PROFINET / EtherCAT Interfaces High Speed Data The Gen 582 platform supports ultra-fast PROFINET IRT and EtherCAT, allowing older machines to integrate into modern PLC networks (like Siemens S7-1500 or Beckhoff).
Fixed Resolution SSI Need for flexible, programmable parameters in the field. Programmable SSI (Gen 582) Flexible Logic Modern TR SSI encoders allow users to program the resolution, counting direction, and preset values, simplifying spare parts inventory.
