SEW FA37DRN80M4 Parallel Shaft Helical Gearmotor

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Series: FA-Series (Parallel Shaft Hollow)

The SEW FA37DRN80M4 is a compact, highly efficient industrial parallel shaft helical gearmotor featuring a hollow shaft design[cite: 19].
