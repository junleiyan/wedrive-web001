KSB Multitec Horizontal High-Pressure Multistage Pumps

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: Horizontal Ring Section High-Pressure Centrifugal Pumps (Flow: Up to 850 m³/h | Head: Up to 1,000 m / 100 bar)

The KSB Multitec Horizontal Multistage High-Pressure Pump Series (encompassing **Multitec A, B, C, and D** modular orientation variants, featured model: Multitec A 050/08) is KSB's heavy-duty fluid handling platform engineered for extreme high-pressure industrial applications including power plant boiler feed water, high-head municipal water transport, reverse osmosis seawater desalination, geothermal heating, mine dewatering, and snow-making systems. Designed with a robust modular **ring section casing** tied together by high-tensile tie bolts, Multitec pumps allow flexible 90-degree orientation choices for suction and discharge nozzles (left, right, top) to adapt seamlessly to existing plant piping. Constructed with precision-cast closed impellers and diffusers available in grey cast iron (JL1040), bronze (CC480K), cast steel (GP240GH), or Duplex stainless steel (**Noridur 1.4593**), Multitec pumps feature uncooled, maintenance-friendly mechanical seals and dynamic balance discs/drum systems to compensate for heavy axial thrust loads. Driven by standardized IEC flange/foot motors (up to **1,000 kW**, compatible with high-efficiency IE3/IE4 motors, SuPremE® IE5 magnetless motors, and PumpDrive VFDs), Multitec pumps achieve discharge pressures up to **100 bar (1,000 meters total dynamic head)** and capacities up to **850 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, stage-by-stage hydraulic calculations, mechanical seal matching, and global express supply for KSB Multitec high-pressure multistage pumps worldwide.
