ABB HXR High Voltage Cast-Iron Rib-Cooled Induction Motors

Brand: ABB | Category: Cast-Iron High Voltage Motors (HXR 355 to HXR 560 Range: 100 kW to 3150 kW)

The ABB HXR High Voltage Motor Series (featured model: HXR 450L4 630 kW 6.6 kV) is ABB's classic heavy-duty cast-iron rib-cooled induction motor platform, recognized globally for its rugged mechanical construction, thermal stability, and long service life in severe industrial environments. Built with a rigid cast-iron frame and **IC411 (totally enclosed fan-cooled, TEFC)** rib cooling design, HXR motors provide complete IP55 protection against dust, water, and corrosive ambient air. Covering power output ratings from 100 kW to 3150 kW across high voltage grid supplies from 2.3 kV up to 11 kV (HXR 355, HXR 400, HXR 450, HXR 500, and HXR 560 frame sizes), HXR motors feature **Vacuum Pressure Impregnated (VPI)** Class F/H insulation rated for line-direct starting or VFD speed control with **ABB ACS800, ACS880, and MEGADRIVE medium voltage inverters**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides 100% original ABB HXR motors, genuine spare parts (terminal boxes, bearings, cooling fans), and direct 1-to-1 mechanical replacement support mapping HXR models to current **ABB AMI and NMI** high-voltage series.
