Explore our comprehensive product catalog covering Sumitomo Cyclo® 6000 Speed Reducers, Paramax® 9000 Heavy Industrial Gearboxes, Bevel Buddybox® BBB4, Hyponic Right-Angle Motors, HSM Shaft Mount Reducers, and Hansen P4 / M4KB Heavy Industrial Reducers[cite: 12]. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% genuine Sumitomo & Hansen drive equipment with worldwide express delivery[cite: 12].

Heavy Power Transmission Excellence — Sumitomo & Hansen:
Sumitomo Heavy Industries revolutionized speed reduction with its patented Cyclo® speed reducer operating on rolling contact rather than sliding gear tooth shear[cite: 12]. Following the integration of Hansen Industrial Transmissions, the group delivers an unbeatable portfolio from compact high-ratio cycloidal reducers to multi-megawatt heavy industrial gearboxes used in mining, water treatment, and materials handling[cite: 12].

Engineering Advantage & Technical Ecosystem:
• Cyclo® 6000 Speed Reducers: Over 500% shock load capacity with smooth, rolling cycloidal disc action; single reduction ratios up to 87:1 in a compact housing[cite: 12].
