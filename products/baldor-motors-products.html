Baldor-Reliance NEMA & IEC Industrial Electric Motors

North American Industrial Standard Leader — Baldor-Reliance (Member of the ABB Group):
Baldor-Reliance® is the benchmark brand for North American NEMA frame industrial electric motors, renowned for rugged construction, NEMA Premium® energy efficiency, and reliable continuous-duty performance.

Engineering Advantage & Cross-Standard Compatibility:
• NEMA General & Severe Duty Motors: Super-E® NEMA Premium efficiency motors, IEEE 841 severe duty motors, and ECP/XCP cast-iron process motors ranging from 1/4 HP to 1500 HP.
• Washdown & Hazardous Duty Motors: SSE All-Stainless Steel Washdown motors (IP69K) and Class I/II explosion-proof motors engineered for food processing, chemical, and oil & gas plants.
• IEC Metric Motors: Baldor-Reliance IEC metric frame motors (IEC 63 to 450) in IE2, IE3, and IE4 efficiency classes for seamless export machinery integration.
