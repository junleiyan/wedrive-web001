TR-Electronic Absolute Encoders: C_V36 Compact Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: CEV36M, CMV36M, CEH36M | Global TR Encode Supplier
Miniaturization Meets Industrial Precision:
As a trusted TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic TR-Electronic miniature rotary encoders. The TR C_V36 compact series packs the high resolution and robust multi-turn mechanical gearing of the standard 58mm series into an ultra-compact 36mm housing, designed specifically for tight installation spaces.

Engineering Excellence for Confined Spaces:
• Compact Form Factor: With an outer diameter of just 36mm, these miniature absolute encoders are the ideal solution for automated guided vehicles (AGVs), medical equipment, and highly compact robotic joints.
• Optical & Magnetic Scanning: Available in high-precision optical variants (CEV36) for medical and laboratory automation, or robust magnetic versions (CMV36) highly resistant to shock and vibration for mobile robotics.
• Standard Interface Support: Despite their small size, the C_V36 series natively supports essential fieldbuses including SSI, CANopen, Analog (4-20mA/0-10V), DRIVE-CLiQ, and advanced Industrial Ethernet (PROFINET / EtherCAT) via specialized compact hoods.

The TR Encoders C_V36 Compact Series guarantees maximum operational reliability where space is at an absolute premium. Available in solid shaft and blind hollow shaft configurations, these miniature encoders deliver distinct, immediately available position values upon power-up without the need for reference runs. Whether you are outfitting a fleet of warehouse AGVs or designing a surgical robotic arm, our technical team ensures you receive the exact compact measurement solution required.
