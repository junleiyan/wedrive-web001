KSB ISORIA, BOAX-B & BOA Industrial Valves & Actuators

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: Industrial Butterfly Valves, Bellows Globe Valves & Actuator Systems (Size: DN 20 to DN 1200 | Pressure: PN 6 to PN 40)

The KSB Industrial Valve Series (encompassing centric butterfly valves **ISORIA 10/16** & **BOAX-B**, double-eccentric valves **MAMMOUTH**, bellows-sealed globe valves **BOA-H**, and check valves **BOA-R**, featured model: ISORIA 10 DN 80) is KSB's comprehensive fluid control platform engineered for absolute bubble-tight shut-off, low flow resistance, and maintenance-free operational safety in industrial water supply, chemical processing, power station cooling circuits, district heating, and HVAC automation. Constructed with durable ductile iron (EN-JS1030), cast steel (1.0619), or stainless steel (1.4408) bodies, KSB butterfly valves feature replaceable elastomer liners (**EPDM, NBR, Viton/FKM**) and stainless steel or Duplex discs that prevent fluid-to-body contact. For steam and thermal oil lines, the **BOA-H globe valve** incorporates a double-wall stainless steel bellows seal that eliminates stem packing leaks entirely. Designed across nominal sizes from **DN 20 to DN 1200** with pressure ratings up to **PN 40 (40 bar)**, KSB valves integrate seamlessly with manual levers, worm gearboxes, or automated pneumatic (**ACTAIR / AMTROQ**) and electric (**ACTELEC**) actuators. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, valve sizing, liner compatibility verification, actuator automation matching, and global express supply for KSB industrial valves worldwide.
