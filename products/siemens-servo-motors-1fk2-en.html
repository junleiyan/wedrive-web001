Siemens SIMOTICS S-1FK2 High-Dynamic Synchronous Servo Motors

Brand: Siemens Motion Control | Series: SIMOTICS S-1FK2 (High Dynamic 1FK21 & Compact 1FK22)
Next-Generation Motion Architecture — Siemens Digital Industries:
The SIMOTICS S-1FK2 series represents Siemens' newest, high-performance synchronous servo motor platform designed in conjunction with the SINAMICS S210 single-axis servo drive system. While standard low-voltage induction motors transitioned to Innomotics, Siemens strictly retains, engineers, and advances this cutting-edge motion control technology.

Engineering Advantages & Single-Cable Innovation:
• One Cable Connection (OCC) Technology: Power conductor lines, brake controls, and digital encoder signals are consolidated into an ultra-slim, single SPEED-CONNECT M12/M17 cable, drastically reducing installation space, weight, and cabinet cabling complexity.
• Dual Dynamic Variants: 1FK21 (High Dynamic) delivers minimal rotor inertia for maximum acceleration and rapid cycle rates; 1FK22 (Compact) provides high torque density and higher rotor inertia for optimal matching with heavier mechanical loads.
• 22-bit / 24-bit Digital Absolute Encoders: Fast, noise-immune digital telemetry without external battery backup, ensuring zero-homing cycles upon restart and extreme positioning accuracy.

The Siemens SIMOTICS S-1FK2 Servo Motor System provides extraordinary dynamic acceleration, thermal stability, and smooth rotation across industrial automation axes. Available in shaft heights (SH) 20, 30, 40, 48, 52, 63, 80, and 100, offering static stall torques from 0.05 Nm to 50 Nm and rated speeds up to 8,000 rpm, 1FK2 motors feature natural cooling, forced ventilation, or protective shaft oil seals. Standard configurations include optional low-backlash 24V holding brakes, planetary gearbox couplings, and IP64/IP65/IP67 ratings. They are heavily utilized in pick-and-place delta robots, automated packaging, blister cartoning, electronics assembly, printing, and multi-axis CNC machines.

1FK2 High Dynamic vs. 1FK2 Compact Selection Matrix

Engineering comparison between the High Dynamic (1FK21) and Compact (1FK22) motor designs:

Design Variant Rotor Inertia Characteristic Peak Acceleration Potential Load Inertia Ratio Matching Optimized Machine Axes
SIMOTICS S-1FK21 (High Dynamic) Extremely low rotor moment of inertia Ultra-high angular acceleration ($a_{max}$) Ideal for low external load inertia ratios ($J_L / J_M \le 3$) Delta packaging robots, high-speed pick-and-place, electronic flying shears, feeder axes.
SIMOTICS S-1FK22 (Compact) Medium-to-high rotor moment of inertia High continuous and stall torque density Excellent control stability with higher load inertia ($J_L / J_M \le 10$) Rotary tables, vertical positioning axes, linear ball screw actuators, conveyor belt drives.
