Samson Self-Operated Regulators (Type 41-23, Type 44-1 C, 1 U Series)

Brand: SAMSON AG | Category: Direct-Acting Self-Operated Pressure & Temperature Regulators (Sizes: DN 15 to DN 100 | Ratings: PN 16 to PN 40 | Temp: Up to +350°C)[cite: 2]

The Samson Self-Operated Regulators Series (featuring the industry-standard Type 41-23 Pressure Reducing Valve, Type 41-73 Excess Pressure Valve, and Type 44-1 C / 1 U Temperature Regulators) represents SAMSON AG's world-leading direct-acting autonomous control technology[cite: 2]. Engineered specifically for steam distribution headers, district heating substations, boiler feed skids, inert gas blanketing, and industrial cooling circuits, these regulators operate entirely on the process medium's own energy, requiring zero auxiliary electric power or compressed air supplies[cite: 2]. Featuring pressure-balanced valve plugs via stainless steel bellows or diaphragms, Samson regulators maintain high control accuracy even under severe upstream pressure fluctuations and fluctuating demand profiles[cite: 2]. The modular construction allows rapid setpoint adjustment via calibrated spring ranges, while heavy-duty cast iron, ductile iron, cast steel, and stainless steel bodies deliver exceptional service life up to PN 40 / Class 300 and temperatures up to +350°C[cite: 2]. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sizing (Cv/Kvs flow capacity, setpoint spring matching, condensation chamber requirements), direct serial replacement verification, and fast global express delivery for the complete Samson self-acting regulator portfolio[cite: 2].

Expert Sizing & Engineering Integration Notes for Samson Regulators

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in comprehensive sizing, serial configuration lookup, flow capacity (Kvs) calculations, and immediate international shipment for the complete Samson AG Self-Operated Regulator portfolio[cite: 2]. Unlike standard actuated control valves, self-acting regulators must be sized with precision based on exact steam mass flow rates, inlet/outlet pressures, operating specific gravity, and allowable velocities to avoid noise and premature seat wear.

When replacing existing valves or designing new pressure reduction stations, our technical engineering team verifies:
• Kvs Coefficient vs. Pipe Diameter: Ensuring nominal size matches calculated flow demand rather than matching existing pipe size, preventing oversized hunting and seat chatter[cite: 2].
• Condensation Chambers (Seal Pots): Mandatory for steam temperatures above +150°C and liquids above +150°C to protect the actuator rolling diaphragm from thermal degradation[cite: 2].
• Actuator Diaphragm Sizing: Matching the diaphragm effective surface area (from 40 cm² up to 640 cm²) to the required setpoint range for maximum sensitivity (e.g., larger diaphragms for ultra-low mbar pressure ranges)[cite: 2].

Simply provide your nameplate photo, valve serial number, or thermodynamic process parameters (P1, P2, Temperature, Medium, Max Flow), and our technical team will deliver a 100% compliant specification proposal and competitive quotation.
