Bonfiglioli HDP 100 Heavy Industrial Gearbox (HDP & HDO Series)

Brand: Bonfiglioli Riduttori | Category: Heavy Industrial Parallel & Bevel-Helical Speed Reducers (HDP & HDO 60 to 180)

The Bonfiglioli HDP 100 (featured model: HDP 100 3 Parallel Shaft) represents the pinnacle of heavy-duty power transmission engineering from Bonfiglioli's high-torque HDP Parallel Shaft and HDO Bevel-Helical series. Engineered for continuous severe industrial operation, heavy shock loads, and high thermal capacity, Bonfiglioli HDP and HDO gear units cover nominal output torques from 5,000 Nm up to 210,000 Nm across 13 standard frame sizes (60 to 180). Manufactured in rigid spheroidal nodular cast iron housings with ground gear teeth geometry, these units offer 2, 3, and 4-stage reduction trains with versatile mounting options (foot, flange, and shaft mounting with torque arm). Featuring solid keyed output shafts, splined shafts, or hollow shafts with shrink disc, Bonfiglioli HDP/HDO gearboxes provide reliable heavy-duty service across mining conveyors, cement mills, bucket elevators, extruders, and harbour crane hoists.
