Kollmorgen AKM Brushless AC Servo Motors

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: High-Performance Brushless Servo Motors (AKM1 to AKM8 Range: 0.18 Nm to 180 Nm)

The Kollmorgen AKM Servo Motor Series (featured model: AKM42H-ANCNC-00 3.4 Nm 6000 RPM) is Kollmorgen's industry-standard flagship brushless AC servo motor platform, recognized worldwide for its exceptional power density, smooth speed control, and extensive motion customization possibilities. Designed around high-coercivity Neodymium-Iron-Boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets and low-cogging stator geometry, AKM motors deliver rapid dynamic acceleration and precise position accuracy. Covering continuous stall torque ratings from 0.18 Nm up to 180 Nm across 8 frame sizes (40mm to 260mm flange dimensions), AKM motors offer seamless multi-voltage winding options (75V DC to 480V AC mains), IP65 shaft/housing protection, holding brakes, and diverse feedback interfaces including **Smart Feedback Device (SFD/SFD3)**, **HIPERFACE DSL single-cable digital feedback**, **EnDat absolute encoders**, and 2-pole resolvers. Engineered for plug-and-play alignment with **Kollmorgen AKD and AKD2G digital servo drives**, AKM motors serve as the direct replacement for legacy **Goldline B/M Series** and **Pacific Scientific PMA/PMB** servo motors in robotics, packaging machines, semiconductor tools, printing presses, and medical devices.
