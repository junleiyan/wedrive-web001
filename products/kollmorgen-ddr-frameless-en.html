Kollmorgen DDR & KBM Frameless Direct Drive Rotary Motors

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: Unhoused Rotor/Stator Direct Drive Motors (KBM10 to KBM163 Range: 0.15 Nm to 3500 Nm)

The Kollmorgen DDR & KBM Frameless Direct Drive Motor Series (featured model: KBM-43H01-A00 12.5 Nm 1200 RPM) represents Kollmorgen's ultimate direct-drive technology, providing unhoused rotor and stator component kits engineered to embed directly into OEM machine structures. By utilizing the machine's own internal bearings and frame, KBM frameless motors completely **eliminate mechanical couplings, gearboxes, belts, and external motor housings**, achieving maximum compact spatial integration, **zero mechanical backlash, extreme torsional stiffness, and whisper-quiet operation**. Designed with high-coercivity Neodymium-Iron-Boron magnets and large hollow-bore rotors for routing cables, laser beams, or fluid lines, KBM motors cover continuous stall torque output from 0.15 Nm up to 3,500 Nm across 14 frame sizes (stator outer diameters from 60mm to 603mm). Optimized for seamless autotuning with **Kollmorgen AKD and AKD2G digital servo drive amplifiers**, KBM frameless direct drive motors deliver uncompromised precision for robotics joints, medical gantry axes, optics azimuth mounts, semiconductor wafer inspection tables, and defense gimbal drives.
