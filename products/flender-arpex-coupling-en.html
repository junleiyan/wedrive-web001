Flender ARPEX ARN-6 NEN 165-6 Flexible Disc Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: ARPEX Torsionally Rigid All-Steel Disc Couplings (ARN-6 / ARN-8 / ARN-10 / ARS-6)

The Flender ARPEX ARN-6 NEN 165-6 is a benchmark high-performance all-steel flexible disc coupling engineered for high-precision, torsionally rigid, and 100% backlash-free power transmission. Utilizing high-strength stainless spring steel disc packs (plate packs), the ARPEX ARN-6 compensates for angular, radial, and axial shaft misalignments entirely through elastic flexing without any sliding parts. Featuring zero maintenance, wear-free operation, infinite service life under alignment limits, and extreme temperature resistance (-270°C to +280°C), ARPEX couplings are available across major product lines—including the latest high-performance ARN-6, ARN-8, and ARN-10 series alongside classic ARS-6, ARC-8, ARP-6, ARW-4/6, and ART-6 series—with torque capacities from 92 Nm to 2,000,000 Nm. They are widely applied in paper machines, centrifugal pumps, compressors, turbomachinery, test benches, wind turbines, generator sets, and marine propulsion.

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

We specialize in providing comprehensive technical sourcing and custom machining solutions for the complete Flender ARPEX all-steel disc coupling family. Whether your drive train requires a standard intermediate spacer coupling (ARN-6 / ARS-6 Type NEN), a long-spacer pump drive (Type NHN), keyless clamping hubs (Type BEN / BHN), close-coupled space-saving designs (Type CEC), or API 610/671 turbomachinery couplings (ARP-6 Type NAN / MCE), all hubs can be precision bore-machined with custom keyways, spline profiles, or taper bores.

When retrofitting an existing drive featuring worn gear couplings or older ARS-6 / ARC-8 units, upgrading to current ARPEX ARN series couplings ensures complete 1-to-1 dimensional interchangeability while eliminating the need for periodic lubrication and routine maintenance. Simply supply us with your shaft diameters, distance between shaft ends (DBSE), operating speed, and motor power, and our engineering team will provide a 100% compliant specification and quote.
