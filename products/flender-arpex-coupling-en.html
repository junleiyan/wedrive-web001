Flender ARPEX ARN-6 NEN 165-6 Flexible Disc Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: ARPEX Torsionally Rigid All-Steel Disc Couplings (ARN-6 / ARN-8 / ARN-10 / ARS-6)

The Flender ARPEX ARN-6 NEN 165-6 is a benchmark high-performance all-steel flexible disc coupling engineered for high-precision, torsionally rigid, and 100% backlash-free power transmission[cite: 25]. Utilizing high-strength stainless spring steel disc packs (plate packs)[cite: 25], the ARPEX ARN-6 compensates for angular, radial, and axial shaft misalignments entirely through elastic flexing without any sliding parts[cite: 25]. Featuring zero maintenance, wear-free operation, infinite service life under alignment limits, and extreme temperature resistance (-270°C to +280°C)[cite: 25], ARPEX couplings are available across major product lines—including the latest high-performance ARN-6, ARN-8, and ARN-10 series alongside classic ARS-6, ARC-8, ARP-6, ARW-4/6, and ART-6 series—with torque capacities from 92 Nm to 2,000,000 Nm[cite: 25]. They are widely applied in paper machines, centrifugal pumps, compressors, turbomachinery, test benches, wind turbines, generator sets, and marine propulsion[cite: 25].
