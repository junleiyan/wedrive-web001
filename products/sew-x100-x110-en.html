SEW X3F180e Industrial Gearbox (Xe Generation)

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Category: Industrial Gear Units (X100-X320 & X100e-X320e, M, MC Series)

The SEW X3F180e is a 3-stage helical heavy-duty industrial gearbox from SEW-Eurodrive's enhanced Xe Generation (Generation e)[cite: 21]. Delivering a nominal output torque rating of 58.0 kNm[cite: 21], the Xe Series features optimized gear teeth geometry, enhanced thermal dissipation capacity, reduced seal friction, and extended bearing operational life[cite: 21]. Covering all 23 frame sizes from X100e to X320e (6.8 to 475 kNm)[cite: 21], the X and Xe series provide complete dimensional backwards compatibility to directly replace legacy MC Series, M Series, and M3 Series industrial drives in heavy mining conveyors, cement kilns, port cranes, and industrial agitators[cite: 21].
