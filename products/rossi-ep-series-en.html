Rossi EP Series Heavy-Duty Planetary Gearmotors

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Coaxial & Bevel-Helical Planetary Gear Units (Torque: Up to 400,000 Nm | Ratio: 1.25 to 3,150)

The Rossi EP Series Heavy-Duty Planetary Gearmotor Platform (featured benchmark model: EP 012 / EP 080) represents the pinnacle of Italian industrial gear engineering, designed specifically for extreme applications demanding ultra-high torque density, superior thermal capacity, and continuous 24/7 reliability in mining conveyors, steel rolling mills, port cranes, large agitators, and sugar processing drives[cite: 5]. Built inside high-rigidity **spheroidal graphite cast iron (ductile iron)** housings, Rossi EP Series reducers feature case-carburized and ground alloy steel planet gears (HRC 58-62) housed in a compact modular casing[cite: 5]. 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) regarding Rossi EP Series

Q1: What mounting options are available for Rossi EP Series planetary units?

A: Rossi EP Series units support flexible mounting orientations including horizontal foot mounting, vertical flange mounting, and shaft-mounted executions with hollow output shafts or shrink discs[cite: 5].

Q2: How does WeDrive ensure that WD-EP replacement gearboxes match original Rossi units?

A: WeDrive engineers execute a 1:1 CAD overlay check covering output shaft diameter, keyway dimensions, center height, flange pitch circle, and torque arm bracket positions. Production proceeds only after 100% dimensional match is verified.

Q3: What lubrication is recommended for Rossi EP Series planetary gearboxes?

A: Synthetic gear oil is recommended to maximize mechanical efficiency (up to 98% per stage)[cite: 5] and extend oil drain intervals. Forced lubrication systems or external oil coolers are supplied for high-thermal severe-duty applications[cite: 5].

Q4: Can Rossi EP Series gearmotors be coupled with standard IEC or NEMA electric motors?

A: Yes. EP Series planetary units feature modular input adapters compatible with standard IEC flanged motors, NEMA C-face motors, hydraulic motors, or solid input power shafts[cite: 5].
