Busch Vacuum Technology Engineering Advantages & Industrial Ecosystem

Busch Vacuum Solutions stands apart from conventional air displacement systems through state-of-the-art rotor profiles and contamination-resistant design:

1. High Volumetric Pumping Efficiency Continuous Heavy Duty

Busch R5 and COBRA series vacuum pumps maintain consistent high pumping speeds even at low ultimate pressures. Featuring heavy-duty cast-iron pumping chambers, integrated oil-mist separators, and corrosion-resistant coatings, Busch pumps operate reliably in harsh packaging, chemical, and plastics degassing environments.

2. Contactless Dry Claw & Screw Tech Maintenance-Free Operation

Busch Mink dry claw and COBRA dry screw technology feature non-contacting internal rotors that operate completely free of operating fluids. This eliminates oil changes, prevents process gas contamination, and reduces power consumption by up to 60% compared to traditional liquid ring or oil-sealed vacuum pumps.
