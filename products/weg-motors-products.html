WEG Industrial Low Voltage, High Voltage & Steel Industry Dedicated Motors

Global Engineering Excellence — WEG Motor Technology:
WEG is a global powerhouse in electric motor manufacturing, renowned for delivering extreme industrial durability, ultra-high energy efficiency, and specialized solutions tailored for the most demanding heavy industries.

Engineering Advantage & Industry Expertise:
• WEG Low Voltage Motors: The legendary W22 cast iron series and W21 aluminum series, covering 0.12 kW up to 500 kW in IE3, IE4, and IE5 ultra-premium efficiency classes.
• WEG High Voltage Motors: Compact W50 and modular M-Line induction motors engineered for severe duty power generation, water plants, and heavy mining up to 13.8 kV.
• WEG Steel and Iron Industry Dedicated Motor: Specifically designed Roller Table motors and severe-duty crane brake motors built to withstand extreme heat, heavy vibration, and continuous reversing operations in steel mills.
• WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original WEG equipment with fast global express delivery and specialized retrofit engineering.
