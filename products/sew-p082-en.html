SEW P082 Series Planetary Gearbox & Combination Gearmotors

Brand: SEW-Eurodrive | Series: P Series Industrial Planetary Gear Units (Size 082)

The SEW P082 Series is a high-torque, heavy-duty industrial planetary gear unit engineered for massive torque power transmission (~260 kNm nominal torque rating) with exceptional structural rigidity and overload protection[cite: 20]. By coupling the P082 planetary output stage with SEW's modular primary gear units—including the K-Series (Helical-Bevel), R-Series (Helical), F-Series (Parallel Shaft), and S-Series (Helical-Worm)—this combination drive delivers extreme overall reduction ratios, right-angle or coaxial layout options, and outstanding operating reliability for heavy apron feeders, bucket wheel reclaimers, industrial mixers, heavy shredders, winches, and mining conveyor drives[cite: 20].

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

We specialize in providing complete industrial drive packages and replacement solutions for the entire SEW-Eurodrive P082 series planetary range[cite: 20]. Whether you require a right-angle bevel-planetary unit (PK082 / P082 KF)[cite: 20], coaxial helical-planetary unit (PR082 / P082 RF)[cite: 20], parallel shaft unit (PF082 / P082 FF)[cite: 20], or worm combination (PS082 / P082 SF)[cite: 20], all configurations can be delivered pre-assembled with modern IE3 DRN series motors (DRN225M4 to DRN315L4).
