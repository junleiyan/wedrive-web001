ABB M3BP Process Performance Low Voltage Cast-Iron Motors

Brand: ABB | Category: Process Performance Heavy-Duty Cast-Iron Motors (M3BP 71 to 450 Range: 0.25 kW to 1000 kW)

The ABB M3BP Motor Series (featured model: M3BP 160MLB4 11 kW IE3) is ABB's flagship Process Performance cast-iron low voltage induction motor platform, engineered for severe continuous-duty industrial environments, chemical processing, pulp and paper lines, mining, and heavy process manufacturing. Built with a high-rigidity cast-iron frame, SPM vibration measurement nipples, regreasable bearings, IP55/IP66 enclosure protection, and Class F insulation (Class B temperature rise margin), M3BP motors fully satisfy **IEC 60034-30-1 IE3 Premium, IE4 Super Premium, and IE5 SynRM (Synchronous Reluctance)** efficiency classes. Covering power ratings from 0.25 kW up to 1000 kW across 2-pole, 4-pole, 6-pole, and 8-pole configurations (IEC frame sizes 71 to 450), M3BP motors feature reinforced VFD-rated winding insulation for frequency inverter operation with **ABB ACS580 and ACS880 drives**. Engineered for ultimate process reliability and long service life, ABB M3BP motors serve as the direct 1-to-1 mechanical upgrade for legacy **ABB M2QA** cast-iron motors in critical industrial pumps, fans, refiners, crushers, and heavy conveyors.
