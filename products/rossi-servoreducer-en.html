Rossi High Precision Low Backlash Servoreducers

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: High Precision Servo Gear Units (Size 32 to 125 Range: 30 Nm to 3,000 Nm)

The Rossi Low Backlash Servoreducer Series (featured model: MR A 63 UO3A Servo Adaptor 1:30 Ratio) is Rossi's high-precision motion control platform, engineered specifically for high-dynamic servo indexing, robotic positioning, automated packaging gantry axes, and CNC auxiliary drives. Combining Rossi's signature **monolithic (single-piece) cast-iron housing** with reduced torsional backlash gear sets, these servoreducers achieve high structural rigidity and angular positioning accuracy **(<4 to <12 arcmin)** under rapid start-stop cycles. Designed for universal integration, Rossi servoreducers feature precision **servo flange adaptors and keyless clamp couplings** compatible with all standard brushless AC servo motor shaft dimensions (Kollmorgen, Siemens, SEW, Yaskawa, etc.). Available in right-angle worm (A Series Servo), coaxial helical (E Series Servo), bevel-helical (G Series Servo), and planetary configurations. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, servo motor coupling calculations, and 100% direct mechanical replacement support across global automation systems.
