3. Parker Calzoni MR & MRE High-Torque Low-Speed Radial Piston Motors

The legendary Parker Calzoni MR and MRE Series radial piston hydraulic motors utilize a unique patented design in which pressurized fluid acts directly on telescopic cylinders between the spherical piston heads and the eccentric crankshaft—eliminating connecting rods, piston rings, and sliding friction pads. This architecture delivers extraordinary starting torque (~95% of theoretical torque), smooth low-speed rotation down to 1 RPM without cogging, and high resistance to thermal shock.

Series & Model Range MR Series (Standard) & MRE Series (Enhanced High-Power): MR33, MR57, MR93, MR110, MR190, MR300, MR450, MR700, MR1100, MR1800, MR2800, MR4500, MR7000 up to MRE23000
Displacement Range 32 cc/rev up to 23,000+ cc/rev (Continuous Torque Output up to 100,000+ Nm)
Operating Pressure Rating MR: up to 210–250 bar continuous (300 bar peak) | MRE: up to 250–300 bar continuous (420 bar peak)
Mechanical & Volumetric Efficiency Starting Torque Efficiency up to 95%; Overall Efficiency > 92% across wide speed/load band
Speed Capability Smooth, pulse-free rotation from < 1 RPM up to 600–1,000 RPM (model dependent)
Shaft & Brake Options DIN 5480 / SAE Involute Splined Shafts, Keyed Cylindrical Shafts, Female Splined Shafts, Integrated Multi-Disc Fail-Safe Parking Brakes & Dual-Displacement Valves
Bi-Directional & Freewheeling Full Reversible Bi-Directional Operation; High Radial & Axial Shaft Load Capacity
Typical Heavy Applications Marine Deck Winches & Anchor Windlasses, Plastic Injection Screw Drives, Tunnel Boring Machines (TBMs), Shredders, Offshore Cranes

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) – Parker Cylinders & Hydraulic Motors

Q1: How can I order an exact replacement for an existing Parker 2H, HMI, or MMB hydraulic cylinder?

Every genuine Parker cylinder carries a stamped metal nameplate with a unique Serial Number and Model Code. Simply send us a photo of that nameplate—we look up the original factory build sheet to match the exact bore, stroke, rod end thread, cushion settings, port size, and seal material.

Q2: Can Parker F11 and F12 bent-axis units be used as both hydraulic motors and pumps?

Yes. Most F11 and F12 fixed-displacement bent-axis models (such as F12-060-MF-IV-D-000 and F12-080-MF-IV-D-000) are bi-directional motors capable of operating in open or closed circuits, and dedicated pump versions (F11-P / F12-P) are also available for high-pressure open-loop pumping up to 420 bar.

Q3: Do you supply genuine OEM seal kits, rod glands, and spare parts for Parker cylinders and Calzoni/F12 motors?

Yes. We supply complete factory-sealed Parker cylinder rod gland cartridges, piston/rod seal kits (Buna-N, Viton FKM, EPR), Calzoni telescopic cylinder spare kits, and F11/F12 shaft seals and bearing kits with fast international air courier delivery.
