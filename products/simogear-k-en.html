Siemens & Innomotics SIMOGEAR Bevel-Helical Geared Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOGEAR K & B Series (B19-B49 / K39-K189 Range)
Brand Ownership & Lineage Notice — Siemens to Innomotics:
SIMOGEAR is the modern, highly efficient right-angle geared motor platform developed by Siemens as the direct successor to the legacy Flender MOTOX K and B series. Following Siemens' corporate restructuring of its electric motor and geared motor division, the entire SIMOGEAR portfolio has been integrated under the Innomotics brand identity.

Engineering Advantage & MOTOX Retrofit Compatibility:
• SIMOGEAR K series features 3-stage wear-free bevel gear teeth achieving up to 96% high transmission efficiency across all gear ratios.
• Integrated with Siemens/Innomotics LE/LA motors (such as 1LE0003 and 1LE1501 IE3 motors) across frame sizes IEC 63 to IEC 225M.
• 100% Direct Drop-in Retrofit: SIMOGEAR K sizes (K39, K49, K69, K89, K109, K129) feature standardized hollow/solid output shafts and torque arm dimensions designed to directly replace legacy Siemens MOTOX K gearmotors (K38, K48, K68, K88, K108, K128) with zero frame modification.

The SIMOGEAR Bevel-Helical Right-Angle Geared Motor Series (K & B Series) delivers maximum torque efficiency, high radial load capacities, and compact right-angle layout for heavy industrial automation. Matched with Siemens / Innomotics SIMOTICS AC motors from 0.09 kW up to 55 kW, SIMOGEAR K/B delivers nominal output torques from 110 Nm up to 19,000 Nm. Available in solid shaft (K/KF), hollow shaft with keyway (KA/KAF), hollow shaft with shrink disc (KAS/KASF), and torque arm configurations, SIMOGEAR K is heavily specified across heavy belt conveyors, crane travel drives, baggage handling systems, bucket elevators, and roller tables.

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing and engineering configuration for the complete Siemens & Innomotics SIMOGEAR Bevel-Helical Geared Motor family. Whether your plant requires a solid shaft foot-mounted unit (K39 to K189), hollow shaft shaft-mounted unit with torque arm (KA39 to KA189), B5 flange execution (KF / KAF), or shrink disc hollow shaft execution (KAS / KASF), all units can be supplied pre-configured with integrated holding brakes, backstops, forced-cooling ventilation, or VFD-rated motor windings.

When replacing older Siemens Flender MOTOX K series gearmotors, choosing modern SIMOGEAR K units ensures higher energy efficiency (IE3/IE4 motors), high wear-free bevel gear durability, and extended lubricant service intervals while guaranteeing 100% exact physical mounting alignment without modifying torque arms or output shafts. Send us your existing MOTOX K or SIMOGEAR nameplate image, and our technical team will deliver a 100% compliant replacement quotation.
