Samson Type 3730 Smart Digital Electropneumatic Positioners

Brand: SAMSON AG | Category: Smart Digital Electropneumatic Positioners with EXPERT+ Diagnostics (Input: 4-20 mA / HART / PROFIBUS / Fieldbus | Stroke: Up to 300 mm)

The Samson Type 3730 Smart Digital Positioner Series (featured model: Type 3730-3 with HART communication) is SAMSON AG's premier intelligent positioner platform, engineered specifically for high-accuracy positioning, predictive maintenance, and SIL-certified fail-safe operation on linear and rotary control valves in chemical refineries, power plants, offshore platforms, and pharmaceutical processing lines[cite: 12]. Replacing traditional mechanical positioners, the Type 3730 features a **one-knob push-and-turn operation** and a multi-line graphic display that simplifies on-site commissioning[cite: 12]. With SAMSON's integrated **EXPERT+ diagnostic software suite**, the positioner continuously monitors valve health, detects packing friction, tracks supply air pressure, conducts Partial Stroke Tests (PST) for emergency shutdown valves, and pinpoints seat erosion before process failures occur[cite: 12]. Built inside a die-cast aluminum or stainless steel housing rated to **IP66 / NEMA 4X** with **ATEX / IECEx / FM explosion-proof approvals**, the Type 3730 supports versatile mounting options including **NAMUR rib attachment (IEC 60534-6-1)**, direct internal duct connection for **Type 3277 actuators**, and VDI/VDE 3845 rotary mounting for stroke lengths up to **300 mm**[cite: 12]. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, communication protocol configuration, mounting kit selection, and global express supply for Samson Type 3730 positioners worldwide[cite: 12].
