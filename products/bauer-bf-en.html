Bauer BF Series Parallel Shaft Geared Motors

Brand: Bauer Gear Motor | Category: Shaft-Mounted Parallel Shaft Geared Motors (BF06 to BF100 Range)

The Bauer BF Series (featured model: BF30-04/D09SA4 0.75 kW IE3) is the flagship parallel shaft helical geared motor from Bauer Gear Motor, setting the global benchmark for space-saving, narrow-profile drive installations. Featuring standard IP65 total enclosure protection (IP66, IP67, and IP68 submersed protection optional), BF series gearmotors are completely dust-tight and resistant to high-pressure water jets. Engineered with a compact offset output shaft, hollow shaft bore options, and specialized rubber-buffered torque arm assemblies, Bauer BF units offer unmatched irreplaceability in overhead cranes, hoists, belt conveyors, and agitators. Covering motor power ratings from 0.03 kW to 75 kW across 11 frame sizes (BF06 to BF100), the BF series seamlessly integrates with modern **IE3 Premium Efficiency motors** (D09 to D18) to replace legacy Bauer DK series and older IE1/IE2 motors in severe industrial environments.
