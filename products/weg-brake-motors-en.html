WEG Heavy-Duty Electromagnetic Brake Motors

Brand: WEG | Category: Integrated Electromagnetic Brake Motors (W22 & W21 Series: 0.12 kW to 160 kW, Frame 63 to 315)

The WEG Heavy-Duty Brake Motor Series (featured model: W22 Brake Motor 225S/M 37 kW 4-Pole) seamlessly integrates high-performance AC induction motor technology with a robust, fail-safe spring-applied electromagnetic DC or AC brake. Engineered specifically for applications requiring immediate stopping, precise holding, safety locking, and high-frequency start-stop cycles, WEG brake motors prevent coasting and back-driving during power interruptions or normal cycle halts. Available in heavy-duty FC-200 cast iron or lightweight aluminum enclosures covering power ratings from 0.12 kW up to 160 kW in IEC frame sizes 63 to 315, these motors feature high brake torque capacity (up to 200% of motor rated torque). Equipped with WISE® insulation for variable frequency drive (VFD) compatibility, optional manual release levers, and IP55/IP66 enclosures, WEG brake motors are widely specified across overhead cranes, hoists, elevators, automated conveyors, packaging machinery, and industrial winches.

Expert Sourcing & Material Handling Engineering Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED is your specialized technical partner for engineering calculations, brake torque verification, and global procurement of the complete WEG Heavy-Duty Brake Motor series. Operating brake motors in lifting, hoisting, inclined transport, and precision indexing applications demands absolute braking reliability and structural integrity to prevent load drops and positioning drift.

WEG brake motors feature fail-safe spring-applied brake pads, high-wear-resistant friction linings, dual-frequency brake coils, and heavy-duty cast iron endshields to absorb repetitive stopping kinetics without thermal fading. Equipped with WEG WISE® insulation system for variable speed operation, available manual release levers for power outage emergencies, and integrated microswitches for PLC feedback, these units deliver maximum equipment safety. Our engineering team assists with brake coil voltage selection, fast-switching rectifier selection (fast-action excitation), forced cooling fan integration for low-speed inverter operation, and encoder feedback integration.
