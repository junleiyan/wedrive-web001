Baldor-Reliance Super-E® NEMA Premium Efficiency Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: NEMA General Purpose AC Motors (EM / CEM Series: 1/4 HP to 500 HP)

The Baldor-Reliance Super-E® NEMA Premium Motor Series (featured model: EM3770T 7.5 HP 213T 4-pole) is North America's premier flagship general-purpose industrial motor line, engineered to deliver exceptional energy savings, low operating temperatures, and long service life. Designed to meet or exceed **NEMA Premium® efficiency standards (equivalent to IEC IE3 Premium Efficiency)**, these motors feature heavy-duty cast-iron frames (180T to 449T) or heavy-gauge steel frames (56 to 140T), precision-balanced rotors, and IP44/IP55 enclosure protection. Operating across **TEFC (Totally Enclosed Fan Cooled)** and **ODP (Open Drip Proof)** designs, Super-E motors incorporate Class H ISR® (Inverter Spike Resistant) copper winding insulation, making them fully rated for variable frequency drive operation with **ABB ACS580 and ACS880 drives**. Covering power ratings from 1/4 HP up to 500 HP across 2-pole, 4-pole, and 6-pole speeds, Baldor Super-E motors serve as the direct 1-to-1 mechanical replacement for industrial fans, centrifugal pumps, belt conveyors, air compressors, and general manufacturing equipment.
