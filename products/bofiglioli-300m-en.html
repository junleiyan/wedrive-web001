Bonfiglioli 305 Planetary Gearbox (300 & 300M Series)

Brand: Bonfiglioli Riduttori | Category: Heavy Industrial Planetary Drives (300 to 325M Range)

The Bonfiglioli 305 (featured model: 305 R2 1/25 HD) represents the industrial benchmark in planetary power transmission from Bonfiglioli's world-renowned 300 & 300M Series. Engineered for extreme duty, high shock loads, and maximum torque density in compact spaces, Bonfiglioli planetary speed reducers cover nominal output torques from 1,250 Nm up to 1,286,000 Nm across 20 standard frame sizes. Manufactured with heavy nodular cast iron housings, planet carriers with dual spherical roller support, and precision ground sun and planet gears, these units are available in coaxial inline (L1 to L4 stage) and right-angle bevel-planetary (R1 to R4 stage) configurations. With modular output shaft options (splined hollow shaft, solid keyed shaft, and shrink disc), Bonfiglioli 300/300M drives provide 100% direct 1-to-1 mechanical replacement for Brevini ED/ET Series, SEW P Series, and Flender PLANUREX planetary units across heavy mining feeders, winches, sugar mills, cement kilns, and mobile construction machinery.

Expert Selection & Retrofit Engineering Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in providing technical sourcing, replacement selection, and global supply for the complete Bonfiglioli 300 & 300M Industrial Planetary Drive family.
