Kollmorgen High-Torque Hybrid Stepper Motors & Drives

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: NEMA 17 to 42 Hybrid Stepper Motors (POWERMAX II, PMX, E/CT Series & P7000 Drives)

The Kollmorgen High-Torque Stepper Motor Series (featured model: P22NRXA-LNN-NS-00 NEMA 23 1.8° Stepper) is Kollmorgen's industry-leading hybrid stepper motor platform, designed for cost-effective, precise open-loop and closed-loop positioning across industrial automation, medical devices, laboratory instrumentation, and 3D printing equipment. Encompassing the flagship **POWERMAX II® (P & M Series)**, **PMX Series**, and **E/CT Series**, these 2-phase 1.8° hybrid steppers incorporate high-flux Neodymium stator magnets and optimized tooth profiles to deliver maximum holding torque per frame size with minimal detent cogging. Covering holding torques from 0.12 Nm up to 28.0 Nm across **NEMA 17, NEMA 23, NEMA 34, and NEMA 42** frame dimensions, Kollmorgen steppers offer dual shaft extensions, rear-mounted incremental encoders, IP65 shaft seals, and thermal overload protection. Matched seamlessly with **Kollmorgen P7000 and P5000 digital microstepping drives**, these motors serve as the direct replacement for legacy **Pacific Scientific (Sigmax / E & H Series)** and **Superior Electric Slo-Syn** stepper motors.
