TR-Electronic Compact Draw-Wire Encoders: SL00 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: SL0001, SL0002, SL0003, SL0005 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Ultra-Compact Cable Pull Mechanics for Confined Spaces:
As a leading TR draw-wire encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic, factory-direct TR-Electronic miniature cable-pull linear transducers. The TR SL00 series packs industrial-grade stainless steel measuring wire and precision spring-drum mechanics into a remarkably slim housing designed for short-to-medium strokes from 1 meter up to 5 meters.

Engineering Excellence for AGVs, Medical Equipment & Compact Machinery:
• Space-Saving Footprint: Engineered specifically for tight installation envelopes where standard SL30 drums are too large. Ideal for automated guided vehicle (AGV) lifting forks, medical imaging tables, and compact packaging machines.
• Flexible Encoder Integration: Can be paired with TR's ultra-compact C_V36 miniature encoders or standard 58mm encoders—supporting Analog (0-10V / 4-20mA), SSI, CANopen, IO-Link, PROFINET, and EtherCAT.
• High Dynamic Response: Low-inertia precision measuring drum and fatigue-resistant stainless steel wire accommodate rapid acceleration and high cycle rates without cable slack or backlash.

The TR Encoders SL00 Compact Draw-Wire Series eliminates the strict axial alignment requirements of rigid linear rods or optical glass scales. By simply mounting the compact housing to a stationary frame and attaching the wire eyelet to the moving carriage, engineers achieve immediate, reliable linear position feedback. Whether you are replacing a worn miniature string pot on an automated forklift mast or designing a compact laboratory positioning stage, our technical team ensures you receive the exact stroke length and output signal required.
