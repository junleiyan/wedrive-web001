TR-Electronic Laser Distance Sensors: LMC55 & LMC-55 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: LMC55, LMC-55, Legacy LE-200 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Long-Range Non-Contact Laser Positioning:
As a leading TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic TR-Electronic optical laser distance measurement systems. The TR LMC55 and LMC-55 series combine high-speed laser phase-comparison technology with rugged industrial housings to deliver millimeter-accurate positioning over distances up to 240 meters.

Engineering Excellence for Material Handling & Cranes:
• Completely Wear-Free Operation: Unlike mechanical encoders or draw-wire sensors, laser distance meters operate with zero physical contact. There are no wheels to slip, no cables to snap, and no mechanical bearings to wear out.
• Extreme Measuring Ranges: Capable of measuring distances from 0.2 meters up to 240 meters (using high-grade reflector foil) or up to 30 meters directly on natural, uncooperative surfaces (like hot steel slabs).
• Seamless Fieldbus & Safety Integration: Equipped with native industrial interfaces including PROFINET, EtherCAT, PROFIBUS DP, SSI, and Analog (4-20mA), plus integrated heating options for cold-storage logistics down to -40°C.

The TR Encoders LMC55 & LMC-55 Laser Series represent the modern standard for dynamic positioning in automated storage and retrieval systems (ASRS), overhead bridge cranes, and transfer shuttles. Featuring a visible red laser beam for effortless alignment and integrated alignment brackets, these sensors ensure fast commissioning and uninterrupted 24/7 operation. Whether you are replacing a legacy LE-200 laser unit or automating a high-bay warehouse, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the exact measurement range and interface required.
