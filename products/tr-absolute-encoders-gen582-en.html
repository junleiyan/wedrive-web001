TR-Electronic Absolute Encoders: GEN 582 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: CEV582, CMV582, CEH582 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Precision Engineering & Industry 4.0 Integration:
As a leading TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic, factory-direct TR-Electronic rotary encoders. The flagship TR encoders GEN 582 series represents the pinnacle of modern German industrial measurement, engineered to deliver distinct, immediately available position values upon power-up, eliminating the need for homing routines.

Engineering Excellence for Heavy Industry:
• Seamless Fieldbus Integration: The highly modular 58mm series (CEV582, CMV582) natively supports modern Industrial Ethernet protocols, including PROFINET, EtherCAT, PROFIBUS, SSI, CANopen, and Ethernet/IP.
• Dual Scanning Technologies: Choose between high-resolution optical scanning (CEV series) for CNC robotics and packaging automation, or robust magnetic scanning (CMV series) providing superior resistance to shock, condensation, and dirt—ideal for wind power pitch control and heavy-duty offshore equipment.
• Battery-Free Multi-Turn: Real mechanical gearing ensures that the rotational position is retained over the entire lifespan of the sensor without battery maintenance, perfectly suited for continuous operations in steel mills, mining hoists, and AGV navigation.

The TR Encoders GEN 582 Series guarantees maximum operational reliability under the most demanding environmental conditions. Available in solid shaft (clamping and synchro flanges), blind hollow shaft, and through hollow shaft (CEH series) configurations, they offer protection ratings up to IP67. Whether you are replacing a legacy CEV65 unit in a steel plant or designing a highly dynamic servo-driven packaging line, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the exact configuration required to keep your automation running.
