WEG W21 Low Voltage Aluminum Motors

Brand: WEG | Category: Low Voltage Induction Motors (W21 Series: 0.12 kW to 37 kW, IE2/IE3)

The WEG W21 Series (featured model: W21 90L-4 1.5 kW IE3) delivers the perfect balance of lightweight design, excellent thermal performance, and exceptional mounting flexibility. Constructed from a high-quality injected aluminum frame, the W21 motor is significantly lighter than its cast-iron counterparts, making it the ideal choice for applications where weight reduction and space constraints are critical. A defining feature of the WEG W21 is its multimounting capability; the removable feet allow the terminal box to be positioned on the top, left, or right side without disassembling the motor, drastically reducing OEM inventory requirements. Covering a versatile power range from 0.12 kW to 37 kW across standard IEC frame sizes (63 to 200L), the W21 series is fully compliant with global energy standards, available in IE2 Standard and IE3 Premium Efficiency. It is the premier general-purpose motor for packaging machinery, HVAC systems, water pumps, light conveyors, and automated manufacturing equipment.

Expert Technical Sourcing & OEM System Integration Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in technical sourcing, exact part number identification, and global supply for the complete WEG W21 Low Voltage Aluminum Motor portfolio. The W21 series is uniquely favored by Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) due to its multimounting architecture. Instead of stocking separate motors with left, right, or top terminal boxes, OEMs can simply unbolt and reposition the W21's feet to adapt to any spatial constraint.

Whether your production line requires standard foot-mounted setups (B3), small flange (B14), large flange (B5), or foot/flange combinations (B34/B35), the WEG W21 adapts instantly. When replacing older, bulky cast iron motors in applications like rooftop HVAC units, commercial water pumps, or clean-environment packaging lines, upgrading to the lightweight WEG W21 IE3 series provides rapid ROI through energy savings and reduced structural wear. Simply provide us with your existing motor's nameplate data or desired IEC frame configuration, and our engineering team will deliver a 100% compliant specification and rapid global supply proposal.
