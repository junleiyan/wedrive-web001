Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS S AC Servo Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS S (1FL6, 1FK7, 1FK2, 1FT7 Servo Range)
Brand Lineage & Regional Production Advantage Notice:
As part of Siemens' motion control transmission lineup, SIMOTICS S Servo Motors are fully supported through our direct OEM factory channels.

Mainstream Chinese / Asian Production Models vs. Overseas Models:
• SIMOTICS S-1FL6 Series (China Production Mainstream): Purpose-built for high-volume industrial automation, paired with SINAMICS V90 drives. Covers Low Inertia (0.05 kW - 1.0 kW) and High Inertia (0.4 kW - 7.0 kW). Offers unbeatable cost performance and off-the-shelf availability.
• SIMOTICS S-1FK7 / 1FK2 Series (Asian & Global Standard): Compact, high-dynamic permanent magnet synchronous servo motors for SINAMICS S120 & S210 drives. Manufactured in both China and Europe.
• SIMOTICS S-1FT7 Series (European High-End Import): Ultra-high-precision servo motors engineered for premium CNC machine tool spindles and high-speed multi-axis motion.
• Direct Cross-Matching: Chinese-produced 1FL6 and 1FK7 models offer 1:1 functional and dimensional performance matches to replace expensive European imported models, significantly reducing machinery BOM costs.

The Siemens SIMOTICS S AC Servo Motor family sets the benchmark for speed control dynamics, positioning accuracy, and power density across modern motion control systems. Engineered with high-coercivity permanent magnets, IP65/IP67 protective housings, DRIVE-CLiQ digital encoder interfaces, and high-resolution absolute encoders (up to 21-bit / 20-bit), SIMOTICS S motors power electronic assembly, semiconductor handling, packaging machines, CNC machine tools, industrial robotics, and automated printing lines.

Expert Selection & Servo Matching Engineering Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing and motion control engineering support for the complete Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS S AC Servo Motor family. Whether your automated machine design specifies cost-effective China-produced servo motors paired with SINAMICS V90 (1FL6 Low Inertia / High Inertia), multi-axis high-dynamic servos for SINAMICS S120 / S210 (1FK7 / 1FK2), or imported high-precision CNC spindle/axis motors (1FT7), all motors can be ordered pre-configured with holding brakes, IP67 shaft oil seals, straight or right-angle rotatable power connectors, and DRIVE-CLiQ encoder feedback cables.

For machine builders seeking to reduce BOM costs without sacrificing motion precision or software compatibility, upgrading from imported European 1FT7/1FK7 motors to domestic Chinese-produced 1FL6 or 1FK7 series provides substantial cost advantages, eliminates long international supply chain delays, and ensures 100% seamless commissioning inside TIA Portal and V-ASSISTANT software. Send us your motor model code or load torque requirement, and our engineering team will provide a fully matched servo drive and motor package.
