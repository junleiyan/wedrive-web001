Lenze i550 Cabinet Frequency Inverters

Brand: Lenze SE | Category: Advanced Control Cabinet Frequency Inverters (0.25 kW to 110.0 kW Range)

The Lenze i550 Cabinet Inverter Series (featured model: i55AE222F1000000S 2.2 kW 3-Phase 400V) is Lenze's versatile, high-performance variable frequency drive platform, engineered for sophisticated machine automation, high-dynamic speed/torque control, and Industry 4.0 connectivity. Featuring integrated **Safe Torque Off (STO SIL3 / PL e)** functional safety, modular fieldbus communication options (PROFINET, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP, CANopen, Modbus RTU), and wireless commissioning via smartphone WLAN app or USB diagnostic module, the i550 delivers exceptional operational efficiency. Covering output power ratings from 0.25 kW up to 110.0 kW across 1-phase 120/230V and 3-phase 230V/400V/480V mains, the i550 supports V/f, Sensorless Vector (SLVC), and Servo motor control. Engineered for seamless system integration, Lenze i550 inverters serve as the direct, modernized replacement for legacy **Lenze 8400 StateLine/HighLine** and **Lenze 8200 Vector** drives across pumps, fans, winders, hoists, and automated packaging lines.
