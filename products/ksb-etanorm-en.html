KSB Etanorm & Etabloc Standardized Volute Casing Water Pumps

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: EN 733 / ISO 2858 Standardized Water & Circulation Centrifugal Pumps (Flow: Up to 1,900 m³/h | Head: Up to 160 m)

The KSB Etanorm Standardized Water Pump Series (encompassing long-coupled **Etanorm** and close-coupled **Etabloc** monobloc models, featured model: Etanorm G 050-032-160) is the world's most widely specified single-stage volute casing pump platform, engineered to EN 733 and ISO 2858 standards for water supply systems, industrial cooling circuits, HVAC water circulation, irrigation, and fire-fighting systems. Driven by standardized IEC electric motors (compatible with high-efficiency IE3, IE4, and magnetless SuPremE® IE5 synchronous motors), Etanorm pumps feature CFD-optimized impellers and refined volute casings that deliver market-leading hydraulic efficiency exceeding ErP Directive energy benchmarks. Constructed with heavy-duty grey cast iron (JL1040), bronze (CC480K), or stainless steel (1.4408) casings and fitted with standardized EN 12756 mechanical shaft seals, Etanorm pumps operate smoothly across discharge sizes from **DN 25 to DN 150** with motor outputs up to **315 kW**, achieving total dynamic heads up to **160 meters** and discharge capacities up to **1,900 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, impeller trimming, motor power pairing, and global express supply for KSB Etanorm and Etabloc standardized pumps worldwide.
