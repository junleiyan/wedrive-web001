ABB AMI High Voltage Rib-Cooled Induction Motors

Brand: ABB | Category: High Voltage Rib-Cooled Induction Motors (AMI 400 to AMI 560 Range: 200 kW to 8000 kW)

The ABB AMI High Voltage Motor Series (featured model: AMI 450L4A 1000 kW 6.6 kV) is ABB's premier high-voltage rib-cooled squirrel-cage induction motor platform, engineered for severe ambient environments, heavy-duty continuous process loads, and high power density requirements. Utilizing a heavy-duty cast iron or welded steel rib-cooled frame with **IC411 (self-ventilated)** or **IC416 (forced-ventilated for VFD duty)** cooling design, AMI motors provide superior heat dissipation without external heat exchangers. Covering output power ratings from 200 kW up to 8000 kW across high-voltage grid supplies from 2.3 kV up to 11 kV (AMI 400, AMI 450, AMI 500, and AMI 560 frame sizes), AMI motors feature **Vacuum Pressure Impregnated (VPI)** Class H insulation, rated for direct variable speed drive operation with **ABB ACS880 and MEGADRIVE medium voltage inverters**. Designed for uncompromising reliability, ABB AMI motors serve as the direct, modernized replacement for legacy **ABB HXR** cast-iron rib-cooled motors in power plant cooling pumps, ID/FD fans, cement kiln drives, and oil & gas pipeline pumps.
