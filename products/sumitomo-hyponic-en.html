Sumitomo Hyponic® Right-Angle Geared Motors & Reducers

Brand: Sumitomo Drive Technologies | Category: Right-Angle Hypoid Geared Motors (15W to 11 kW Range: 20 Nm to 1,470 Nm)

The Sumitomo Hyponic® Geared Motor Series (featured model: RNYM1-1430-30 0.75 kW 1:30 Ratio) is Sumitomo's high-efficiency, space-saving right-angle drive platform, engineered specifically for commercial food processing, material handling, conveyor systems, packaging machines, and bottling automation. Utilizing advanced **hypoid gear technology** with all-steel hardened pinion and gear sets, Hyponic units achieve efficiency ratings of **up to 85%**—delivering far greater energy efficiency and higher thermal capacity than conventional worm gear reducers. Pre-lubricated at the factory with **maintenance-free lifetime synthetic grease**, these drives eliminate oil changes and can be mounted in any orientation without breathers or oil-level plugs. Available in hollow shaft (RNYM series) and solid shaft (RNHM series) configurations across lightweight aluminum and heavy-duty cast-iron housings. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model configuration decoding, and 100% direct replacement support for Sumitomo Hyponic right-angle geared motors across global manufacturing operations.
