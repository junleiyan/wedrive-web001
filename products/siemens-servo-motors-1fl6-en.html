Siemens SIMOTICS S-1FL6 Standard Synchronous Servo Motors

Brand: Siemens Motion Control | Series: SIMOTICS S-1FL6 (Low Inertia & High Inertia Variants)
Standard Motion Control Excellence — Siemens Digital Industries:
The SIMOTICS S-1FL6 servo motor system is Siemens' premier, cost-optimized standard servo drive platform developed specifically for pairing with the SINAMICS V90 servo drive. While general low-voltage AC induction motors operate under the Innomotics brand, Siemens maintains complete ownership, R&D, and global manufacturing of this high-demand motion control portfolio.

Engineering Advantages & System Integration:
• Seamless V90 Plug-and-Play Integration: Direct electronic matching with SINAMICS V90 (Pulse/Direction PTI and PROFINET PN variants) ensures rapid commissioning via auto-tuning, automatic suppression of mechanical resonances, and optimized load adaptation.
• Dual Inertia Topologies: 1FL6 Low Inertia (LI) offers maximum dynamic responsiveness and acceleration across shaft heights 20, 30, 40, and 50 (0.05 kW to 2.0 kW); 1FL6 High Inertia (HI) provides superior control stability under high external load inertias across shaft heights 45, 65, and 90 (0.4 kW to 7.0 kW).
• Rugged Industrial Execution: Equipped with industrial-grade military-style connectors or quick-locking pre-assembled cables, IP65 degree of protection (with shaft oil seal), and optional integrated 24V holding brakes.

The Siemens SIMOTICS S-1FL6 Synchronous Servo Motor Family delivers outstanding torque linearity, cost-effective motion precision, and heavy-duty reliability for commercial automation machinery. Spanning rated power ratings from 0.05 kW up to 7.0 kW and rated speeds of 2,000 rpm and 3,000 rpm (with maximum speeds reaching 5,000 rpm), 1FL6 motors are available with TTL incremental encoders (2500 ppr) or multi-turn absolute encoders (20-bit + 12-bit). Standard options include plain or keyed shafts, IP65 housing protection, and high-flex MOTION-CONNECT 300 cabling. They are specified worldwide in electronic assembly equipment, pick-and-place packaging, labeling machines, textile feeding mechanisms, semiconductor handling, and laser cutting machines.

1FL6 Low Inertia vs. 1FL6 High Inertia Selection Matrix

Engineering comparison between the Low Inertia (LI) and High Inertia (HI) motor architectures:

Design Variant Rotor Inertia Characteristics Rated Voltage Supply Load Inertia Ratio ($J_L / J_M$) Target Automation Applications
SIMOTICS S-1FL6 Low Inertia (LI) Extremely low rotor inertia for rapid acceleration and high-frequency stops 1-phase / 3-phase 200 V to 240 V AC Optimized for low load inertia systems ($J_L / J_M \le 5$) High-speed electronic assembly machines, pick-and-place arms, high-cycle packaging, labeling machines.
SIMOTICS S-1FL6 High Inertia (HI) Larger rotor inertia matching heavier moving mechanical payloads 3-phase 380 V to 480 V AC (also 200 V) Stable control with high load inertia systems ($J_L / J_M \le 15$) Rotary tables, vertical positioning axes, metal stamping feeders, winding machines, CNC tool turrets.
