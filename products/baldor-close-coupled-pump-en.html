Baldor-Reliance Close-Coupled Pump Motors (JM & JP Frame)

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: NEMA Close-Coupled Commercial & Industrial Pump Motors (1 HP to 75 HP)

The Baldor-Reliance Close-Coupled Pump Motor Series (featured model: JMM3218T 5 HP 184JM 2-pole) is North America's premier electric motor platform designed specifically for mounting directly to centrifugal, booster, and fluid circulation pumps. Engineered to meet strict NEMA C-face dimensions with specialized extended shafts, these motors are divided into **JM Frame** models (designed for mechanical shaft seals) and **JP Frame** models (designed for packing gland seals). Operating at **Super-E® NEMA Premium Efficiency** levels, Baldor pump motors feature heavy-duty cast-iron or thick gauge steel frames, locked drive-end bearings to limit axial shaft movement, IP54/IP55 splash-proof enclosures, and Class H ISR® (Inverter Spike Resistant) copper winding insulation. Covering power output ratings from 1 HP up to 75 HP across 2-pole (3600 rpm) and 4-pole (1800 rpm) speeds (NEMA frame sizes 140JM/JP to 365JM/JP), Baldor close-coupled pump motors deliver 100% direct mechanical alignment to replace legacy OEM pump motors in HVAC chilled water loops, industrial wash systems, agricultural irrigation, and municipal water boosting.
