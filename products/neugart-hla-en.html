Neugart HLA Hygienic Design Stainless Steel Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: EHEDG Certified Stainless Steel Planetary Reducers (HLA070 to HLA115 Range: 27 Nm to 350 Nm)

The Neugart HLA Hygienic Design Planetary Gearbox Series (featured model: HLA070 1-Stage 5:1 Ratio) is the world's first **EHEDG-certified hygienic design planetary gearhead**, engineered specifically for food processing, primary packaging, pharmaceutical dosing, and chemical washdown environments. Built with high-grade electropolished stainless steel casings featuring smooth, rounded contours, the HLA series eliminates horizontal flat surfaces, blind pockets, and exposed bolt threads where organic residue or bacteria can accumulate. Certified to **IP69K water tightness standards**, these units withstand high-pressure, high-temperature chemical jet washdowns (up to 100 bar at 80°C). Pre-filled with **NSF H1 food-grade synthetic grease**, HLA reducers utilize specialized radial shaft seals with PTFE sealing lips to prevent lubricant leakage into food zones. Incorporating precision ground helical gearing, HLA gearboxes deliver exceptional positioning accuracy (<3 to <5 arcmin backlash) and high dynamic efficiency across 3 frame sizes (HLA070, HLA090, HLA115). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter matching for Neugart HLA hygienic planetary drives worldwide.
