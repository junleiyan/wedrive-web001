Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS XP Dust Ignition-Proof Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS XP (Zone 21 Ex tb & Zone 22 Ex tc Dust Explosion-Proof Range)
Combustible Dust Safety Heritage — Siemens to Innomotics:
The SIMOTICS XP dust explosion-proof motor series represents Siemens' dedicated hazardous area electric drive platform engineered specifically for environments containing conductive or non-conductive combustible dusts, flyings, and fibers. Following the corporate transition of low-voltage motors, this specialized portfolio is engineered and manufactured under the independent brand identity Innomotics.

Engineering Protection & Dust Ingress Safety Standards:
• Dual Zone Certification: Fully certified under ATEX, IECEx, and China Ex (CCC-Ex) for Zone 21 (Ex tb IIIC Db) with IP65/IP66 enclosure protection and Zone 22 (Ex tc IIIB Dc) with IP55 protection.
• Maximum Surface Temperature Control: Strictly limits external casing surface temperatures ($T120^\circ\text{C}$, $T130^\circ\text{C}$, or $T135^\circ\text{C}$) under full load and abnormal conditions, ensuring temperatures stay well below the smoldering and cloud ignition points of industrial dusts.
• Robust Cast-Iron & Aluminum Topologies: Built across the 1MB15 series (rugged cast iron for severe chemical/mining atmospheres) and 1MB10 series (die-cast aluminum for grain elevators and textile mills) covering power ratings from 0.09 kW to 315 kW+.

The Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS XP Dust Explosion-Proof Motor Series provides fail-safe explosion protection, premium energy efficiency (IE2, IE3, and IE4), and reliable drive power in dusty process industries. Designed for direct online (DOL) grid operation and variable frequency drive (VFD) inverter duty up to 690V, these motors feature high-integrity radial shaft seals, special gasketed terminal boxes, Class F insulation utilized to Class B thermal limits, and optional PTC thermistors or Pt100 RTDs for continuous winding temperature supervision. They are heavily specified worldwide in grain storage silos, flour mills, animal feed plants, sugar refineries, coal pulverizing plants, plastics processing, pharmaceutical tableting, and wood finishing facilities.

Zone 22 (Category 3D) Ex tc IIIB T120°C Dc (Protection by enclosure) IP55 standard (IP65 optional) Approved for Group IIIB (non-conductive) and Group IIIA (combustible flyings) Secondary process areas, bulk storage packaging halls, conveyor galleries where dust clouds occur only rarely and briefly.
