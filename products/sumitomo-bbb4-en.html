Sumitomo Bevel Buddybox® BBB4 Right-Angle Speed Reducers

Brand: Sumitomo Drive Technologies | Category: Right-Angle Cyclo-Bevel Reducers (Size A to G Range: 250 Nm to 18,000 Nm)

The Sumitomo Bevel Buddybox® BBB4 Series (featured model: YNHM2-6135Y-B-87 1.5 kW 1:87 Ratio) is Sumitomo's high-torque, heavy-duty right-angle speed reducer platform, designed specifically for demanding material handling, mining conveyors, wastewater agitators, and bulk processing equipment. Uniquely combining a **shock-resistant Cyclo® input speed reduction stage** with a **high-efficiency spiral bevel gear output stage**, BBB4 reducers deliver **500% momentary shock load resistance** without gear teeth breakage. Constructed with all-ductile iron housings for ultimate structural rigidity, these units eliminate the need for expensive motor bedplates or fluid couplings. Available with hollow output shafts utilizing the patented **Taper-Grip® keyless bushing system** (enabling easy shaft mounting and fretting corrosion prevention) as well as solid output shafts and C-face motor adapters. Covering output torques from 250 Nm up to 18,000 Nm across reduction ratios from 11:1 to 26,000:1, Sumitomo BBB4 speed reducers serve as the direct 1-to-1 mechanical upgrade for legacy **Sumitomo BBB2 and BBB3** right-angle drives.
