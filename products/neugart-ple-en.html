Neugart PLE & PLQE Economy Line Inline Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: Economy Low-Backlash Inline Planetary Gearheads (Frame 40 to 160 Range: 8 Nm to 1,000 Nm)

The Neugart PLE & PLQE Economy Line Planetary Gearbox Series (featured model: PLE080 1-Stage 1:5 Ratio) is Neugart's industry-leading cost-effective inline drive platform, engineered specifically for dynamic motion control, high efficiency, and quiet power transmission in packaging machinery, industrial automation, medical equipment, and conveyor axes. Built with precision-ground spur gear teeth and low-friction deep-groove ball bearings, the **PLE series** offers high efficiency (up to 97%) and low torsional backlash (**<7 to <10 arcmin**) in a lightweight aluminum/steel housing. For applications demanding higher radial and axial load capacity, the **PLQE variant** incorporates reinforced output bearings and a robust square output flange. Available across 5 modular frame sizes (40, 60, 80, 120, 160) covering 1-stage (3:1 to 10:1), 2-stage (12:1 to 100:1), and 3-stage (up to 512:1) reduction ratios with output torques up to **1,000 Nm**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter matching for Neugart PLE & PLQE inline planetary gearboxes worldwide.
