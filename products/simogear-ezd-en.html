Siemens & Innomotics SIMOGEAR Coaxial Helical Geared Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOGEAR E, Z, D Series (E39-E149, Z19-Z189, D19-D189)
Brand Ownership & Lineage Notice — Siemens to Innomotics:
SIMOGEAR is the modern, highly efficient geared motor platform developed by Siemens as the direct successor to the legacy Flender MOTOX series[cite: 1]. Following Siemens' corporate restructuring of its electric motor and geared motor division, the entire SIMOGEAR portfolio has been integrated under the Innomotics brand identity[cite: 1].

Engineering Advantage & MOTOX Retrofit Compatibility:
• SIMOGEAR coaxial helical units offer up to 96% high transmission efficiency per stage, higher torque density, and plug-in motor pinion interfaces[cite: 1].
• Integrated with Siemens/Innomotics LE/LA motors (such as 1LE0003 and 1LE1501 IE3 motors) across frame sizes IEC 63 to IEC 225M[cite: 1].
• 100% Direct Drop-in Retrofit: SIMOGEAR sizes (e.g., Z39, Z49, D69, D89, D109) feature standardized output shaft diameters and foot/flange bolt patterns designed to directly replace legacy Siemens MOTOX gearmotors (Z38, Z48, D68, D88, D108) with zero frame modification[cite: 1].

The SIMOGEAR Coaxial Inline Helical Geared Motor Series (comprising 1-stage E, 2-stage Z, and 3-stage D executions) provides superior energy efficiency, low acoustic noise levels, and compact dimensions for industrial automation[cite: 1]. Matched with Siemens / Innomotics SIMOTICS AC motors from 0.09 kW up to 55 kW, SIMOGEAR delivers nominal output torques from 30 Nm up to 19,000 Nm[cite: 1]. Available in foot-mounted, B5 flange-mounted (EF/ZF/DF), B14 flange-mounted (EZ/ZZ/DZ), and agitator design options, SIMOGEAR is heavily specified across conveyor systems, roller tables, water treatment mixers, car assembly plants, and automated logistics warehouses[cite: 1].
