Flender RUPEX RWN 360 Flexible Pin & Bush Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: RUPEX Heavy-Duty Shock-Damping Couplings (Size 105 to 2000)[cite: 24]

The Flender RUPEX RWN 360 is a heavy-duty, shock-damping flexible pin and bush coupling engineered for demanding industrial drive trains requiring high torque capacity, fail-safe reliability, and impact load absorption[cite: 24]. Featuring a fail-safe design with conical buffer pins mounted in spherical flexible elastomer bushes[cite: 24], the RUPEX RWN 360 delivers a nominal torque capacity of 13,200 Nm (peak torque up to 33,000 Nm)[cite: 24]. Covering 25 frame sizes from size 105 to 2000 (200 Nm to 1,300,000 Nm)[cite: 24], RUPEX couplings feature cast iron/steel standard designs (RWN), high-speed all-steel executions (RWS), integrated brake discs (RWB/RBS), brake drums (RWD), and shear-pin overload protection (RWT)[cite: 24]. They are extensively specified across heavy cranes, cement ball mills, mine hoists, bucket wheel excavators, crushers, and marine winch drives[cite: 24].
