Flender RUPEX RWN 360 Flexible Pin & Bush Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: RUPEX Heavy-Duty Shock-Damping Couplings (Size 105 to 2000)

The Flender RUPEX RWN 360 is a heavy-duty, shock-damping flexible pin and bush coupling engineered for demanding industrial drive trains requiring high torque capacity, fail-safe reliability, and impact load absorption. Featuring a fail-safe design with conical buffer pins mounted in spherical flexible elastomer bushes, the RUPEX RWN 360 delivers a nominal torque capacity of 13,200 Nm (peak torque up to 33,000 Nm). Covering 25 frame sizes from size 105 to 2000 (200 Nm to 1,300,000 Nm), RUPEX couplings feature cast iron/steel standard designs (RWN), high-speed all-steel executions (RWS), integrated brake discs (RWB/RBS), brake drums (RWD), and shear-pin overload protection (RWT). They are extensively specified across heavy cranes, cement ball mills, mine hoists, bucket wheel excavators, crushers, and marine winch drives.
