TR-Electronic Draw-Wire Encoders: SL30 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: SL3002, SL3005, SL3010, SL3015, SL3030 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Long-Range Linear Measurement via Rugged Cable Pull Mechanics:
As a leading TR draw-wire encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic, factory-direct TR-Electronic cable-pull linear transducers. The flagship TR SL30 series pairs high-precision, precision-wound stainless steel measuring drums with TR's modular 58mm rotary encoders (such as CEV582 or IEV58), converting linear displacement up to 30 meters into exact digital or analog position feedback.

Engineering Excellence for Hoisting, Telescoping & Industrial Automation:
• Flexible Routing & Easy Installation: Unlike rigid linear glass scales or magnetostrictive rods, draw-wire encoders require no precise axial alignment. Using deflection pulleys, the measuring cable can even be routed around corners or obstacles.
• Modular Encoder Pairing: The SL30 mechanics can be equipped with virtually any TR-Electronic rotary encoder—supporting modern Industrial Ethernet protocols (PROFINET, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP), classic fieldbuses (PROFIBUS DP, CANopen, SSI), Analog (4-20mA / 0-10V), or Incremental (HTL/TTL) outputs.
• Heavy-Duty Spring & Cable Durability: Engineered with high-grade, polyamide-coated stainless steel measuring ropes and fatigue-resistant return springs, guaranteeing millions of operating cycles in crane hoists, hydraulic scissor lifts, and stage automation.

The TR Encoders SL30 Draw-Wire Series is the ideal solution wherever long linear travel distances must be measured reliably in confined spaces or harsh environments. Available in graduated measuring lengths from 1 meter up to 30 meters, these sensors feature dirt-wiping brush seals and rugged anodized aluminum housings. Whether you are replacing a damaged string pot on a mobile crane boom or designing a new automated warehouse lift, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the exact drum circumference, encoder protocol, and mechanical accessories required.
