Festo Solenoid Valves & Valve Terminals: VUVG, CPE, VTUG/MPA-S/CPX & MFH/VSNC NAMUR

Brand: Festo | Core Protocols: PROFINET, EtherCAT, IO-Link, Multi-Pin | Global Festo Pneumatic Supplier
High-Flow Directional Control & Smart Fieldbus Automation:
As a leading global Festo pneumatic supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies the full spectrum of Festo directional control solenoid valves and modular valve terminals. Engineered for ultra-fast switching times, low power consumption, and seamless PLC integration, Festo solenoid valves maximize machine throughput across factory and process automation.

Core Valve Families Covered on This Page:
• VUVG Series (Compact High-Flow Solenoid Valves): Ultra-slim inline and semi-inline valves (10 mm, 14 mm, 18 mm widths; M5, G1/8, G1/4) delivering maximum flow density for individual mounting or VTUG valve manifolds.
• CPE Series (Universal Solenoid Valves): Proven high-flow 3/2 and 5/2-way valves (CPE10, CPE14, CPE18, CPE24) designed for heavy-duty cyclic operation, individual sub-base mounting, or CPV/CPE manifolds.
• VTUG / MPA-S / CPX Series (Modular Fieldbus Valve Terminals): Integrated multi-station valve islands supporting PROFINET, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP, PROFIBUS DP, Modbus TCP, and IO-Link with IP65/IP67/IP69K washdown options.
• MFH / VSNC / NAMUR Series (Tiger Classic & Process Valves): Rugged poppet-seat Tiger Classic valves (MFH/JMFH) and VDI/VDE 3845 NAMUR interface pilot valves (VSNC) with ATEX Ex-coil options for quarter-turn process actuators.

The Festo Solenoid Valves lineup covers every industrial control architecture—from standalone 24V DC / 230V AC pilot valves to complex CPX/MPA-S automation platforms with integrated digital/analog I/O and multi-pressure zones. Whether you need a single replacement valve slice, a complete pre-configured VTUG manifold, or an ATEX-certified NAMUR valve for oil & gas process lines, our technical team verifies valve function (5/2 monostable, 5/2 bistable, 5/3 closed/exhausted, 2x3/2), coil voltage, and electrical connection standards to ensure 100% factory-compliant supply.

Festo Solenoid Valve & Terminal Comparison: VUVG vs. CPE vs. VTUG/MPA-S vs. VSNC

Engineering selection guide to match flow demand, control network topology, and environmental conditions with the optimal Festo valve series:

Series Family Mounting & Integration Concept Key Engineering Advantage Recommended Operating Condition
VUVG Series Individual inline valve or manifold-mounted on VTUG Exceptionally high flow-to-size ratio (up to 1,380 l/min in 18 mm width) with exchangeable electrical E-boxes and LED status indicators. Space-constrained machines requiring fast response times and flexible single-valve or manifold deployment.
CPE Series Standalone body-ported or aluminum manifold strip (PAL) Long service life (>100 million cycles), integrated manual override, and wide AC/DC coil compatibility for global machine builds. Continuous high-cycle industrial automation where robust individual valves or simple air bars are preferred.
VTUG / MPA-S / CPX Centralized multi-station valve island with single bus node Eliminates discrete solenoid wiring; supports up to 32 valve positions, hot-swappable slices (MPA-S), and integrated CPX diagnostic I/O modules. Complex automated lines controlled via Siemens PROFINET, Beckhoff EtherCAT, Allen-Bradley EtherNet/IP, or IO-Link.
MFH / VSNC NAMUR Direct NAMUR flange mount (VSNC) or rugged threaded body (MFH) VSNC features rotatable seal for instant 3/2 to 5/2 conversion, -20°C to +60°C range, and ATEX/SIL2 certifications for process valves. Process automation (chemical, pharmaceutical, water treatment) and heavy-duty pneumatic control cabinets.
