Kollmorgen AKD & AKD2G Digital Servo Drives

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: High-Bandwidth Digital Servo Amplifiers (AKD / AKD2G Series: 3A to 28A Arms)

The Kollmorgen AKD & AKD2G Digital Servo Drive Series (featured model: AKD-P00606-NBEC-0000 EtherCAT Drive) represents Kollmorgen's flagship digital servo amplifier ecosystem, engineered specifically to deliver maximum dynamic speed, position, and torque control for high-performance automation. Featuring an ultra-fast **62.5 microsecond servo update loop** and high-bandwidth current loops, AKD drives provide plug-and-play parameter recognition when paired with **Kollmorgen AKM, AKM2G, Cartridge DDR, and KBM frameless motors**. The **AKD2G Series** expands control capabilities with dual-axis power density, integrated color displays, SafeMotion Monitor (STO SIL3 / PL e), and single-cable SFD3 / HIPERFACE DSL digital feedback interfaces. Supporting Ethernet fieldbus protocols including **EtherCAT, PROFINET RT/IRT, CANopen, EtherNet/IP, and SynqNet**, Kollmorgen AKD drives cover continuous output currents from 3A to 24A Arms (up to 48A peak) across single-phase 120/240V and three-phase 240/480V mains. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Kollmorgen AKD drives, firmware options, mating cable assemblies, and direct engineering replacement support for legacy **SERVOSTAR 300 / 600** amplifiers.
