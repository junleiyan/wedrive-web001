Rossi IEC Premium Efficiency Electric Motors

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Heavy Duty IEC Asynchronous Electric Motors (Frame 63 to 315 Range: 0.12 kW to 315 kW)

The Rossi Electric Motor Series (featured model: HB3Z 100L4 3.0 kW IE3) represents Rossi's high-efficiency industrial AC motor line, engineered specifically to seamlessly integrate with Rossi gear reducers or operate as standalone industrial drives. Built to strict **IEC 60034-30-1 IE3 Premium and IE4 Super Premium efficiency standards**, Rossi electric motors feature low-loss steel laminations, high-purity copper windings, Class F/H thermal insulation, and low-vibration rotor balancing. Covering rated power outputs from 0.12 kW up to 315 kW across IEC frame sizes 63 to 315 (2-pole, 4-pole, 6-pole, and 8-pole configurations), these motors are housed in robust lightweight aluminum (up to frame 160) or heavy-duty cast iron (frames 180 to 315). Available in standard asynchronous executions (HB Series), high-efficiency executions (HE Series), and heavy-duty safety brake executions (HBZ with DC brake, HBF with AC brake). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model decoding, and 100% compliant replacement support for Rossi electric motors across worldwide manufacturing facilities.
