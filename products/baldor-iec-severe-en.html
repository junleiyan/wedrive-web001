Baldor-Reliance IEC Severe Duty Cast-Iron Process Motors & Cross-Brand Selection

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: Metric IEC Severe Duty Cast-Iron Motors (IEC 80 to 355 Range: 0.75 kW to 355 kW)

The Baldor-Reliance IEC Severe Duty Motor Series (featured model: 15 kW 4-pole IEC 160L) is Baldor's heavy-duty process performance metric motor platform, engineered for severe continuous-duty industrial applications in petro-chemical plants, mining operations, paper mills, and heavy process manufacturing where extreme environmental durability is paramount. Combining North American IEEE 841 severe duty design principles with strict **IEC 60034-30-1 metric mounting standards**, these motors feature **IE3 Premium Efficiency and IE4 Super Premium Efficiency** ratings. Constructed with heavy cast-iron frames, cast-iron fan covers and terminal boxes, InPRO/SEAL® bearing isolators on both ends, SPM vibration monitoring nipples, IP56/IP66 enclosure protection, and Class H ISR® VFD-rated copper winding insulation. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides full technical selection, fast express sourcing, and 100% direct mechanical replacement cross-references mapping Baldor IEC Severe Duty models to equivalent **ABB M3BP, Siemens 1LE16 Severe Duty, WEG W22 Severe Duty, and SEW DRN Series** severe process motors.
