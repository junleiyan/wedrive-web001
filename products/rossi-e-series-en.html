Rossi E Series Coaxial Helical Gearmotors & Speed Reducers

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Monolithic Coaxial Helical Gearmotors (Size 32 to 180 Range: 100 Nm to 10,000 Nm)

The Rossi E Series Coaxial Helical Gearmotor Platform (featured model: MR E 80 UO2A 3.0 kW 1:14.5 Ratio) is Rossi's flagship inline drive platform, engineered specifically for high power density, quiet performance, and structural rigidity in heavy industrial conveyors, mixers, pumps, packaging machinery, and material handling systems. Built around Rossi's signature **monolithic (single-piece) cast-iron housing**, E Series inline gearboxes eliminate split-housing joints and gasket distortion, achieving zero oil leakage, high resistance to peak shock loads, and high radial and axial overhung load support. Featuring carburized, hardened, and precision-ground helical gear sets, these units achieve dynamic transmission efficiency exceeding **96% per reduction stage**. Covering continuous torque output ratings from 100 Nm up to 10,000 Nm across 12 frame sizes (Size 32 to Size 180) and gear reduction ratios from 2.24:1 to 6,300:1 (in multi-stage and combined unit configurations). WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Rossi E Series gearmotors, replacement units, and exact technical model code decoding across global industrial plants.
