Renowned worldwide for engineering excellence, structural rigidity, and modular versatility, Rossi S.p.A. (Moog Group) delivers top-tier power transmission solutions. Explore our comprehensive catalog covering Rossi Coaxial Helical Gearmotors (E Series), Bevel-Helical & Parallel Shaft Gearboxes (G Series), Epicyclic Planetary Drives (EP Series), Worm Gearmotors (A Series), and Heavy Duty Extruder Gear Units. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Rossi equipment with full factory certifications and global express delivery.

Italian Engineering Standard — Rossi S.p.A.:
Rossi is an Italian pioneer in drive technology, celebrated for introducing single-piece monolithic cast-iron housings that maximize mechanical strength and torsional stiffness under severe operational duty.

Engineering Advantage & Technical Ecosystem:
• Monolithic Cast Iron Housings: Unmatched structural rigidity preventing gear deflection under heavy shock loads.
• Modular System (E, G, EP, A Series): Standardized dimensions and interchangeable motor mounts for rapid retrofitting and flexible installation.
• Extreme Industrial Torque: Heavy industrial helical and planetary gear units capable of output torques exceeding 3,000,000 Nm.
