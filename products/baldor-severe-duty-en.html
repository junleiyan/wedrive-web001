Baldor-Reliance NEMA Severe Duty & IEEE 841 Cast-Iron Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: NEMA Heavy-Duty Cast-Iron Process Motors (ECP / XCP Series: 1 HP to 500 HP)

The Baldor-Reliance NEMA Severe Duty & IEEE 841 Motor Series (featured model: ECP83770T-4 7.5 HP 213T 4-pole) represents North America's premier industrial motor platform built specifically for hostile process environments in chemical plants, oil refineries, paper mills, and mining operations. Engineered to meet or exceed rigorous **IEEE 841 standards** for vibration, thermal margin, and bearing longevity, these motors operate at **Super-E® NEMA Premium Efficiency** levels. Built with all-cast-iron frames, endshields, conduit boxes, and fan covers, they feature **InPRO/SEAL® IP56/IP66 bearing isolators** on both drive and non-drive ends, lead-free epoxy internal corrosion coatings, stainless steel hardware, and Class H ISR® (Inverter Spike Resistant) VFD-duty copper winding insulation. Covering output power ratings from 1 HP up to 500 HP across NEMA frame sizes 143T to 449T, Baldor severe duty motors provide complete physical interchangeability to replace legacy process motors in pumps, fans, compressors, blowers, and heavy conveyors.
