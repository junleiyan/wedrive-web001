WEG W50 High Voltage & Heavy Duty Induction Motors

Brand: WEG | Category: Medium & High Voltage Induction Motors (W50 Series: 75 kW to 1,250 kW, up to 6.6 kV)

The WEG W50 Series (featured model: W50 355M/U 250 kW 6.6 kV 4-Pole) is engineered specifically for severe-duty industrial applications requiring high power density, superior mechanical strength, and exceptional thermal management. Built with a rigid FC-200 cast-iron frame and advanced internal air-flow architecture, the W50 series minimizes vibration, reduces sound pressure levels, and delivers continuous performance under extreme environmental conditions. Spanning output ratings from 75 kW up to 1,250 kW in IEC frame sizes 315 to 450, the W50 accommodates low, medium, and high voltage supplies from 380 V up to 6,600 V (6.6 kV). Designed to meet IE3 and IE4 efficiency standards, the W50 is widely specified across heavy industry sectors for driving centrifugal pumps, large HVAC fans, axial blowers, screw compressors, ball mills, and heavy-duty conveyors.
