NORD SI50 UNIVERSAL Worm Gearmotor

Brand: NORD DRIVESYSTEMS | Series: UNIVERSAL SI / SMI (Aluminium Housing) & UNICASE Helical-Worm (SK 02040...42125)

The NORD SI50 is a highly compact, versatile right-angle worm gearmotor from NORD DRIVESYSTEMS' UNIVERSAL SI Series[cite: 24]. Featuring a smooth, corrosion-resistant die-cast aluminum housing[cite: 24], the SI50 offers high single-stage reduction ratios, quiet operation, and washdown suitability for light to medium industrial processing[cite: 24]. Available in complete mounting configurations—including hollow shaft keyway (SI50A), B5 flange (SI50AF), B14 face flange (SI50AZ), shrink disc (SI50ASH), GRIPMAXX™ keyless bushing system (SI50AM), and nsd tupH™ sealed surface treatment[cite: 24]—it is extensively applied in food & beverage processing, bottling plants, packaging equipment, and light material handling conveyors[cite: 24].
