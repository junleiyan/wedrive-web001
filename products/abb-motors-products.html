ABB Industrial Low Voltage Motors, High Voltage Motors & ACS Drives

Global Engineering Excellence — ABB Motion Control:
ABB is the global technology leader in electric motors and variable speed drives, offering unmatched reliability, IE3/IE4/IE5 energy efficiency, and extreme industrial durability.

Engineering Advantage & Cross-Series Compatibility:
• Low Voltage AC Motors: Heavy-duty Cast Iron M3BP Process Performance and Aluminum/Cast Iron M2BAX General Performance series covering 0.12 kW up to 1000 kW in IE2, IE3, and IE4 ultra-efficiency classes.
• High Voltage Motors: Modular NMI induction motors and rib-cooled AMI high voltage motors engineered for severe duty power plants, mining, and oil & gas operations up to 13.8 kV / 20 MW.
• ACS Frequency Inverters: Complete drive ecosystem including ACS380 machinery drives, ACS580 general purpose drives, and ACS880 industrial heavy-duty VFDs featuring direct torque control (DTC).
• WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original ABB equipment with factory certificate validation and fast global express delivery.
