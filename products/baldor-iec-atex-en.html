Baldor-Reliance IEC ATEX & IECEx Explosion-Proof Motors

Brand: Baldor-Reliance | Category: Metric IEC Hazardous Location Motors (IEC 63 to 355 Range: 0.12 kW to 355 kW)

The Baldor-Reliance IEC ATEX & IECEx Motor Series (featured model: M3JAA132SMA4 7.5 kW Ex d IIC T4 Gb) represents Baldor's international hazardous area motor portfolio, engineered specifically to satisfy European **ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU** and global **IECEx** explosion protection standards for metric dimensioned export machinery. Designed to combine North American heavy-duty manufacturing rigor with global IEC mounting compliance, these motors feature certified protection schemes including flameproof **Ex d / Ex db IIC T4 Gb** (Zone 1 and Zone 2 gas atmospheres), non-sparking **Ex ec IIC T3 Gc** (Zone 2), and dust-ignition proof **Ex tb / Ex tc IIIC T125°C Db** (Zone 21 and Zone 22). Operating at **IE2 High Efficiency and IE3 Premium Efficiency** levels, Baldor IEC ATEX motors feature rugged cast-iron or lightweight aluminum enclosures, IP55/IP66 washdown protection, PTC thermistor protection standard, and Class H VFD-rated copper winding insulation. Covering output power ratings from 0.12 kW up to 355 kW across IEC metric frame sizes 63 to 355, these motors deliver 100% direct mechanical alignment for international OEMs producing chemical process pumps, offshore skids, industrial mixers, and pharmaceutical centrifuges.
