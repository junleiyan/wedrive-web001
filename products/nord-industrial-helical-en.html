NORD SK 7207 MAXXDRIVE® H Industrial Helical Gear Unit

Brand: NORD DRIVESYSTEMS | Series: MAXXDRIVE® H Parallel Shaft Industrial Helical (SK 5207...11407)

The NORD SK 7207 is a heavy-duty, 2-stage parallel shaft industrial helical gearbox from NORD DRIVESYSTEMS' flagship MAXXDRIVE® H Series[cite: 22]. Engineered with NORD's single-piece UNICASE cast iron block housing[cite: 22], the SK 7207 delivers exceptional thermal dissipation capacity, extreme radial and axial load tolerance, and a nominal output torque capacity of 48,000 Nm[cite: 22]. Covering 11 frame sizes from SK 5207 to SK 11407 (15,000 Nm to 282,000 Nm)[cite: 22], MAXXDRIVE® H units support solid output shafts (V), hollow shafts with keyway (A) or shrink disc (S), B5 output flanges (F), torque arms (G), swing bases (SB), cooling fans, heat exchangers, mechanical backstops (RLS), and auxiliary maintenance drives[cite: 22]. They are heavily specified in mining conveyors, bucket wheel excavators, crushers, agitators, mixers, crane hoists, port cranes, and steel rolling mills[cite: 22].
