Festo Pneumatic Cylinders: DSBC, ADN/AEN, DFM/DGSL & DSNU/DGC Series

Brand: Festo | Core Standards: ISO 15552, ISO 21287, ISO 6432 | Global Festo Pneumatic Supplier
Precision Linear Motion & Actuator Engineering:
As a leading global Festo pneumatic supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED delivers the complete range of Festo linear actuators and guided drives. From high-speed clamping and pressing to ultra-compact assembly and long-stroke positioning, Festo pneumatic cylinders are engineered for maximum cycle life and zero-maintenance operation.

Core Actuator Families Covered on This Page:
• DSBC Series (ISO 15552 Profile Cylinders): Standard double-acting cylinders (Ø 32–125 mm) featuring Festo's patented self-adjusting pneumatic cushioning (PPS) that eliminates manual screw tuning and absorbs high kinetic energy smoothly.
• ADN / AEN Series (ISO 21287 Compact Cylinders): Ultra-compact double-acting (ADN) and single-acting (AEN) actuators (Ø 12–125 mm) requiring up to 50% less installation space than standard ISO cylinders, with integrated T-slots for SMT-8M sensors.
• DFM / DGSL Series (Twin-Piston Guided Drives & Mini Slides): High-precision linear actuators with integrated plain-bearing (GF) or recirculating ball-bearing (KF) guide rods for maximum torsional rigidity and lateral load absorption.
• DSNU / DGC Series (ISO 6432 Round & Rodless Linear Cylinders): Stainless-steel barrel round cylinders (Ø 8–63 mm) for fast-cycle tasks and space-saving rodless linear drives (DGC) with strokes up to 8,500 mm.

The Festo Pneumatic Cylinders portfolio provides unmatched versatility across automotive welding fixtures, packaging machines, electronics assembly, and food & beverage washdown lines. With integrated magnetic pistons for contactless position sensing and modular mounting accessories (foot, flange, clevis, trunnion), our engineering team verifies exact bore sizes, custom stroke lengths, piston rod threads, and cushioning variants to ensure 100% factory-compliant delivery.

Festo Cylinder Series Comparison: DSBC vs. ADN vs. DFM vs. DGC

Engineering selection guide to match load characteristics, mounting footprint, and stroke length with the optimal Festo linear actuator:

Series Family Structural Design Key Mechanical Advantage Recommended Operating Condition
DSBC Series Profile barrel with integrated sensor slots (ISO 15552) Self-adjusting PPS pneumatic cushioning automatically adapts to changing loads and speeds without manual adjustment. Standard linear pushing/pulling tasks with medium-to-long strokes and high cycle rates.
ADN / AEN Series Ultra-short housing compliant with ISO 21287 Up to 50% shorter overall length than ISO 15552 cylinders; available with non-rotating piston rod (ADN-...-Q) or through rod (ADN-...-S2). Compact jigs, fixtures, and multi-axis tooling where axial mounting space is strictly limited.
DFM / DGSL Series Integrated twin guide rods + drive cylinder in one housing Eliminates external guide rails; absorbs high lateral forces and torques directly on the yoke plate with high positioning repeatability. Vertical lifting, heavy stopper gates, cantilevered loads, and precision press-fit operations.
DSNU / DGC Series Round stainless barrel (ISO 6432) / Rodless carriage DSNU offers compact cylindrical mounting; DGC eliminates piston rod buckling over strokes up to 8.5 meters with 50% space savings. DSNU for light-duty high-speed sorting; DGC for long-travel linear transfer and packaging conveyors.
