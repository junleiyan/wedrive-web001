Siemens Beide 1TL0 General Purpose AC Motors

Brand: Siemens Beide / Innomotics | Series: 1TL0001 (IE2) & 1TL0003 (IE3) Range
Brand & Lineage Notice — Siemens Beide (西门子贝德):
The 1TL0 Series is the flagship low-voltage three-phase motor line manufactured by Siemens Motor (China) Co., Ltd. (SMC) under the iconic Siemens Beide (西门子贝德) brand[cite: 12]. Following Siemens' corporate carve-out of its low-voltage motors business, Beide products are now integrated into the global Innomotics portfolio[cite: 12].

Power Rating & Overseas Model Direct Replacement Note: Siemens Beide 1TL0 motors (1TL0001 IE2 and 1TL0003 IE3) comply strictly with global IEC standards[cite: 12]. They share 100% identical B3 foot mounting hole distances, B5/B14 flange pitch circles, and shaft dimensions with overseas Siemens models (such as 1LE1, 1LE5, legacy 1LG4, and 1LA7)[cite: 12]. Choosing domestic Siemens Beide 1TL0 motors allows direct 1:1 drop-in replacement across all corresponding power ratings (0.55 kW to 315 kW), drastically cutting procurement costs and lead times[cite: 12].

The Siemens Beide 1TL0 Series (1TL0001 IE2 High Efficiency and 1TL0003 IE3 Premium Efficiency) is designed for extreme reliability, high torque density, and cost-effective operation in industrial environments[cite: 12]. Engineered with high-rigidity cast iron frames, Class F insulation, IP55 protection, and low-vibration rotor assemblies covering IEC sizes 80M to 355L (0.55 kW to 315 kW)[cite: 12], 1TL0 motors are extensively deployed in HVAC fans, centrifugal water pumps, air compressors, conveyor systems, and general machine tools[cite: 12].
