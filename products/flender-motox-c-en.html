MOTOX C48-LA80M4 Helical Worm Gearmotor

Series: MOTOX Helical Worm Gear Units | Size C48 (2KJ1602 Order Code)[cite: 4]
Brand & Ownership Evolution Notice:
The iconic MOTOX gearmotor series was originally created and engineered by FLENDER[cite: 4]. Following Siemens' acquisition of Flender, MOTOX was integrated into Siemens' drive portfolio under the 2KJ catalog line (2KJ1602 for C48)[cite: 4]. Subsequently, Siemens carved out its heavy-duty motors and gearmotors business under the INNOMOTICS brand[cite: 4]. Today, MOTOX products have transitioned back to the original manufacturing plants and authentic OEM supplier channels, ensuring 100% exact design continuity, mechanical interchangeability, and original quality standards[cite: 4].

The MOTOX C48-LA80M4 is a 2-stage right-angle helical worm gearmotor combining a high-efficiency primary helical gearing stage with a heavy-duty worm gear output set[cite: 4]. Cataloged under Siemens 2KJ order code 2KJ1602[cite: 4], the C48 size delivers nominal output torques up to 260 Nm and is paired with 0.55 kW LA80M4 motors[cite: 4]. Designed for quiet running, space-saving installation, and elevated mechanical efficiency over standard pure worm reducers[cite: 4], MOTOX C series gearmotors are available in versatile configurations (foot C48, hollow shaft CA48, flange CF48/CAF48, shrink disc CAD48, and housing flange CZ48)[cite: 4] ideal for conveyor lines, stage machinery, food packaging, and water treatment drives[cite: 4].
