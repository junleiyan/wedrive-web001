Samson Type 3241 Pneumatic Globe Control Valves

Brand: SAMSON AG | Category: Standard Single-Stage Globe Control Valves (Size: DN 15 to DN 300 | Pressure: PN 10 to PN 40 / Class 150 to 300)

The Samson Type 3241 Globe Control Valve Series (featured model: Type 3241 DN 50 PN 40 with Type 3277 Actuator) is SAMSON AG's world-leading control valve platform, engineered specifically for high-precision process control, thermal management, and reliable fluid regulation in chemical plants, oil refineries, food pasteurization systems, district steam loops, and pharmaceutical manufacturing lines. Built with a top-entry, cast-steel (1.0619 / A216 WCB), stainless steel (1.4408 / A351 CF8M), or forged body, Type 3241 valves feature **exchangeable plug and seat trim sets** that can be replaced without removing the valve body from the pipeline. To handle aggressive, toxic, or high-temperature media, the valve can be equipped with an extended bonnet or a **double-wall stainless steel bellows seal** to guarantee zero fugitive stem emissions. Actuated by compact **Type 3271 or Type 3277 multi-spring pneumatic actuators** and integrated **Type 3730 smart positioners**, Type 3241 valves deliver exceptional rangeability (up to 50:1), tight shut-off options (Class IV to Class VI), and low hysteresis across sizes from **DN 15 to DN 300 (1/2" to 12")** with Kvs values from **0.1 to 1000**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, Kvs flow calculations, fail-safe spring matching, and global express supply for Samson Type 3241 control valve assemblies worldwide.
