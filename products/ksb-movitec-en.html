KSB Movitec Vertical Multistage High-Pressure Pumps

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: Vertical Multistage High-Pressure Centrifugal Pumps (Flow: Up to 160 m³/h | Head: Up to 400 m / 40 bar)

The KSB Movitec Vertical Multistage High-Pressure Pump Series (encompassing standard **Movitec V** and integrated variable-speed **Movitec VE** models, featured model: Movitec V 15-06) is KSB's advanced fluid handling platform engineered for demanding high-pressure industrial applications including boiler feed water, high-pressure washing systems, reverse osmosis water treatment, fire-fighting pressure boosting, irrigation, and HVAC water booster sets. Designed with a compact vertical inline construction, Movitec pumps allow direct installation into straight pipe runs, saving valuable floor space. Constructed with laser-welded high-grade stainless steel impellers, diffusers, and stage casings (**AISI 304 / 1.4301** for standard media, **AISI 316L / 1.4404** for aggressive chemicals), Movitec pumps feature easy-access **cartridge mechanical seals** that reduce maintenance down-time without removing the electric motor. Driven by standardized IEC flange motors (up to **45 kW**, compatible with high-efficiency IE3/IE4 motors and magnetless SuPremE® IE5 synchronous motors) and optional **PumpDrive VFDs**, Movitec pumps deliver discharge pressures up to **40 bar (400 meters total head)** and capacities up to **160 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, stage calculations, mechanical seal matching, and global express supply for KSB Movitec high-pressure multistage pumps worldwide.
