Rossi Heavy Duty Extruder Gear Units & Drives

Brand: Rossi S.p.A. | Category: Single-Screw Plastics & Rubber Extruder Gearboxes (Size 100 to 451 Range: 3,000 Nm to 150,000 Nm)

The Rossi Extruder Heavy Duty Gear Unit Platform (featured model: MR 2I 140 FO1A 1:14 Ratio) is Rossi's specialized heavy-duty drive series, engineered specifically to handle the extreme axial thrust loads, high thermal stresses, and continuous continuous torque demands of plastics, polymers, and rubber single-screw extrusion machinery. Built around Rossi's signature **monolithic (single-piece) cast-iron housing**, these extruder gearboxes feature an integrated housing nose housing oversized **spherical roller thrust bearings (294.. series)** that absorb continuous axial screw reaction forces directly through the gearbox frame without transferring stress to the gear mesh or motor. Utilizing case-hardened, precision-ground helical gear sets, Rossi extruder gearboxes deliver maximum fatigue strength and quiet operation. Covering output torque capacity from 3,000 Nm up to 150,000 Nm across 12 frame sizes (Size 100 to Size 451) and gear ratios from 6.3:1 to 28:1. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED supplies 100% original Rossi extruder gear units, forced lubrication systems, oil cooling units, and technical replacement support across global extrusion facilities.
