TR-Electronic Absolute Linear Measurement: LA46 & LP46 Series

Brand: TR-Electronic | Core Models: LA-46, LP-46 | Global TR Encode Supplier
Wear-Free Magnetostrictive Precision:
As a leading TR absolute encoders supplier, WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides authentic TR-Electronic linear measurement systems. The TR LA46 and LP46 series rely on highly accurate, non-contact magnetostrictive technology to measure linear displacement without mechanical wear, guaranteeing lifelong reliability in harsh industrial environments.

Engineering Excellence for Linear Positioning:
• Rod & Profile Architectures: The LA-46 rod-style sensors are designed to withstand high pressures, making them ideal for direct integration into hydraulic cylinders. The LP-46 profile-style sensors feature a flat aluminum extrusion for easy mounting on automated machine axes and guide rails.
• Absolute Position Feedback: No homing routines required. The exact linear position is available instantly upon power-up, significantly improving safety and startup times for injection molding machines and woodworking equipment.
• Multi-Interface Capability: Fully compatible with modern control architectures, offering native support for Analog (4-20mA, 0-10V), SSI, CANopen, PROFINET, and EtherCAT.

The TR Encoders LA46 & LP46 Linear Series provide exceptional durability and precision for critical automation tasks. Because the position magnet travels freely over the sensor rod or profile without physical contact, the system is entirely immune to dust, oil, and continuous vibrations. Whether you are replacing a faulty sensor in a heavy hydraulic press or outfitting a new continuous casting line, our technical sourcing team ensures you receive the exact measurement length and interface required.

TR-Electronic Linear Mechanical Topologies

Engineering comparison between the two primary physical forms of the 46-series linear sensors:

Mechanical Topology Series Designation Environmental Resistance Ideal Installation Use Case
Rod Style (Cylinder Mount) LA-46, LA-66 Extreme pressure resistance (350+ bar). Encapsulated stainless steel rod withstands hydraulic fluids. Direct insertion into the bore of a hydraulic cylinder. The position magnet is mounted onto the piston head for internal stroke measurement.
Profile Style (Surface Mount) LP-46, LMP-30 High tolerance to industrial dust, dirt, and vibrations. Flat extrusion is easy to clean. Mounted externally alongside linear machine axes, guide rails, or injection molding clamping units using simple brackets.
Free-floating Magnet Available on both Eliminates mechanical wear completely. Sensor immune to slight misalignments. Applications running 24/7 where traditional contact potentiometers would wear out and cause downtime.
