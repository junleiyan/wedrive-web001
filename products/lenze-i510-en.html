Lenze i510 Cabinet Frequency Inverters

Brand: Lenze SE | Category: Compact Control Cabinet Frequency Inverters (0.25 kW to 15.0 kW Range)

The Lenze i510 Cabinet Inverter Series (featured model: i51AE175C1000000S 0.75 kW 3-Phase 400V) is Lenze's streamlined, ultra-compact variable frequency drive platform, engineered for space-restricted control cabinets and essential speed-control tasks. Measuring as narrow as 60 mm in width, i510 inverters offer high power density, easy side-by-side mounting, and intuitive commissioning via pluggable memory modules or optional USB/WLAN diagnostic interfaces. Covering output power ratings from 0.25 kW to 15.0 kW across 1-phase 230V and 3-phase 400V/480V grid supplies, the i510 supports V/f linear and quadratic motor control, Modbus RTU, and CANopen communication. Engineered for exceptional reliability, Lenze i510 inverters serve as the direct, modernized replacement for legacy **Lenze 8200 Vector** and **Lenze 8400 BaseLine** inverters across pumps, fans, belt conveyors, and packaging equipment.
