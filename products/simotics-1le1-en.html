Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS 1LE1 Low-Voltage AC Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS GP (1LE10) & SIMOTICS SD (1LE15 / 1LE16)
Global Heritage & Brand Transition Notice — Siemens to Innomotics:
The SIMOTICS 1LE1 platform represents Siemens' flagship European and global low-voltage three-phase asynchronous motor series. Following Siemens' corporate carve-out of its large and low-voltage motor business, the entire 1LE1 product portfolio is now manufactured and supplied under the independent brand identity Innomotics.

Engineering Consistency & Sourcing Value:
• 100% Unchanged Manufacturing Quality: Built in the established European factories (Czech Republic and Germany), Innomotics 1LE1 motors preserve identical winding materials, terminal box layouts, dynamic balancing, and IEC 60034 test standards.
• Dual Material Execution: SIMOTICS GP 1LE10 features lightweight aluminum enclosures (IEC 80 to 200), while SIMOTICS SD 1LE15 / 1LE16 features ultra-rugged cast-iron casings (IEC 71 to 315) engineered for aggressive chemical, marine, and process industrial environments.
• 100% Direct Retrofit for Legacy 1LA & 1LG: 1LE1 is the direct drop-in successor to legacy Siemens 1LA7, 1LA9, 1LG4, and 1LG6 motors, ensuring identical shaft centerlines, foot bolt centers, and flange diameters with substantially higher efficiency (IE3 / IE4).

The Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS 1LE1 Series provides unmatched operational reliability, world-class electrical efficiency, and modular flexibility across global industrial networks. Designed for direct online (DOL) operation and variable frequency drive (VFD) inverter duty, 1LE1 motors are supplied in 2, 4, 6, and 8 poles covering 0.55 kW to 250 kW. Equipped with DURIGNIT IR 2000 Class F insulation (Class B temperature rise), IP55/IP65 protection, and optimized thermal dissipate ribs, 1LE1 motors are specified worldwide for heavy pumps, continuous ventilation fans, industrial compressors, mixers, and conveyor drive stations.
