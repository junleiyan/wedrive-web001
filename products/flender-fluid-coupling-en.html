Flender FLUDEX FAD 450 Hydrodynamic Fluid Coupling

Brand: Flender GmbH | Series: FLUDEX Hydrodynamic Soft-Start Couplings (Size 222 to 840)

The Flender FLUDEX FAD 450 is a benchmark hydrodynamic fluid coupling operating on the wear-free Föttinger principle, where input and output components transmit power via fluid kinetic movement without mechanical contact[cite: 21]. Featuring an integrated delay chamber (Type FAD)[cite: 21], the FLUDEX FAD 450 delivers soft, shock-free starting of heavy masses, protects motors from high starting current peaks, and provides automatic overload protection across heavy industrial drivetrains[cite: 21]. Available in 15 standard sizes (sizes 222 to 840) covering power transmission from 1.2 kW up to 2,500 kW[cite: 21], FLUDEX couplings offer flexible configurations with basic designs (FAO), delay chambers (FAD/FGD), extended delay chambers (FVO), integrated brake discs/drums (FADB/FNDB), and attached N-EUPEX or ARPEX flexible couplings[cite: 21]. They are extensively specified in overland belt conveyors, bucket elevators, ball mills, crushers, centrifuges, and industrial agitators[cite: 21].
