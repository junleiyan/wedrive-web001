Bauer BS Series Helical Worm Geared Motors

Brand: Bauer Gear Motor | Category: Helical Worm Right-Angle Geared Motors (BS02 to BS40 Range)

The Bauer BS Series (featured model: BS10-31/D08MA4 0.55 kW IE3) is the benchmark economical right-angle helical worm geared motor from Bauer Gear Motor, setting the global standard for compact right-angle installation, high single-stage reduction ratios, and ultra-quiet acoustic performance. Featuring standard IP65 total enclosure protection (IP66, IP67, and IP68 submersed protection optional), BS series gearmotors are completely dust-tight and resistant to high-pressure water washdown. Engineered with proprietary compact right-angle dimensions, hollow output shaft bore options, and specialized rubber-buffered torque arm assemblies, Bauer BS units offer unmatched irreplaceability in light conveyors, stage machinery, packaging equipment, and food processing lines. Covering motor power ratings from 0.03 kW to 5.5 kW across 8 frame sizes (BS02 to BS40), the BS series seamlessly integrates with modern **IE3 Premium Efficiency motors** (D08 to D11) to replace legacy Bauer S series and older IE1/IE2 motors in severe industrial environments.
