MOTOX D88-LA100L4 Coaxial Helical Gearmotor

Series: MOTOX Coaxial Helical Gear Units | Size D88 (2KJ1205 Order Code)[cite: 24]
Brand & Ownership Evolution Notice:
The iconic MOTOX gearmotor series was originally created and engineered by FLENDER[cite: 24]. Following Siemens' acquisition of Flender, MOTOX was integrated into Siemens' drive portfolio under the 2KJ catalog line (2KJ1205 for D88)[cite: 24]. Subsequently, Siemens carved out its heavy-duty motors and gearmotors business under the INNOMOTICS brand[cite: 24]. Today, MOTOX products have transitioned back to the original manufacturing plants and authentic OEM supplier channels, ensuring 100% exact design continuity, mechanical interchangeability, and original quality standards[cite: 24].

The MOTOX D88-LA100L4 is a 3-stage coaxial inline helical gearmotor offering high mechanical efficiency (94%), rigid cast iron housing construction, and compact inline layout[cite: 24]. Cataloged under Siemens 2KJ order code 2KJ1205[cite: 24], the D88 size delivers nominal output torques up to 1,600 Nm and is paired with 1.5 kW LA100L4 motors[cite: 24]. Designed for heavy industrial power transmission, conveyor drives, agitators, and roller tables[cite: 24], MOTOX coaxial helical gearmotors are available in versatile configurations (foot D88, B5 flange DF88, B14 flange DZ88, and agitator flange DR88)[cite: 24] ensuring high overhung load capacities and extended service life[cite: 24].
