Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS XP 1MB5 Explosion-Proof Motors

Brand: Siemens / Innomotics | Series: SIMOTICS XP (1MB55, 1MB58 Heavy-Duty Flameproof Range)
Heavy-Duty Hazardous Area Lineage — Siemens to Innomotics:
The SIMOTICS XP 1MB5 platform represents Siemens' flagship heavy-frame explosion-proof low-voltage motor line engineered specifically for large-capacity installations in hazardous gas and dust environments. Following Siemens' corporate carve-out, the entire 1MB5 hazardous area motor family is now engineered, certified, and manufactured under the independent brand identity Innomotics.

Engineering Protection & Global Safety Certification:
• Maximum Safety in Extreme Zones: Fully certified under international directives including ATEX, IECEx, and China Ex (CCC-Ex) for Zone 1 (Ex db / Ex db eb), Zone 2 (Ex ec), Zone 21 (Ex tb), and Zone 22 (Ex tc).
• High Power Output Across Large Frames: Built on heavy-duty grey cast iron frames (IEC sizes 315, 355, 400, and 450) covering rated capacities from 200 kW up to 1,000 kW and beyond, filling the critical high-power niche in petrochemical refineries, gas transport pipelines, offshore drilling platforms, and underground mining operations.
• Direct Retrofit for Legacy Flameproof Lines: Serves as the modern, high-efficiency (IE3 / IE4) direct replacement for legacy Siemens 1MA8, 1MJ8, and heavy 1LG6 explosion-proof motors, preserving mounting footprints while drastically reducing thermal stress and operational energy costs.

The Siemens & Innomotics SIMOTICS XP 1MB5 Series is built to provide infallible safety, superior explosion containment, and continuous duty in explosive atmospheres classified under Temperature Classes T1 to T4 and Gas Groups IIA, IIB, and IIC. Designed for direct online (DOL) operation and variable frequency drive (VFD) converter duty up to 690V, 1MB5 motors incorporate reinforced DURIGNIT IR 2000 Class F insulation with Class B temperature rise (80K reserve), flameproof cast-iron terminal boxes, certified pressure-resistant enclosures, standard Pt100 RTDs in stator windings and bearing housings, and IP65/IP66 ingress protection. They are specified worldwide for heavy refinery crude pumps, offshore gas compressors, hydrogen transfer blowers, and large industrial mixers.
