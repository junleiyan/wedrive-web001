Sumitomo Cyclo® 6000 Speed Reducers & Geared Motors

Brand: Sumitomo Drive Technologies | Category: Cycloidal Speed Reducers (Frame 6090 to 6275 Range: 100 Nm to 68,000 Nm)

The Sumitomo Cyclo® 6000 Speed Reducer Series (featured model: CNHM2-6125-59 1.5 kW 1:59 Ratio) is Sumitomo's flagship cycloidal drive platform, engineered for severe industrial applications requiring extreme reliability, high torque density, and ultimate shock-load resistance. Operating on smooth **rolling contact between cycloidal discs and stationary pin gears**—rather than sliding gear tooth shear stress—Cyclo 6000 reducers withstand **momentary shock loads up to 500%** without structural tooth breakage. Constructed with high-rigidity cast-iron housings, precision-ground bearing steel components, and high thermal capacity lubrication sumps, Cyclo 6000 units achieve single reduction ratios from 6:1 to 87:1, double reduction up to 7,569:1, and multi-stage reduction exceeding 1,000,000:1. Available in foot-mounted (CNHM / CHHM), flange-mounted (CVMW / CVVM), and shaft-mounted configurations with C-face or integral motor options. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, model decoding, and 100% direct mechanical replacement support for legacy **Sumitomo Cyclo 3000 and Cyclo 4000** speed reducers.
