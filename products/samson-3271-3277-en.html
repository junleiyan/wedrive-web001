Samson Type 3271 & Type 3277 Pneumatic Diaphragm Actuators

Brand: SAMSON AG | Category: Multi-Spring Rolling Diaphragm Control Valve Actuators (Diaphragm Area: 120 cm² to 2800 cm² | Stroke: Up to 120 mm)

The Samson Type 3271 and Type 3277 Pneumatic Actuator Series (featured model: Type 3277 350cm² with direct-mount positioner interface) represents SAMSON AG's world-leading actuation technology engineered for linear globe, angle, and 3-way control valves across chemical processing, power plants, oil refineries, food pasteurization, and district heating loops[cite: 12]. Constructed with a low-friction **rolling diaphragm** and precision multi-spring assembly, these actuators exhibit extremely low hysteresis and instant response to minute pneumatic signal changes[cite: 12]. The **Type 3277 variant** is specially designed with an internal air duct connecting directly to Type 3730 or Type 3725 positioners, eliminating external piping and protecting air connections from mechanical damage and corrosive ambient weather[cite: 12]. Both Type 3271 and Type 3277 actuators feature field-reversible fail-safe action (**Fail-Close / Actuator stem extends**, or **Fail-Open / Actuator stem retracts**) by swapping spring configurations or air connection ports[cite: 12]. Available across diaphragm areas from **120 cm² to 2800 cm²** (and tandem arrangements up to 2x2800 cm²) providing thrust forces over **100 kN**[cite: 12]. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, thrust force calculations, spring range selection, and global express supply for Samson Type 3271 and 3277 actuators worldwide[cite: 12].
