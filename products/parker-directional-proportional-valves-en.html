2. Parker D1FP & D3FP VCD Direct-Operated Proportional Valves

Powered by Parker's patented Voice Coil Drive (VCD) linear force motor technology, the Parker D1FP (NG06 / CETOP 3) and D3FP (NG10 / CETOP 5) proportional directional valves bridge the performance gap between conventional proportional valves and high-cost servo valves. Unlike standard proportional solenoids, the VCD actuator generates high driving forces in both directions without relying on return springs, eliminating spool sticking and achieving true servo-valve dynamics.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) – Parker Valves & Gear Pumps

Q1: What is the difference between Parker D1FP/D3FP VCD valves and D41F/D91F/DFplus proportional valves?

D1FP (NG06) and D3FP (NG10) are direct-operated proportional valves driven by Parker's Voice Coil Drive (VCD) linear motor, offering ultra-fast response (<3.5 ms) for flows up to 100 l/min. D41F/D41FP (NG16), D91F/D91FP (NG25), and D111FP (NG32) are two-stage pilot-operated valves designed for high flow capacities up to 1,000 l/min while retaining closed-loop spool position control.

Q2: Can you replace discontinued Commercial Intertech / Commercial Hydraulics P30, P50, or P75 gear pumps?

Yes. Parker Hannifin acquired Commercial Intertech and transitioned the P30, P50, and P75 cast-iron gear pumps directly into the Parker PGP330, PGP350, and PGP365 series. Send us your existing pump tag code and our engineers will match the exact gear width, shaft spline, mounting flange, and port threads.

Q3: Do you supply replacement solenoid coils, connectors, and OEM seal kits for D1VW, D1FP, and PGP series?

Yes. Alongside complete valves and pumps, we supply genuine Parker replacement solenoid coils (24VDC, 110VAC, 230VAC), 6+PE and M12 mating plugs, Viton (FKM) and Buna-N (NBR) seal kits, and PGP/PGM gear pump overhaul kits with fast global air courier delivery.
