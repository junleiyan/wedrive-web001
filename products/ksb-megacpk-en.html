KSB MegaCPK Heavy-Duty Chemical Process Pumps

Brand: KSB SE & Co. KGaA | Category: ISO 5199 / ISO 2858 Standardized Chemical Process Pumps (Flow: Up to 1,160 m³/h | Head: Up to 162 m)

The KSB MegaCPK Chemical Process Pump Series (featured model: MegaCPK 040-025-200) is KSB's benchmark heavy-duty process handling platform, engineered strictly to **ISO 5199** and **ISO 2858** international standards for aggressive, volatile, toxic, and hot fluid handling in chemical plants, oil refineries, petrochemical processing, fertilizer manufacturing, and biofuel production lines. Driven by standardized IEC motors (compatible with explosion-proof ATEX motors and energy-saving IE3/IE4 drives), MegaCPK pumps feature optimized hydraulic flow channels and reinforced bearing brackets that minimize shaft deflection and mechanical stress under thermal shock. Available in specialized corrosion-resistant alloys including **Noridur 1.4593**, Duplex stainless steel (1.4517), Hastelloy C, and cast steel (1.0619). The enlarged seal chamber accommodates standardized single, double, and cartridge mechanical seals compliant with **API 682 flushing plans**. Operating reliably across discharge sizes from **DN 25 to DN 250** with motor outputs up to **315 kW**, achieving total dynamic heads up to **162 meters** and discharge capacities up to **1,160 m³/h**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, metallurgy selection, mechanical seal flushing plan engineering, and global express supply for KSB MegaCPK chemical process pumps worldwide.
