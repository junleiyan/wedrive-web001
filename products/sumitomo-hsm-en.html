Sumitomo HSM Shaft Mounted Helical Speed Reducers

Brand: Sumitomo Drive Technologies | Category: Shaft Mounted Helical Reducers (HSM 107 to HSM 315 Range: 1,000 Nm to 42,000 Nm)

The Sumitomo HSM Shaft Mounted Helical Speed Reducer Series (featured model: HSM207-25 25:1 Ratio Shaft Mount) is Sumitomo's heavy-duty shaft-mounted drive platform, engineered specifically for direct mounting onto driven equipment shafts in severe bulk material handling, aggregate processing, grain elevators, and mining belt conveyors. By eliminating foundation bedplates, couplings, and complex alignment procedures, HSM reducers deliver compact, high-torque power transmission directly at the head pulley shaft. Built with case-hardened, precision ground helical gearing and rugged cast-iron housings, HSM reducers offer standard nominal reduction ratios of **5:1, 14:1, 20:1, and 25:1**. Output shaft connection features Sumitomo's patented **Taper-Grip® keyless bushing system**—allowing easy installation and removal without fretting corrosion—as well as standard keyed hollow shafts. Equipped with integrated backstop options to prevent conveyor reversal, heavy-duty double-lip seals, and high overhung load capabilities, Sumitomo HSM reducers deliver a 100% direct mechanical replacement for legacy shaft-mounted gearboxes across global processing plants.

Expert Technical Sourcing & Shaft Mount System Integration Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in technical sourcing, exact ratio decoding, and global express supply for the complete Sumitomo HSM Shaft Mounted Helical Speed Reducer portfolio. Whether your material handling application requires standard 25:1 or 20:1 double reduction units (HSM107, HSM115, HSM203, HSM207, HSM215), 5:1 single reduction units, Taper-Grip® keyless bushing kits, internal mechanical backstops, or screw conveyor drive adaptors, all units can be supplied pre-configured directly from factory channels.
