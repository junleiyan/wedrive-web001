Hansen M4KB Bevel-Helical Industrial Gearbox

Brand: Hansen (Sumitomo Drive Technologies) | Series: Hansen M4KB / P4 Industrial Gearboxes (Size 10 to 19)

The Hansen M4KB is a line of heavy-duty bevel-helical (right-angle) industrial gearboxes engineered for demanding industrial applications requiring high torque transmission capability, absolute reliability, and optimized thermal performance. As part of the prestigious Hansen M4 / P4 product family, the M4KB model combines high-precision spiral bevel gear sets with high-quality helical gears, delivering smooth operation, extremely low energy losses, and long service life. Its robust casing construction and superior heat dissipation design allow the Hansen M4KB to operate stably in harsh environments such as cooling towers, mining conveyors, chemical agitators, cement/clay mills, and wastewater treatment plants.
