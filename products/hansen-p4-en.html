Hansen P4 Heavy Industrial Gearboxes & Reducers

Brand: Hansen Industrial Transmissions | Category: Heavy Duty Parallel & Bevel-Helical Industrial Gearboxes (19 Sizes: up to 800,000 Nm Torque)

The Hansen P4 Heavy Industrial Gearbox Series (featured model: P4-PND3-R42 Parallel 3-Stage Helical) represents Hansen Industrial Transmissions' flagship multi-megawatt drive platform, engineered for severe continuous-duty industrial operations in mining, water aeration, paper pulping, cement manufacturing, and heavy material handling conveyors. Built with a rigid single-piece **carcass housing design** that provides optimal load distribution and low sound emission levels, Hansen P4 gearboxes incorporate precision-carburized helical and bevel-helical gear tooth geometry. Featuring the patented **Carpex oil-tight sealing system** to prevent environmental contamination and oil leakage, these units offer superior thermal ratings supported by axial cooling fans, internal oil lubrication circuits, and water-cooling options. Available in **Parallel Shaft (2, 3, and 4-stage helical)** and **Right-Angle Shaft (2, 3, and 4-stage bevel-helical)** configurations across 19 modular frame sizes (P4 Size 19 to Size 26), covering torque ratings up to **800,000 Nm** and gear ratios from 1.25:1 to 630:1. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio calculations, and 100% compliant mechanical replacement support for legacy **Hansen Power5 and Hansen HPP** industrial gearboxes.

Expert Technical Sourcing & Industrial System Integration Notes

WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED specializes in technical sourcing, ratio calculations, and global express supply for the complete Hansen P4 Heavy Industrial Gearbox portfolio.
