Neugart WPLN & WPSN Precision Right-Angle Planetary Gearboxes

Brand: Neugart GmbH | Category: Precision Right-Angle Bevel-Helical Planetary Gearheads (Frame 070 to 190 Range: 27 Nm to 1,800 Nm)

The Neugart WPLN & WPSN Precision Right-Angle Planetary Series (featured model: WPLN090 1-Stage 1:5 Ratio) is Neugart's flagship precision 90° drive system, engineered specifically for high-end CNC machine tools, robotics, printing machinery, and dynamic servo axes where space is severely restricted. By pairing a high-precision **spiral bevel hypoid input stage with precision ground helical planetary gearing**, the WPLN (round output flange) and WPSN (square output flange) series eliminate axial motor projection while delivering maximum positioning accuracy and whisper-quiet running. Equipped with preloaded tapered roller bearings, these gearheads absorb extreme radial (up to 21,500 N) and axial loads, providing unyielding tipping rigidity under high dynamic start-stop cycles. Offering ultra-low torsional backlash (**<3 to <5 arcmin**) and dynamic efficiency up to 96%, WPLN/WPSN gearboxes are available across 5 modular frame sizes (070, 090, 115, 142, 190) covering ratios from 3:1 to 100:1 with output torques up to **1,800 Nm**. WE DRIVE HONGKONG LIMITED provides technical sourcing, ratio verification, and custom PCS motor adapter selection for Neugart WPLN & WPSN precision right-angle planetary gearboxes worldwide.
