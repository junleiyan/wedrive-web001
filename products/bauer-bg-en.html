Bauer BG Series Coaxial Helical Geared Motors

Brand: Bauer Gear Motor | Category: Coaxial Inline Helical Geared Motors (BG06 to BG100 Range)

The Bauer BG Series (featured model: BG30-34/D09SA4 0.75 kW IE3) is the flagship coaxial inline helical geared motor from Bauer Gear Motor, setting the global standard for mechanical durability, energy efficiency, and extreme environmental sealing. Featuring standard IP65 total enclosure protection (IP66, IP67, and IP68 submersed protection optional), BG series gearmotors are completely dust-tight and resistant to high-pressure water jets. Engineered with proprietary compact axial dimensions and specialized foot/flange bolt patterns, Bauer BG units offer unmatched irreplaceability in legacy OEM equipment. Covering motor power ratings from 0.03 kW to 75 kW across 11 frame sizes (BG06 to BG100), the BG series seamlessly integrates with modern **IE3 Premium Efficiency motors** (D09 to D18) to replace legacy Bauer G series and older IE1/IE2 motors in cranes, wastewater clarifiers, roller conveyors, and food washdown facilities.
