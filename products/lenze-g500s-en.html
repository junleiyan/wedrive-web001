Lenze g500-S Shaft-Mounted Parallel Helical Geared Motors

Brand: Lenze SE | Category: Shaft-Mounted Parallel Shaft Geared Motors (g500-S130 to g500-S1600 Range)

The Lenze g500-S Series (featured model: g500-S400/m550-P90L4 1.5 kW IE3) is Lenze's slim-line shaft-mounted parallel helical geared motor platform, engineered for narrow installation spaces, high overhung radial loads, and direct hollow-shaft machine mounting. Achieving gear transmission efficiency up to 94% to 96%, g500-S gearboxes feature lightweight aluminum housing construction (g500-S130, S220, S400) and rigid cast iron housings (g500-S660, S1600), covering output torques from 130 Nm to 1,600 Nm. Matched with modern **Lenze m550 IE3 Premium Efficiency Three-Phase Motors** (or Lenze Smart Motors), g500-S shaft-mounted geared motors provide 100% direct 1-to-1 mechanical compatibility to replace legacy **Lenze GFA Series** gearmotors in overhead crane hoists, belt feeders, agitators, and material handling conveyors.
