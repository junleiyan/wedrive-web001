Kollmorgen Cartridge DDR Direct Drive Rotary Servo Motors

Brand: Kollmorgen | Category: Self-Contained Zero-Backlash Direct Drive Rotary Motors (C04 to C13 Range: 4.5 Nm to 510 Nm)

The Kollmorgen Cartridge DDR (C-DDR) Servo Motor Series (featured model: C052A-11-1100 22 Nm 700 RPM) is an innovative motion control solution that combines the extraordinary performance advantages of a frameless direct drive rotary motor with the effortless installation of a standard factory-assembled servo motor. By mounting directly onto the machine load shaft using a patented compression coupling, Cartridge DDR motors completely **eliminate mechanical gearboxes, timing belts, pulleys, and flexible couplings**, achieving true **zero backlash, zero compliance, and maintenance-free operation**. Featuring factory-integrated high-precision bearings and high-resolution optical / sine-cosine feedback encoders (up to 2,097,152 counts/rev), C-DDR motors deliver continuous stall torque output from 4.5 Nm up to 510 Nm across 5 distinct frame sizes (C04, C05, C06, C09, C13). Optimized specifically for seamless autotuning with **Kollmorgen AKD and AKD2G digital servo drive amplifiers**, Cartridge DDR motors serve as the ideal direct-drive replacement for mechanical rotary indexing tables, printing press cylinders, semiconductor wafer handlers, and high-precision packaging machinery.
