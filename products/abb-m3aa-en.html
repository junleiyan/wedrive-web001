ABB M3AA Process Performance Aluminum Low Voltage Motors

Brand: ABB | Category: Process Performance Aluminum Three-Phase Motors (M3AA 56 to 280 Range: 0.06 kW to 90 kW)

The ABB M3AA Motor Series (featured model: M3AA 132SMA4 7.5 kW IE3) is ABB's premium Process Performance aluminum low voltage AC motor platform, engineered for applications where low weight, high corrosion resistance, and superior thermal conductivity are critical. Constructed from high-grade aluminum alloy with removable feet, IP55 protection, and Class F insulation (Class B temperature rise margin), M3AA motors fully satisfy **IEC 60034-30-1 IE2, IE3 Premium, and IE4 Super Premium Efficiency** classes. Covering power ratings from 0.06 kW to 90 kW across 2-pole, 4-pole, 6-pole, and 8-pole configurations (IEC frame sizes 56 to 280), M3AA motors incorporate VFD-rated winding insulation for frequency inverter operation with **ABB ACS380, ACS580, and ACS880 drives**. Designed for high adaptability, ABB M3AA aluminum motors serve as the direct 1-to-1 mechanical replacement for legacy **ABB M2AA** aluminum motors in marine machinery, food & beverage washdown equipment, HVAC air handlers, medical devices, and mobile industrial OEM equipment.
