SAMSON VALVES & AUTOMATION

SAMSON Control Valves & Industry Application Solutions

Precision engineered Globe Valves, Rotary Valves, Self-operated Regulators, and Smart Digital Positioners for critical process automation.

Chemicals, Petrochemicals & Industrial Gases

High-precision control under corrosive media, extreme temperatures, and cryogenics. SAMSON provides modular globe control valves and SIL-certified intelligent positioners to ensure maximum process uptime and leak-tight operation.

Featured Products: SAMSON Type 3241 Globe Valve, Type 3251 Severe Service Valve, TROVIS 3730 Positioner

Power, Energy, Oil & Gas

Engineered for severe service applications including high-pressure boiler feed steam, gas pipeline regulation, and off-shore production, featuring noise-reduction trims and anti-cavitation valve designs.

Featured Products: SAMSON Type 3254 Severe Service Valve, VETEC Rotary Plug Valves, LEUSER High-Pressure Valves

Pharmaceuticals, Biotech, Food & Beverages

Ultra-clean, FDA-compliant sanitary control solutions for sterile processing. High-purity aseptic diaphragm valves and sterile angle seat valves supporting CIP/SIP cleaning cycles.

Featured Products: SAMSON SED Aseptic Diaphragm Valves, Type 3510 Micro-flow Valve, Type 3321 Food Grade Valve

District Energy & Building Automation

Energy-efficient HVAC and district heating/cooling fluid control. SAMSON self-operated temperature and pressure regulators deliver maintenance-free, non-electric automatic balancing.

Featured Products: SAMSON Type 44-1 B Pressure Regulator, Type 1 2 4 Self-operated Temperature Regulators, TROVIS Electric Actuators

Metallurgy, Mining, Pulp & Paper

Designed for handling abrasive slurries, fiber suspensions, and harsh chemical liquors. Wear-resistant ceramic/hardened alloy linings ensure robust operational reliability.

Featured Products: SAMSON PFEIFFER Lined Globe Valves, PFEIFFER Butterfly Valves, VETEC Eccentric Rotary Plug Valves

Water, Wastewater, Marine & General Industry

Versatile, compact control valves for OEM equipment, water distribution, and marine engine cooling. Cost-effective automation with standardized pneumatic or electric actuators.

Featured Products: SAMSON V2001 Control Valves, Type 3323 Three-Way Valve, CERA SYSTEM Ball Valves

SAMSON offers products and systems to control all kinds of media. We specialize in control valve engineering. As a trendsetter for over 100 years, SAMSON drives forward new developments and introduces innovations to the market. We have had a considerable impact on the evolution of valves from analog components to smart control valves.

Our industrial customers are very well aware of the fact that SAMSON provides technologically advanced valves, self-operated regulators, and customized automation solutions that fulfill even the most complex requirements. We are now fully focusing on the opportunities provided to us through Industry 4.0. WeDrive ensures precision control and reliable integration with SAMSON VALVES technology.

Modular Systems and Digital Solutions:

SAMSON’s modular design enables us to provide a wide variety of valves, such as globe valves, angle valves, and three-way valves, in all commonly available valve sizes, pressure ratings, and materials, with exchangeable trims. Special versions for cryogenic applications and steam conditioning round off the range. Combined with smart automation, machine learning is used to implement asset management as well as predictive maintenance and diagnostics via tools like SAM GUARD.

At SAMSON, we combine our decade-long product experience in valve engineering and in-depth understanding of processes with smart information. Our pneumatic and electric automation systems can be seamlessly integrated. We supply the entire control valve assembly consisting of the valve, actuator, and accessories (e.g., positioners, limit switches) perfectly tailored to one another.

SAMSON Valves FAQ

Q: What makes SAMSON control valves the preferred choice?
A: SAMSON specializes in control valve engineering and smart process automation, offering robust, modular valves tailored to specific control tasks in varying sizes, pressure ratings, and materials.

Q: Which industrial sectors rely on SAMSON solutions?
A: SAMSON provides systems for diverse sectors including chemicals, petrochemicals, power and energy, food and beverages, metallurgy, mining, oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, and water/wastewater management.

Q: Does SAMSON offer solutions for Industry 4.0?
A: Yes, SAMSON fully focuses on Industry 4.0 opportunities. Through SAM DIGITAL and tools like SAM GUARD, machine learning is utilized to implement advanced asset management, predictive maintenance, and diagnostics.

Q: What other products does SAMSON offer besides valves?
A: In addition to control valves, SAMSON offers self-operated regulators, actuators, valve accessories (like smart positioners), signal converters, sensors, thermostats, and complete automation systems.
