NORD Gearmotor Selection

NORD helical gear units provide efficient power transmission for a wide range of industrial applications. NORD parallel shaft gear units are suitable for applications requiring high torque and a compact parallel shaft arrangement. NORD bevel gear units offer high efficiency and flexible mounting options, while NORD worm gear units provide a compact solution for lower-power speed reduction applications.

A complete NORD gearmotor combines a suitable NORD gearbox with an electric motor according to the required power, speed, torque and transmission ratio. We can assist with NORD motor, NORD gearbox and NORD geared motor selection based on your application requirements.

NORD Gearbox Shaft Load Selection

The radial force FR1 and axial force FA1 values should be evaluated together with the corresponding input power when selecting a NORD gearmotor. These parameters are especially relevant for applications involving belts, chains, pulleys, sprockets and other external loads on the gearbox input shaft.

For NORD motor and gearbox selection, please provide the required motor power, input speed, output speed or gear ratio, shaft load, mounting arrangement and application conditions. This information allows the appropriate NORD geared motor configuration to be evaluated for your application.
