Brand Heritage Clarification: Innomotics Motor & Siemens Motor

To accelerate market agility and product innovation, Siemens officially spun off its low-voltage electric motor, geared motor, and medium-voltage converter divisions into an independent global entity operating under the brand Innomotics.

Engineering Continuity for Global Customers: Every Innomotics motor is the direct, identical continuation of the legendary Siemens motor. The original factories (such as the prestigious manufacturing plants in Mohelnice & Frenštát, Czech Republic, Nuremberg, Germany, and Yizheng, China), the precision tooling, raw material standards, and engineering drawings remain 100% unchanged.

Siemens Brand Retained Products: The Siemens brand continues to develop, manufacture, and support high-precision Siemens servo motor lines (SIMOTICS S-1FK7, S-1FT7, S-1FK2), servo planetary gearboxes, high-voltage industrial motors, and integrated CNC motion control systems.

Siemens & Innomotics Motors FAQ

Q: What is the relationship between Siemens motor and Innomotics motor?
A: Innomotics was created when Siemens transferred its low-to-medium voltage electric motors, geared motors, and medium-voltage converter divisions into a dedicated company. The engineering designs, manufacturing facilities, and quality standards remain 100% identical to the original Siemens operations.

Q: Does Siemens still manufacture servo motors?
A: Yes. Siemens has retained its motion control portfolio, including high-precision Siemens servo motor lines (SIMOTICS S-1FK7, S-1FT7, S-1FK2), servo planetary gearboxes, and large high-voltage industrial drives under Siemens Digital Industries.

Q: What models of Siemens low voltage motor do you supply?
A: As an experienced Siemens motor supplier, we supply SIMOTICS GP (aluminum) and SIMOTICS SD (cast iron) motors across IE2, IE3, and IE4 standards (including 1LE0003, 1LE0004, 1LE1501, 1LE1503 series), as well as 1MB1 explosion-proof motors.

Q: Can an Innomotics low voltage motor directly replace an existing Siemens motor?
A: Yes. Innomotics motors utilize the exact same frame dimensions, shaft center heights, and electrical winding designs as previous Siemens low voltage motor units, providing a 100% seamless drop-in replacement.
