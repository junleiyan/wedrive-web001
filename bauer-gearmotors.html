BAUER geared motors, industrial gearboxes, and speed reducers are globally recognized for providing precise motion control, superior energy efficiency, and unyielding torque transmission. Whether operating under hygienic washdown conditions in food processing or delivering massive torque in heavy mineral extraction, BAUER premium speed reducers ensure maximum uptime.

BAUER Geared Motor Type Designation & Model Code Identification

Comprehensive breakdown guide for BAUER BG, BF, BK, and BS series gearboxes, geared motors, and speed reducers model selection.

Example Model Code: BK 50 Z - 1 1 U W A / D.. 09L A 4 - TF - S / ES 010 A 9 HN / C2

Understanding BAUER product nomenclature allows engineers and procurement officers to quickly identify gearbox configuration, shaft type, mounting arrangements, motor frame size, pole count, and protective features (such as ATEX, TF thermistors, and SSV covers).

1. Gearbox Unit Designation Breakdown (BK 50 Z - 1 1 U W A)

Code Segment Feature Category Technical Specifications / Options
B • Gear Type BG (Helical), BF (Shaft Mounted), BK (Bevel), BS (Worm)
•• Gear Size 03, 04, 05, 06, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100
Z Pre-Stage Gearbox Z = Gearbox with pre-stage gear reduction
X Radial Bearing Design X = Reinforced radial bearings for high radial loads
- Separator Separates gear type from gear design configuration
1st Digit (Housing) Gear Housing Design 0 = No surfaces except torque arm bore (BF) | 1 = Foot | 2 = Standard small A-Flange (Dia 1) | 3 = Standard A-Flange (Dia 2) | 4 = Standard large A-Flange (Dia 3) | 5 = Torque Arm screw-on (BK+BS) | 6 = Foot-threaded bores | 7 = C-Flange | 8 = Completely machined | 9 = Footplate only BG (Universal)
2nd Digit (Shaft) Output Shaft Design 0 = Splined Shaft acc. to DIN 5480 | 1 = Solid Shaft, front | 2 = Solid Shaft, rear | 3 = Solid Shaft, front and rear | 4 = Hollow Shaft with Keyway | 5 = Hollow Shaft for Shrink disk (rear) | 6 = Hollow Shaft for Shrink disk (front) | 7-9 = Solid Shaft variants flush with Standard-Flange
U / O / R / L / V / H Terminal / Mounting Position U = Foot down / Torque Arm screwed upward | O = Foot top / Torque Arm screwed upward | R = Foot right / Torque Arm right | L = Foot left / Torque Arm left | V = Flange A/C or Torque Arm front | H = Flange A/C or Torque Arm rear
W / A Sealing & Cover Options W = Double Shaft Seals (for abrasive/wet environments) | A = SSV Protective Cover

2. Electric Motor Unit Designation Breakdown (D.. 09L A 4 - TF - S)

Code Segment Motor Component Technical Specifications / Options
D Motor Type Three-Phase AC Electric Motor
.. Prefix Motor Special Execution A = Aseptic Motor (Food/Pharma) | NF = Motor without Gearbox (In-flange design) | PE = Premium Efficiency Three-Phase Motor (IE3 Level)
09 Motor Frame Size Standardized motor frame dimension indicator
LA Core Length & Design Stator core length classification for power output optimization
4 Number of Motor Poles 4 = 4-Pole Motor (approx. 1500 RPM @ 50Hz / 1800 RPM @ 60Hz)
TF Thermal Motor Protection TF = Thermistor temperature sensors for winding protection
S Rectifier System Integrated brake rectifier unit for electromagnetic brake systems

Bauer Gear Motor FAQ

Q: What are the main types of Bauer gear motors available?
A: We supply a full range including BG series helical gear motors, BF series shaft-mounted gear motors, BK series bevel gear motors, and BS series worm gear motors.

Q: Are Bauer gear motors suitable for outdoor or washdown applications?
A: Yes, Bauer offers high IP protection ratings (up to IP65 or IP66) and specialized coatings, making them ideal for food and beverage or outdoor industrial use.
