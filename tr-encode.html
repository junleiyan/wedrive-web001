TR-Electronic Encoders FAQ

Q: What are the main communication interfaces supported by TR absolute encoders?
A: TR Electronic absolute encoders support virtually all modern industrial fieldbus and Ethernet protocols, including PROFINET, EtherCAT, PROFIBUS, SSI, CANopen, DeviceNet, and Ethernet/IP.

Q: Can I replace an old TR encoder model with a new one?
A: Yes, TR-Electronic is designed for high backward compatibility. Our experts can assist in mapping legacy TR encoders (such as the CEV65 series) to the new generation (CEV582 series) while keeping the same mechanical fit and electronic interface.

Q: What is the difference between a TR absolute encoder and a TR incremental encoder?
A: A TR incremental encoder counts pulses during movement and requires a reference run after power loss. A TR absolute encoder always knows its exact position via a unique digital code, meaning no referencing is needed after a restart.

Q: Are TR encoders suitable for heavy-duty environments?
A: Absolutely. TR provides specialized heavy-duty lines with thick-walled stainless steel housings, high shock resistance, and IP69K ratings, perfect for steel manufacturing and offshore applications.
