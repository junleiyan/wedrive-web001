Hansen Industrial Gearbox FAQ

Q: Can you supply direct drop-in replacements for existing Hansen P4 and Hansen M4 ACC gearboxes?
A: Yes. We supply brand-new, 100% original heavy-duty industrial gearboxes engineered to match existing Hansen P4 and M4 ACC mounting footprints, output shaft dimensions, thermal ratings, and gear ratios without requiring foundation modifications.

Q: What is the difference between Hansen M4 ACC and Hansen P4 gear units?
A: The Hansen M4 ACC series is specifically engineered for Air-Cooled Condensers (ACC) and wet cooling towers with reinforced output bearings for high axial fan thrust. The Hansen P4 series covers multistage horizontal, vertical, and UniMiner heavy-duty applications across mining, cement, and bulk handling.

Q: Where can I find Sumitomo Cyclo® 6000, HYPONIC®, or BUDDYBOX® gearmotors?
A: All Sumitomo Drive Technologies small-to-medium geared motors and cycloidal drives are organized on our dedicated Sumitomo Gearbox & Gearmotor Page.

Q: Which industrial sectors rely heavily on Hansen P4 and M4 industrial drives?
A: These heavy-duty gearboxes are widely used in power plant cooling towers, mining conveyors, bucket elevators, cement kilns, port cranes, chemical agitators, and pulp and paper mills.
