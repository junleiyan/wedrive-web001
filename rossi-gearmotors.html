H Rossi Industrial gear unit series (High-torque)
RCI Gear unit design:
  • I = Helical gear unit
  • RCI = Bevel-helical gear unit
  • EP = Planetary gear unit
250 Gear unit sizes: e.g., 100 – 400
UO3A Housing & Mounting design:
  • UO3A = Universal housing design, standard mounting
  • /F = Flange mounting
