Q: How does the Grundfos Hydro MPC optimize energy consumption?
A: The Hydro MPC booster system adjusts pump speed and stages pumps in and out dynamically based on real-time flow and pressure demands. By running each pump at its optimal operating point, it reduces energy consumption significantly compared to traditional fixed-speed sets.

Q: What materials are available for the Grundfos CR multistage series?
A: Grundfos CR pumps are offered in four material variants: CR (cast iron head/base, AISI 304 wetted parts), CRI (all AISI 304 stainless steel), CRN (all AISI 316 stainless steel for aggressive fluids), and CRT (Titanium for saline water, chlorides, and harsh chemicals).
