SEW-Industrial Gear (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. Plant 5 Project Officially Commences

In early summer, the Tianjin region is brimming with vitality. On May 29, 2026, the groundbreaking ceremony for the SEW-Industrial Gear (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. Fifth Plant (F5 Plant) project was grandly held in the Tianjin Economic-Technological Development Area (TEDA). Zhang Shengli, Executive Chairman of SEW China; Ma Jianjun, Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee and Deputy Director of the TEDA Management Committee; Dai Min, Secretary of the Party Committee and Chairman of Sinomach Automobile Co., Ltd. and China Auto Industrial Engineering Co., Ltd. (AE); along with leaders from various levels of the TEDA Management Committee and representatives of partner organizations, attended this grand ceremony.

Strategic Cooperation and Project Details

Prior to the ceremony, SEW China and China Auto Industrial Engineering Co., Ltd. (AE) signed a "Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement" at the SEW China headquarters. As leading forces in their respective industries, the collaboration between SEW China and AE has long transcended traditional supply and demand relationships. Transitioning from early drive system supply to the current strategic alliance, this signing specifies four major collaborative directions: collaborative technical innovation, engineering construction linkage, joint market expansion, and brand value co-creation. A joint working group has been established, adhering to the combination of "specific project contract stipulations" and "framework agreement guidance" to ensure every cooperative effort yields concrete results. As the first landmark project of this strategic implementation, the groundbreaking of the SEW-Industrial Gear (Tianjin) Plant 5 project will inject strong momentum into the regional smart manufacturing industrial upgrade.

The SEW-Industrial Gear (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. Fifth Plant project is located in TEDA. It covers a total area of approximately 76,000 square meters, with a total building area exceeding 60,000 square meters and a total investment of over 2 billion RMB. It is another major project under SEW China's strategic investment plan of "re-embarking with tens of billions of momentum." The project will comprehensively adopt SEW's globally leading intelligent manufacturing technologies to build a benchmark factory in the field of heavy-duty industrial gear manufacturing, becoming a new milestone in SEW's development in the Chinese market. The F5 Plant will focus specifically on the production of large, heavy-duty gear units. In addition to meeting domestic market demand, it will primarily cater to customized product orders for the international market.

Plant 5 Project Highlights

The new SEW Plant 5 (F5) marks a significant step forward in SEW-EURODRIVE's expansion and technological advancement in the high-end equipment manufacturing sector:

  • Total Area & Investment: Covers ~76,000 square meters with an investment of over 2 billion RMB.
  • Core Products: Specialized production of large, heavy-duty industrial gear units.
  • Global Reach: Designed to fulfill customized product orders for the international market while supporting domestic demand.
  • Smart Manufacturing: Extensively incorporates SEW's world-leading intelligent manufacturing and automation technologies.

Leaders Highlight the Significance of the Milestone

Zhang Shengli, Executive Chairman of SEW China, stated in his speech that the F5 project has been in planning since the second half of 2025, and all preparations were rapidly advanced and fully readied in 2026. This could not have been achieved without the support of all parties. He expressed his sincere gratitude to the leaders at all levels of TEDA for their high regard and comprehensive support, and extended thanks to the AE design team, engineering team, and all participating construction units for their hard work. He hoped that all builders would unite their efforts, maintain high standards and strict requirements, ensure safety and quality, and forge the F5 project into a premium and benchmark engineering marvel in Tianjin.

1. Deepening Roots in TEDA for High-End Manufacturing

Ma Jianjun, Deputy Secretary of the Party Committee and Deputy Director of the TEDA Management Committee, expressed in his speech that as a leading enterprise in the global power transmission industry and an "hidden champion" in industrial automation, SEW has been deeply rooted in TEDA for over thirty years. The groundbreaking of the Plant 5 project is not only an important milestone for SEW's development in China but also a grand event for the development of high-end equipment manufacturing in TEDA. This project is not a simple capacity expansion, but rather SEW's strategic layout for advancing into the high-value-added areas of the industrial chain, laying a solid foundation for the company's high-end development. TEDA will continue to uphold its original aspiration of "walking hand-in-hand for win-win development," continuously optimize the business environment, strengthen service guarantees, and fully escort SEW to grow and thrive in the fertile soil of TEDA.

2. Building an Industrial Art-Level Premium Project

Dai Min, Secretary of the Party Committee and Chairman of Sinomach Automobile and AE, stated in his address that the signing of the strategic cooperation agreement and the foundation laying of the Fifth Plant are a double blessing, marking a brand-new upgrade of their cooperation following the previous Foshan project. AE will take "surpassing Foshan, benchmarking against Germany, and creating an industrial-art-level premium project" as its goal. They will collaborate closely and efficiently with SEW to fully forge the project into a benchmark of Chinese intelligent manufacturing, standing tall in the highland of Binhai's manufacturing industry.

3. Adhering to Strict Construction Standards

Ms. Bai Jie, Chief Financial Officer of BlueScope China, noted in her speech that as a partner, BlueScope China will assemble a highly professional construction team, strictly adhere to construction standards, and rigorously control quality and safety checkpoints. They will fully cooperate with SEW to advance the project's construction, ensuring that the F5 Plant becomes a model project in the industry.

4. Embracing the Future with Sustainable Development

For thirty years, SEW China has adhered to a strategic policy of safe, healthy, and sustainable development. The company continuously introduces the world's most advanced core technologies for intelligent manufacturing, pursuing excellence and constant refinement. By dedicating premium products and high-quality services to society, SEW strives to ensure the utmost satisfaction of its vast customer base. SEW China firmly believes that tomorrow will be better, and for the sake of that more beautiful tomorrow, let us set forth tonight!
