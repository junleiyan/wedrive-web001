Embracing the Future: Grand Opening Ceremony Held for SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd. and SEW South China Manufacturing Base

Published on: November 20, 2026 | Last updated: November 20, 2026 | Event Date: September 16, 2026

SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd. Officially Opens in Shunde, Foshan

On September 16, 2026 (the sixth day of the eighth lunar month), amidst crisp autumn skies and a season of bountiful harvest, the grand opening ceremony for SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd. and the SEW South China Manufacturing Base was solemnly held at the newly built smart factory in Shunde, Foshan.

At 11:18 AM, accompanied by resounding gongs and drums, fluttering banners, and dynamic performances by traditional lion dancers and humanoid robots, distinguished guests took the stage to launch the opening ceremony. Officiating leaders included Ding Xifeng, Member of the Standing Committee of the Foshan Municipal Party Committee and Minister of the United Front Work Department; Chen Xinwen, Member of the Standing Committee of the Foshan Municipal Party Committee and Secretary of the Shunde District Party Committee; Lyu Yuanyuan, Deputy Secretary of the Shunde District Party Committee and District Mayor; Dr. Joerg Hermes, Chief Operating Officer (COO) of SEW-EURODRIVE Headquarters in Germany; Zhang Shengli, Executive Chairman of SEW China; and representatives of key strategic partners. Amidst spectacular sound and lighting effects, government officials, SEW Group executives, over one hundred strategic partners from across China, industry elites, and prominent guests gathered to witness this historic milestone for SEW-EURODRIVE in China.

$300 Million Smart Industrial Gearbox Factory & Global Export Hub

Phase I of SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd. covers a total area of 160,000 square meters with a total investment of USD 300 million. The smart factory extensively integrates SEW-EURODRIVE's core intelligent manufacturing technologies and systematic drive automation solutions, setting an industry-leading benchmark in intelligent and digitalized operations management. This facility serves both as a vital strategic fulcrum for the SEW Group to deepen its presence in the South China market and enhance regional competitiveness, and as a premier international export hub for SEW China supplying heavy-duty industrial gearboxes and geared motors to overseas customers worldwide.

SEW Foshan Manufacturing Base Key Highlights

The commissioning of the SEW South China Manufacturing Base in Shunde, Foshan significantly expands SEW-EURODRIVE's heavy-duty gearbox production and global export capacity:

  • Phase I Scale & Investment: 160,000 square meters site area with a USD 300 million investment.
  • Three Core Functional Centers: Intelligent Manufacturing Center, Intelligent Assembly Center, and Digital Management Center.
  • Specialized Heavy-Duty Solutions: Over 100 sets of innovative SEW China products showcased on-site, including dedicated smart drive solutions for mining, metallurgy, and port machinery.
  • Greater Bay Area & Global Export Synergy: Directly fulfills customized industrial gear unit orders across South China while serving as a strategic export gateway for international markets.

Leaders & Strategic Partners Highlight the Milestone

1. Focusing on the Greater Bay Area to Stay Closer to Customers

Zhang Shengli, Executive Chairman of SEW China, stated in his address: "Following deep reflection and extensive market research, we have placed the future strategic development focus of SEW China in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. The construction of the Foshan factory will strongly support SEW's business expansion in South China, truly achieving closer proximity to our customers and closer proximity to the market. Alongside the implementation of the national Greater Bay Area strategy, this carries profound long-term significance."

2. Creating Greater Value for Global Industrial Customers

Dr. Joerg Hermes, Chief Operating Officer of SEW-EURODRIVE Germany, remarked: "Today is an extraordinarily special day and an important witness to SEW's successful development in China. With the grand opening of the new Foshan factory, I believe that in the future SEW Foshan will continue to create even greater value for our broad customer base across China and beyond!"

3. Strategic Partners Celebrate Win-Win Industrial Cooperation

Representatives of SEW China's strategic partners—Zhang Bo, Deputy General Manager of China Auto Industrial Engineering Co., Ltd. (AE); Zheng Zuhua, Executive Director and CEO of CIMC-TianDa Holdings Co., Ltd.; and Cui Peijun, Founder of Henan Mine Crane Co., Ltd.—delivered congratulatory speeches in turn, expressing warm congratulations on the grand opening of the SEW Foshan project and strong confidence in advancing win-win cooperation.

4. Government Support for Smart Manufacturing & International Collaboration

Chen Xinwen, Member of the Standing Committee of the Foshan Municipal Party Committee and Secretary of the Shunde District Party Committee, stated that over three years of rooting in Shunde, SEW's corporate spirit of "pursuing excellence and continuous refinement" has resonated with Shunde's pioneering spirit. Shunde will fully support SEW in building a benchmark smart manufacturing factory and sprinting toward its second 10-billion-RMB development goal.

Ding Xifeng, Member of the Standing Committee of the Foshan Municipal Party Committee and Minister of the United Front Work Department, congratulated SEW on the smooth commissioning of its South China Manufacturing Base, affirming that Foshan will continue to deliver high-level corporate services and build the SEW South China Manufacturing Base into an exemplary model of international industrial cooperation.

5. Intelligent Manufacturing, Assembly & Digital Management Centers

Phase I of SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd. includes an Intelligent Manufacturing Center, an Intelligent Assembly Center, and a Digital Management Center. Over 100 sets of innovative products from SEW China were showcased at the venue, highlighting dedicated intelligent drive solutions for mining, metallurgy, and port machinery. Backed by SEW Group's world-leading R&D strength and the complete industrial supply chain of the Greater Bay Area, the official commissioning of the Foshan plant further strengthens SEW's capability to meet customized customer requirements in South China while laying a solid foundation for international market expansion.

As a wholly German-owned enterprise led by a local Chinese management team, SEW China consistently adheres to its premium product strategy, innovation-led growth, customer-first service, localized development, and global vision. Amidst celebratory salutes, the opening ceremony concluded on September 16, 2026, marking the official start of operations at SEW-Industrial Gear (Foshan) Co., Ltd.—setting sail toward a bright new chapter!
