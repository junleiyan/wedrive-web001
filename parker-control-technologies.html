Parker Hydraulics & Control FAQ

Q: Which Parker hydraulic pump series are most commonly used in heavy industrial systems?
A: The Parker PVplus (PV016–PV360) variable displacement axial piston pumps and Parker Denison T6/T7 high-pressure vane pumps are industry standards for continuous-duty open-circuit industrial hydraulics, delivering pressures up to 350–420 bar with low noise and fast response.

Q: Can you cross-reference legacy Parker or Denison hydraulic part numbers?
A: Yes. Our hydraulic specialists provide full technical cross-referencing for legacy Parker, Denison, Commercial Hydraulics, and Voac part numbers, identifying exact drop-in replacements or current-generation equivalents with matching SAE/ISO mounting flanges, shaft splines, and port threads.

Q: What proportional and servo valves does Parker offer for closed-loop motion control?
A: Parker offers the D1FP/D3FP direct-operated proportional directional valves with Voice Coil Drive (VCD) technology, D41F/D91F pilot-operated proportional valves, and DFplus high-response control valves capable of dynamic closed-loop position, velocity, and force control.

Q: Do you supply genuine Parker replacement filter elements and seal kits?
A: Yes. We supply original Parker replacement filter elements (ParFit, Eppensteiner, Domnick Hunter, and Finite series) as well as genuine OEM seal kits and repair cartridges for Parker pumps, valves, and HMI/2H series hydraulic cylinders.
