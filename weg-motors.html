WEG Electric Motors - Type Designation Structure

The following example shows the standard nomenclature structure for WEG industrial motors:

Example: W22 - 132M - 7.5kW - 4P - IE3 - B3R - IP55
W22 WEG Motor Platform Line (W22 = Industrial Cast Iron Three-Phase Motor)
132M IEC Frame Size & Core Length:
  • 132 = Shaft Center Height in mm
  • S/M/L = Short, Medium, or Long Stator Core Length
7.5kW Rated Power Output: Kilowatts (kW) or Horsepower (HP) rating
4P Number of Poles / Speed:
  • 2P = ~3000 RPM (50Hz) / ~3600 RPM (60Hz)
  • 4P = ~1500 RPM (50Hz) / ~1800 RPM (60Hz)
  • 6P = ~1000 RPM (50Hz) / ~1200 RPM (60Hz)
  • 8P = ~750 RPM (50Hz) / ~900 RPM (60Hz)
IE3 Energy Efficiency Class:
  • IE1 = Standard Efficiency
  • IE2 = High Efficiency
  • IE3 = Premium Efficiency
  • IE4 = Super Premium Efficiency
  • IE5 = Ultra Premium Efficiency (W22 Magnet)
B3R Mounting Configuration:
  • B3T = Foot mounted with top terminal box
  • B3R = Foot mounted with right terminal box
  • B5 = Large Flange mounted (FF Flange)
  • B14 = Small Flange mounted (FT Flange)
  • B35 = Foot and B5 Flange mounted
IP55 Degree of Protection:
  • IP55 = Standard weather and dust protected enclosure
  • IP56 / IP66 = Severe duty & washdown protection
